\section{Quantum LDPC codes at the Pauli hashing bound}
\label{sec:problem-6}

\paragraph{Problem.}
For a probability vector $\mathbf p=(p_I,p_X,p_Y,p_Z)$, the qubit Pauli
channel is
\begin{equation}
  \Lambda_{\mathbf p}(\rho)
  =p_I\rho+p_XX\rho X+p_YY\rho Y+p_ZZ\rho Z,
  \qquad p_I+p_X+p_Y+p_Z=1,
  \label{eq:p6-pauli-channel}
\end{equation}
where every $p_i\ge0$, $I$ is the identity, and $X,Y,Z$ are the Pauli
operators.  The qubit depolarizing channel is the specialization of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p6-pauli-channel} given by
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal D_p(\rho)
  =(1-p)\rho+\frac p3\bigl(X\rho X+Y\rho Y+Z\rho Z\bigr),
  \qquad 0\le p\le1.
  \label{eq:p6-depolarizing-channel}
\end{equation}
Thus Eq.~\eqref{eq:p6-depolarizing-channel} has
$\mathbf p=(1-p,p/3,p/3,p/3)$.  For the general channel in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p6-pauli-channel}, define the hashing rate by
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{hash}}(\mathbf p)
  :=\max\{0,1-H(\mathbf p)\},
  \qquad
  H(\mathbf p):=-\sum_{i\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}}p_i\log_2p_i,
  \qquad 0\log_2 0:=0.
  \label{eq:p6-hashing-bound}
\end{equation}
A family of stabilizer codes $[[n,k_n]]$ is quantum low-density parity-check
(LDPC) if it has stabilizer generators of uniformly bounded weight and each
qubit participates in a uniformly bounded number of generators.  Does there
exist, for a given $\mathbf p$, such a family with decoding error tending to
zero under $\Lambda_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n}$ and asymptotic transmission rate
$R:=\liminf_{n\to\infty}k_n/n$ satisfying
$R\ge R_{\mathrm{hash}}(\mathbf p)$ from
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p6-hashing-bound}?

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Contributor: Bikun Li.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The XZZX surface code is an explicit quantum LDPC family whose
  numerically estimated code-capacity threshold closely matches the zero-rate
  hashing threshold for every single-qubit Pauli channel; for depolarizing
  noise its reported threshold is $18.7(1)\%$.  It encodes only $O(1)$ qubits
  into $n=O(d^2)$ qubits, however, so its asymptotic rate is zero
  \sourcecite{ref:p6-xzzx}{BAT+21}.

  \item Kasai reported a nonbinary quantum LDPC code of rate $1/3$ with
  $312{,}000$ physical and $104{,}000$ logical qubits, attaining frame-error
  rate $10^{-4}$ at depolarizing error probability $p=9.45\%$.  This is strong
  finite-length numerical progress, but at that $p$ Eq.~\eqref{eq:p6-hashing-bound}
  gives $R_{\mathrm{hash}}\simeq0.399>1/3$, and no asymptotic
  capacity-achieving theorem is proved
  \sourcecite{ref:p6-kasai-degeneracy}{Kas25}.

  \item More recently, fixed-degree quantum LDPC ensembles were proved to have
  nonvanishing rate and relative distance with high probability; selected
  finite degree choices attain the CSS Gilbert--Varshamov distance bound.
  These distance guarantees do not by themselves establish reliable Pauli
  channel transmission at the rate in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p6-hashing-bound}
  \sourcecite{ref:p6-kasai-gv}{Kas26}.

  \item Quantum polar codes attain the hashing rate without establishing the
  LDPC constraints.  The entanglement-assisted CSS construction of Renes,
  Dupuis, and Renner has net rate $1-H(\mathbf p)$ and efficient operations
  for Pauli channels, but can consume preshared ebits
  \sourcecite{ref:p6-renes-2012}{RDR12}.  The later unassisted two-level CSS
  construction has vanishing error and rate at least
  $\max\{0,1-H(\mathbf p)\}$, with $O(n\log n)$ encoding and decoding once
  its frozen sets are specified; efficient construction of the outer frozen
  set was left open \sourcecite{ref:p6-renes-2015}{RSDR15}.  Neither work
  proves bounded check weight or bounded qubit degree.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

Polar codes attain the hashing rate without LDPC constraints; the other
results separately address threshold, finite-block performance, or distance.
The open task is a quantum LDPC theorem for Eq.~\eqref{eq:p6-pauli-channel}
with vanishing decoding error and rate at least
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p6-hashing-bound}.
