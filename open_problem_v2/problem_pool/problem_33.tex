\section{POVM steering threshold of higher-dimensional Werner states}
\label{sec:problem-33}

\paragraph{Problem.}
What is the exact steering threshold for arbitrary POVMs on a
higher-dimensional Werner state?  For $d\geq3$, let $F$ be the swap operator
on $\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^d$ and define
\begin{equation}
  \rho_f^{(d)}
  :=\frac{(d-f)I+(df-1)F}{d(d^2-1)},
  \qquad -1\leq f\leq1.
  \label{eq:p33-werner-state}
\end{equation}
If Alice applies a POVM $\{M_{a\mid x}\}_a$ to the state in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p33-werner-state}, Bob's subnormalized conditional states are
\begin{equation}
  \sigma_{a\mid x}
  :=\operatorname{Tr}_A\!\left[
    (M_{a\mid x}\otimes I)\rho_f^{(d)}
    \right].
  \label{eq:p33-assemblage}
\end{equation}
The assemblage in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p33-assemblage} is unsteerable when it admits
a local-hidden-state decomposition
\begin{equation}
  \sigma_{a\mid x}
  =\int_\Lambda\mu(d\lambda)\,
    p(a\mid x,\lambda)\tau_\lambda,
  \label{eq:p33-lhs-model}
\end{equation}
where $\mu$ is a probability measure, $p(a\mid x,\lambda)$ are response
functions, and $\tau_\lambda$ are density operators.  Determine the critical
value $f_{\mathrm{POVM}}(d)$ characterized by
\begin{equation}
  \rho_f^{(d)}\ \text{is unsteerable from Alice to Bob for every POVM}
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  f\geq f_{\mathrm{POVM}}(d).
  \label{eq:p33-povm-threshold}
\end{equation}
In particular, decide whether the threshold in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p33-povm-threshold} equals the exact projective-measurement
threshold
\begin{equation}
  f_{\mathrm{PVM}}(d)=-1+\frac1d+\frac1{d^2}
  \label{eq:p33-pvm-threshold}
\end{equation}
for every $d\geq3$.  Equation~\eqref{eq:p33-lhs-model} fixes the notion of
unsteerability used in both threshold statements.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

After resolving the qubit case, Zhang--Chitambar and Renner explicitly leave
the arbitrary-POVM steering threshold of higher-dimensional Werner states
open \sourcecite{ref:p33-zhang}{ZC24},
\sourcecite{ref:p33-renner}{Ren24}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The projective-measurement boundary is exactly
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p33-pvm-threshold}
  \sourcecite{ref:p33-werner}{Wer89},
  \sourcecite{ref:p33-jones}{JWD07}.

  \item Explicit local-hidden-state constructions give nontrivial
  arbitrary-POVM unsteerable intervals in every dimension, but for $d\geq3$
  their bounds do not reach Eq.~\eqref{eq:p33-pvm-threshold}
  \sourcecite{ref:p33-nguyen}{NG20}.

  \item For $d=2$, projective measurements and arbitrary POVMs have the same
  exact threshold.  In the visibility parametrization
  $\rho_W(r)=r\lvert\Psi^-\rangle\!\langle\Psi^-\rvert+(1-r)I/4$, the state
  is unsteerable for every POVM exactly when $r\leq1/2$
  \sourcecite{ref:p33-zhang}{ZC24},
  \sourcecite{ref:p33-renner}{Ren24}.  These qubit constructions have not
  determined Eq.~\eqref{eq:p33-povm-threshold} for $d\geq3$.

  \item Higher-dimensional steering inequalities detect steering for
  selected measurement families, but do not determine the all-POVM boundary
  \sourcecite{ref:p33-yang}{YQ26}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The exact arbitrary-POVM boundary is known in dimension two but not for any
general $d\geq3$.  The remaining question is whether genuinely
nonprojective POVMs lower the unsteerable Werner interval below the
projective threshold.
