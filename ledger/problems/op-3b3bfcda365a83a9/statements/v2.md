Can the exact quantum Fourier transform on $n$ qubits be implemented with $O(n)$ one- and two-qubit gates?

Define

$$
F_{2^n}|x\rangle=2^{-n/2}\sum_{y=0}^{2^n-1}e^{2\pi ixy/2^n}|y\rangle.
\tag{1}
$$

Let $C_{\mathrm{exact}}(n)$ be the minimum size of a uniform circuit that implements the unitary in Eq. (1) exactly on arbitrary inputs. Gates may be arbitrary efficiently specified one- or two-qubit unitaries; clean ancillas may be used but must be restored, and all gates on them count. Is

$$
C_{\mathrm{exact}}(n)=O(n)?
\tag{2}
$$

The target in Eq. (2) concerns exact coherent implementation, not approximate QFT or sampling only.
