Can the exact quantum Fourier transform on $n$ qubits be implemented by a uniform circuit of $O(n)$ one- and two-qubit gates?

On $n$ qubits, define

$$
F_{2^n}|x\rangle
=2^{-n/2}\sum_{y=0}^{2^n-1}e^{2\pi ixy/2^n}|y\rangle.
\tag{1}
$$

Let $C_{\mathrm{exact}}(n)$ be the minimum number of one- and two-qubit gates in a uniform circuit implementing this unitary exactly on arbitrary input states. Clean ancillas may be used but must be restored; their preparation and all gates acting on them count. Gates may be arbitrary one- or two-qubit unitaries with efficiently specified parameters.

Question: Is

$$
C_{\mathrm{exact}}(n)=O(n)?
\tag{2}
$$

An alternative decisive result would be a superlinear lower bound in this model. This is not a claim about exact synthesis over Clifford+$T$, a finite gate set that cannot exactly express all rotations occurring in the QFT family. Nor is the task merely to sample a QFT measurement distribution.

The displayed definitions, constraints, and target bounds are recorded in Eqs. (1), (2).
