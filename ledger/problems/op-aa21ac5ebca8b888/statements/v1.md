What is the entanglement cost of a qubit Bell-diagonal state for an arbitrary probability vector $\mathbf p=(p_I,p_X,p_Y,p_Z)$? Let $\lvert\Phi^+\rangle=(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $\lvert\Phi_P\rangle=(I\otimes P)\lvert\Phi^+\rangle$ for $P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}$. The state is

$$
\rho_{\mathbf p}
 :=\sum_{P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}}p_P
 \lvert\Phi_P\rangle\!\langle\Phi_P\rvert,
 \qquad p_P\geq0,
 \qquad \sum_{P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}}p_P=1.
\tag{1}
$$

For the state in Eq. (1), determine $E_C(\rho_{\mathbf p})$ for every $\mathbf p$ in the probability simplex, where $E_C$ is the infimum asymptotic rate of ebits consumed by LOCC protocols that prepare $\rho_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n}$ with trace-norm error tending to zero.
