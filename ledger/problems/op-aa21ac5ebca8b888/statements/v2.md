What is the LOCC entanglement cost of an entangled qubit Bell-diagonal state? Let $\mathbf p=(p_I,p_X,p_Y,p_Z)$ be a probability vector, and let $\lvert\Phi^+\rangle=(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $\lvert\Phi_P\rangle=(I\otimes P)\lvert\Phi^+\rangle$ for $P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}$. The state is

$$
\rho_{\mathbf p}
 :=\sum_{P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}}p_P
 \lvert\Phi_P\rangle\!\langle\Phi_P\rvert,
 \qquad p_P\geq0,
 \qquad \sum_{P\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}}p_P=1.
\tag{1}
$$

The state in Eq. (1) is entangled exactly when $p_\star:=\max\{p_I,p_X,p_Y,p_Z\}>1/2$ [ZCH10](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/12/8/083002). Determine $E_C(\rho_{\mathbf p})$ for every probability vector satisfying $p_\star>1/2$, where $E_C$ is the infimum asymptotic rate of ebits consumed by protocols using local operations and classical communication (LOCC) that prepare $\rho_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n}$ with trace-norm error tending to zero.
