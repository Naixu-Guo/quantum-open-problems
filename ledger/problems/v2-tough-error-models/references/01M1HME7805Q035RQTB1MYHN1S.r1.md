The Knill--Laflamme--Viola existence argument and the projection
  construction recorded in the source give the ceiling-sensitive bounds
  \begin{equation}
    \left\lceil
      \frac{\left\lceil n/e^2\right\rceil}{e^2+1}
    \right\rceil
    \le c(e,n)
    \le\left\lceil\frac{n}{e}\right\rceil.
    \label{eq:p14-known-bounds}
  \end{equation}
  The upper bound uses an error space spanned by nearly equal-rank orthogonal
  projections.  The parameter gap is polynomial in $e$
  [KLV00],
  [KW05].
