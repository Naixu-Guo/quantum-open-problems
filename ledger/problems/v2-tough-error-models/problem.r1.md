## Comment

The source calls an error model $E$ ``tough'' when the largest code correcting
$E$ has dimension close to $c(e,n)$
[KW05].  The unresolved task is to close
the bounds in Eq. \eqref{eq:p14-known-bounds} for $2\le e<n$ and construct
such models at the correct scale.  Results for structured Pauli noise or
arbitrary operator systems do not determine
Eq. \eqref{eq:p14-guaranteed-code-dimension}.
