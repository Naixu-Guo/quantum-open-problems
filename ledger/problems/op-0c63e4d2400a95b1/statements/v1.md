Can every set of three orthogonal maximally entangled pure states on $\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^d$, for every integer $d\geq4$, be distinguished exactly from one copy using unrestricted two-way local operations and classical communication (LOCC)? Alice and Bob know the set $\mathcal S:=\{|\psi_i\rangle\}_{i=1}^3$ and hold systems $A$ and $B$, respectively. The density operators $\rho_i:=|\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|$ satisfy Eq. (1):

$$
\langle\psi_i|\psi_j\rangle=\delta_{ij},
\qquad \operatorname{Tr}_B\rho_i=\frac{I_A}{d},
\qquad i,j\in\{1,2,3\}.
\tag{1}
$$

Here $I_A$ is the identity on Alice’s system and $\delta_{ij}$ is the Kronecker delta. The task is to implement a positive-operator-valued measurement $\mathbb M:=\{M_i\}_{i=1}^3$ by LOCC, with no additional shared entanglement and no requirement to preserve the input state. Writing $I_{AB}$ for the joint identity, perfect discrimination means Eq. (2):

$$
\begin{gathered}
M_i\geq0,\qquad \sum_{i=1}^3M_i=I_{AB},\\
\operatorname{Tr}(M_i\rho_j)=\delta_{ij}
\quad\text{for all }i,j\in\{1,2,3\}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{2}
$$

The measurement must be exactly implementable by LOCC, rather than merely approximable by a sequence of LOCC measurements.
