For the zero-temperature pure-loss channel, the constrained capacity
  is known exactly:
  \begin{equation}
    \mathcal Q(\Phi_{\eta,0},N_{\rm S})
    =\left[g(\eta N_{\rm S})-g((1-\eta)N_{\rm S})\right]_+,
    \qquad
    g(x):=(x+1)\log_2(x+1)-x\log_2x.
    \label{eq:p51-pure-loss-capacity}
  \end{equation}
  Equation \eqref{eq:p51-pure-loss-capacity} does not extend directly to a
  thermal environment [WQ18].
