A data-processing decomposition gives, for $1/2\leq\eta<1$,
  \begin{equation}
    \mathcal Q(\Phi_{\eta,\nu},N_{\rm S})
    \leq[U(\eta,\nu,N_{\rm S})]_+,
    \qquad
    U:=g(\eta'N_{\rm S})-g((1-\eta')N_{\rm S}),
    \quad
    \eta':=\frac{\eta}{1+(1-\eta)\nu}.
    \label{eq:p51-data-processing-upper-bound}
  \end{equation}
  The upper bound in Eq. \eqref{eq:p51-data-processing-upper-bound} generally
  does not meet Eq. \eqref{eq:p51-convexified-achievable-rate}
  [SWA+18].
