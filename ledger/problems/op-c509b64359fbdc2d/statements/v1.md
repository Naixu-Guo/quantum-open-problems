Do complex Gaussian permanents satisfy a polynomial lower-tail bound at their root-mean-square scale? For each integer $n\geq1$, let $X\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$ have independent entries with density $\pi^{-1}e^{-|z|^2}$. Write $S_n$ for the permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and define

$$
\operatorname{Per}X=\sum_{\pi\in S_n}\prod_{j=1}^nX_{j,\pi(j)}.
\tag{1}
$$

Does there exist a polynomial $p$, positive on $[1,\infty)^2$, such that the permanent in Eq. (1) satisfies

$$
\Pr_X\!\left[|\operatorname{Per}X|<
 \frac{\sqrt{n!}}{p(n,1/\delta)}\right]<\delta
 \qquad(n\geq1,\ 0<\delta<1)?
\tag{2}
$$

The same polynomial must work for every pair $(n,\delta)$ in Eq. (2).
