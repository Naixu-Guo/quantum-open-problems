Sandwiched-Rényi converses bound the fixed-error limsup by
  \begin{equation}
    \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac1nD_{H,{\rm ch}}^\varepsilon
    (\mathcal N^{\otimes n}\|\mathcal M^{\otimes n})
    \leq\inf_{\alpha>1}
    \widetilde D_{\alpha,{\rm ch}}^\infty(\mathcal N\|\mathcal M).
    \label{eq:p46-renyi-converse}
  \end{equation}
  Closing Eq. \eqref{eq:p46-renyi-converse} requires the still-unproved
  continuity identity
  \begin{equation}
    \inf_{\alpha>1}\widetilde D_{\alpha,{\rm ch}}^\infty
       (\mathcal N\|\mathcal M)
    \stackrel{?}{=}D_{\rm ch}^\infty(\mathcal N\|\mathcal M).
    \label{eq:p46-renyi-continuity}
  \end{equation}
  Equation \eqref{eq:p46-renyi-continuity} is the general strong-converse
  threshold problem [FGW25].
