The weak channel Stein lemma proves
  \begin{equation}
    \lim_{\delta\downarrow0}\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n
    D_{H,{\rm ch}}^\delta
    (\mathcal N^{\otimes n}\|\mathcal M^{\otimes n})
    =D_{\rm ch}^{\infty}(\mathcal N\|\mathcal M).
    \label{eq:p46-weak-stein}
  \end{equation}
  Equation \eqref{eq:p46-weak-stein} yields the required lower bound at
  every fixed error, but not the converse inequality
  [FGW25].
