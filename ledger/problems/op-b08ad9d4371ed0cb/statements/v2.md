Does an absolutely maximally entangled state of eight ququarts exist? More precisely, with $[8]=\{1,\ldots,8\}$, determine whether there is a unit vector $|\psi\rangle\in(\mathbb{C}^{4})^{\otimes 8}$ such that

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{S^{c}}\!\left(|\psi\rangle\!\langle\psi|\right)
 =\frac{I_{4^{4}}}{4^{4}}
 \qquad\text{for every }S\subseteq[8]\text{ with }|S|=4.
\tag{1}
$$

Condition (1) is equivalently the existence of a rank-eight perfect tensor of bond dimension $4$: every flattening across a $4|4$ partition is proportional to a unitary [PYHP15](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06\(2015\)149). Under the pure-code correspondence, it is also equivalent to a pure quantum MDS code with parameters $\bigl[\!\bigl[8,0,5\bigr]\!\bigr]_{4}$ [SZZL26](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.01011). Equivalently, the question asks whether there is a quantum maximum-distance-separable code with parameters $((7,4,4))_{4}$: a four-dimensional subspace $\mathcal{Q}\subseteq(\mathbb{C}^{4})^{\otimes 7}$ whose orthogonal projector $P$ satisfies

$$
PEP=c(E)\,P
 \quad\text{for every operator }E\text{ acting nontrivially on at most three ququarts},
 \qquad
 \dim\mathcal{Q}=4=4^{\,7-2(4-1)},
\tag{2}
$$

where $c(E)\in\mathbb{C}$ and the last equality is saturation of the quantum Singleton bound [HG20](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2020-06-18-284).
