What is the smallest rank of a finite-dimensional bipartite state with negative partial transpose (NPT) that is undistillable under two-way local operations and classical communication (LOCC)? Let $\rho_{AB}$ be a density operator on $\mathbb C^{d_A}\otimes\mathbb C^{d_B}$, with finite integers $d_A,d_B\geq2$, $\rho_{AB}\succeq0$, and $\operatorname{Tr}\rho_{AB}=1$. Write $\rho_{AB}^{T_B}=(\operatorname{id}_A\otimes T_B)(\rho_{AB})$ for partial transposition on $B$. The state is NPT when $\rho_{AB}^{T_B}\not\succeq0$.

A fixed state is undistillable precisely when, for every integer $n\geq1$ and every unit vector $\lvert\psi_n\rangle$ of Schmidt rank at most two across the bipartition $A^n:B^n$, it satisfies

$$
\langle\psi_n\rvert(\rho_{AB}^{T_B})^{\otimes n}\lvert\psi_n\rangle\geq0.
\tag{1}
$$

Determine the exact minimum

$$
r_{\min}:=\min_{\rho_{AB}}\operatorname{rank}(\rho_{AB}),
\tag{2}
$$

where the minimization ranges over all NPT density operators satisfying Eq. (1), allowing arbitrary finite local dimensions. The rank in Eq. (2) is the operator rank of the joint density matrix. A complete solution must exhibit a state attaining the proposed minimum and prove that every NPT state of smaller rank is distillable, in every finite-dimensional bipartite system.
