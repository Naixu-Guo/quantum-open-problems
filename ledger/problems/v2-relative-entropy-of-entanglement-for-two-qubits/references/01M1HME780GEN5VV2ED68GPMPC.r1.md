Miranowicz and Ishizaka solved the inverse problem: from a boundary
  separable state $\sigma$, they parameterized entangled states for which
  $\sigma$ is closest.  Their construction yields formulas for special
  families but does not invert to a closed map $\rho\mapsto\sigma_\rho$ for an
  arbitrary input
  [MI08].
