## Formal statement

Find a closed formula for the relative entropy of entanglement of every
two-qubit density operator $\rho$, including an explicit closest separable
state.  With $\operatorname{Sep}(\mathbb{C}^2:\mathbb{C}^2)$ denoting the
two-qubit separable states, the quantity is
\begin{equation}
  E_R(\rho)
  :=\min_{\sigma\in\operatorname{Sep}(\mathbb{C}^2:\mathbb{C}^2)}
       D(\rho\Vert\sigma),
  \qquad
  D(\rho\Vert\sigma)
  :=\begin{cases}
    \operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\rho(\log\rho-\log\sigma)\right],
      &\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\operatorname{supp}\sigma,\\
    +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:p12-relative-entropy-entanglement}
\end{equation}
In the finite branch of Eq.~\eqref{eq:p12-relative-entropy-entanglement}, the
trace is evaluated on $\operatorname{supp}\rho$, with $0\log 0:=0$.  The
formula sought must determine at least one minimizing state $\sigma_\rho$ for
every $\rho$.
