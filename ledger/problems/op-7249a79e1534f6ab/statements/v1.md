Does the Graph Isomorphism problem admit a polynomial-time quantum algorithm?

Let $G=(V_G,E_G)$ and $H=(V_H,E_H)$ be finite simple graphs with $|V_G|=|V_H|=n$. The graphs are isomorphic if there exists a bijection $\pi:V_G\to V_H$ satisfying

$$
\{u,v\}\in E_G
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \{\pi(u),\pi(v)\}\in E_H
 \qquad
 \forall u,v\in V_G .
\tag{1}
$$

The open question is whether there is a bounded-error quantum algorithm running in time polynomial in $n$ that, given $G$ and $H$, decides whether a bijection satisfying (1) exists.
