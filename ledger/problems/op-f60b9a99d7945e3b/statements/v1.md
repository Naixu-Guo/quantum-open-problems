Does there exist an $\operatorname{AME}(4,6)$ state whose coefficients are real in a product basis? Fix $[6]=\{0,\ldots,5\}$ and ask for a normalized state

$$
|\psi\rangle
 =\sum_{i,j,k,l\in[6]}T_{ijkl}|i\rangle_A|j\rangle_B
 |k\rangle_C|l\rangle_D,
 \qquad
 T_{ijkl}\in\mathbb{R},
 \qquad
 \sum_{i,j,k,l\in[6]}T_{ijkl}^{2}=1 .
\tag{1}
$$

The state in (1) must have maximally mixed reductions on every pair of parties: for each $S\subseteq\{A,B,C,D\}$ with $|S|=2$,

$$
\rho_S:=\operatorname{Tr}_{S^{c}}|\psi\rangle\!\langle\psi|
 =\frac{I_{36}}{36} .
\tag{2}
$$

Equivalently, define the $36\times36$ flattening $U$ and its reshuffling $U^R$ and second-factor partial transpose $U^\Gamma$ by

$$
U_{(i,j),(k,l)}:=6T_{ijkl},
 \qquad
 (U^R)_{(i,k),(j,l)}:=U_{(i,j),(k,l)},
 \qquad
 (U^\Gamma)_{(i,j),(k,l)}:=U_{(i,l),(k,j)} .
\tag{3}
$$

Here the three matrices in (3) are the coefficient flattenings for the bipartitions $AB|CD$, $AC|BD$, and $AD|CB$, up to the displayed ordering of tensor factors. Consequently, (2) is equivalent to the orthogonal $2$-unitarity conditions

$$
U\in O(36),
 \qquad
 U^R\in O(36),
 \qquad
 U^\Gamma\in O(36) .
\tag{4}
$$

Thus the problem is also to construct an orthogonal $2$-unitary matrix of order $36$, or prove that none exists.
