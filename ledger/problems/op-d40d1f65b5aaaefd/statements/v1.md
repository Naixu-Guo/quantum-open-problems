Is the exact deterministic LOCC entanglement cost of the two-qubit elegant joint measurement $\mathcal M_{\mathrm{EJM}}$ equal to one ebit when arbitrary finite-dimensional pure entangled resources are allowed? Alice and Bob hold qubits $A$ and $B$ separately, and the measurement returns only a shared classical outcome. Its tetrahedral directions are given by Eq. (1):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf n_1&:=\frac{(1,1,1)}{\sqrt3},&
\mathbf n_2&:=\frac{(1,-1,-1)}{\sqrt3},\\
\mathbf n_3&:=\frac{(-1,1,-1)}{\sqrt3},&
\mathbf n_4&:=\frac{(-1,-1,1)}{\sqrt3}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
$$

For $j\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, define the singlet $|s\rangle$, orthonormal measurement eigenstates $|e_j\rangle$, and rank-one projectors $P_j$ by Eq. (2):

$$
\begin{gathered}
|s\rangle:=\frac{|01\rangle-|10\rangle}{\sqrt2},\\
|e_j\rangle:=\left[\left(\frac12 I_A+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\mathbf n_j\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma_A\right)\otimes I_B\right]|s\rangle,
\qquad P_j:=|e_j\rangle\langle e_j|.
\end{gathered}
\tag{2}
$$

Here $I_A,I_B$ are the qubit identities and $\boldsymbol\sigma_A:=(X_A,Y_A,Z_A)$ are the Pauli matrices. For every input density operator $\rho$, the measurement channel is Eq. (3):

$$
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{EJM}}(\rho):=\sum_{j=1}^4\operatorname{Tr}(P_j\rho)
|j\rangle\langle j|_{C_A}\otimes|j\rangle\langle j|_{C_B},
\tag{3}
$$

where $C_A,C_B$ are four-valued classical registers; no postmeasurement quantum state is prescribed.

Allow any normalized pure resource $|\chi\rangle\in\mathbb C^r\otimes\mathbb C^r$ on auxiliary systems $A',B'$ for any finite positive integer $r$. Write $\chi:=|\chi\rangle\langle\chi|$, $\chi_{A'}:=\operatorname{Tr}_{B'}\chi$, and $S(\sigma):=-\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\log_2\sigma)$ for a density operator $\sigma$. With all additional shared entanglement included in $\chi$, minimize over deterministic LOCC channels $\Lambda$ across $AA':BB'$ as in Eq. (4):

$$
E_{\mathrm{LOCC}}^{\mathrm{exact}}(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{EJM}}):=\inf\left\{
S(\chi_{A'}):\ \begin{gathered}
r\in\mathbb N,\quad |\chi\rangle\in\mathbb C^r\otimes\mathbb C^r,\\
\langle\chi|\chi\rangle=1,\quad \exists\Lambda\in\mathrm{LOCC},\\
\Lambda(\rho\otimes\chi)=\mathcal M_{\mathrm{EJM}}(\rho)\quad\text{for every }\rho
\end{gathered}\right\}.
\tag{4}
$$

The target is to decide whether the infimum in Eq. (4) equals $1$.
