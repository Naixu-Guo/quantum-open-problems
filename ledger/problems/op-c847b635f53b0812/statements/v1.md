What is the exact minimum CNOT count, and the count–depth tradeoff, for ancilla-free Clifford preparation of the logical zero state of the $[[23,1,7]]_2$ quantum Golay code?

Let $C_{23}\subseteq\mathbb F_2^{23}$ be the classical binary $[23,12,7]$ Golay code, let $C_{23}^{\perp}$ denote its Euclidean dual, and fix the logical zero state of the associated quantum $[[23,1,7]]_2$ CSS code to be

$$
|0_G\rangle=2^{-11/2}\sum_{c\in C_{23}^{\perp}}|c\rangle.
\tag{1}
$$

Consider unitary Clifford circuits on exactly $23$ qubits, initialized in $|0\rangle^{\otimes23}$, with arbitrary pairwise connectivity, free single-qubit Clifford gates, and CNOT gates of unit cost. No additional ancillary qubits, measurements, or postselection are allowed. For such a circuit $U$, let $N_{\mathrm{CX}}(U)$ count its CNOT gates and let $d_2(U)$ count its two-qubit layers, where gates in the same layer act on disjoint pairs. Define

$$
g_G(D)=\min\left\{N_{\mathrm{CX}}(U):
U|0\rangle^{\otimes23}=e^{i\theta}|0_G\rangle,
\quad d_2(U)\le D
\right\},
\tag{2}
$$

where $\theta\in\mathbb R$ is an irrelevant global phase, the minimum of an empty set is $+\infty$, and $D=\infty$ removes the depth constraint. What are the exact minimum $g_G(\infty)$ and its depth-constrained tradeoff $g_G(D)$, particularly at $D=7$ and $D=8$?

The displayed definitions, constraints, and target bounds are recorded in Eqs. (1), (2).
