Does any finite qutrit stabilizer-code protocol distill depolarized Strange states above the ternary Golay code’s threshold?

Let

$$
|S\rangle=\frac{|1\rangle-|2\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
\rho_S(\epsilon)=(1-\epsilon)|S\rangle\langle S|+\epsilon\frac{I_3}{3},
\qquad 0\le\epsilon\le1,
\tag{1}
$$

where $I_3$ is the identity on one qutrit. Here $\epsilon$ is the depolarizing mixing parameter, not the state infidelity; indeed $1-\langle S|\rho_S(\epsilon)|S\rangle=2\epsilon/3$.

Consider an $n$-to-$1$ protocol $\mathcal P$ based on a qutrit stabilizer code: it projects $n$ independent copies of $\rho_S(\epsilon)$ onto an accepted syndrome sector, applies a Clifford decoder and any Clifford correction, and twirls the surviving qutrit back to the family $\rho_S$. Conditioned on acceptance, let $F_{\mathcal P}(\epsilon)$ be the output mixing parameter. Define its iterative distillation threshold by

$$
\epsilon_*(\mathcal P)=
\sup\left\{a\in[0,1]:
\lim_{r\to\infty}F_{\mathcal P}^{\circ r}(\epsilon)=0
\text{ for every }0<\epsilon<a
\right\},
\tag{2}
$$

requiring a nonzero acceptance probability at every finite iteration. Let $\mathcal P_G$ be the protocol based on the ternary quantum $[[11,1,5]]_3$ Golay code and set $\epsilon_G:=\epsilon_*(\mathcal P_G)\approx0.38715$. Does there exist a finite $n$ and a qutrit stabilizer-code protocol $\mathcal P$ with $\epsilon_*(\mathcal P)>\epsilon_G$?

The displayed definitions, constraints, and target bounds are recorded in Eqs. (1), (2).
