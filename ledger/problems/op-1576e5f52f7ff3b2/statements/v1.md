Is the following bipartite Quantum Max-Cut promise problem in $\mathrm{BPP}$? Given a bipartite graph $G=(V,E)$ with $|V|=n$, polynomially bounded nonnegative rational weights $w_{ij}$, and rational thresholds $a<b$ with $b-a\geq1/\operatorname{poly}(n)$, define

$$
H_G:=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E}w_{ij}(X_iX_j+Y_iY_j+Z_iZ_j),
 \qquad E_0:=\lambda_{\min}(H_G).
\tag{1}
$$

Here $X_i,Y_i,Z_i$ are Pauli operators on qubit $i$. For the energy in Eq. (1), distinguish $E_0\leq a$ from $E_0\geq b$, promised one holds, using a randomized classical algorithm polynomial in the input length and correct with probability at least $2/3$.
