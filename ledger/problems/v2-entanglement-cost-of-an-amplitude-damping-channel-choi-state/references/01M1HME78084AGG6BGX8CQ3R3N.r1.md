The entanglement cost equals the regularized entanglement of formation
  \begin{equation}
    E_C(\omega_p)
      =\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n E_F(\omega_p^{\otimes n})
      \le E_F(\omega_p)
      =h_2\!\left(\frac{1+\sqrt p}{2}\right)
    \label{eq:p8-regularized-entanglement-of-formation}
  \end{equation}
  [HHT01].  Consequently,
  Eq. \eqref{eq:p8-regularized-entanglement-of-formation} reduces the problem
  to evaluating the regularization for this particular family of Choi
  states.
