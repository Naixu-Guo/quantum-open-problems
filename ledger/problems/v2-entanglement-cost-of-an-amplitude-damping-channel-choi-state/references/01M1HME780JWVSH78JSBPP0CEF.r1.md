Wootters' two-qubit formula, together with the concurrence of
  $\omega_p$, gives the exact single-copy entanglement of formation
  \begin{equation}
    C(\omega_p)=\sqrt{1-p},
    \qquad
    E_F(\omega_p)=h_2\!\left(\frac{1+\sqrt p}{2}\right),
    \label{eq:p8-entanglement-of-formation}
  \end{equation}
  where $h_2(x):=-x\log_2x-(1-x)\log_2(1-x)$, with $0\log_2 0:=0$,
  [Woo98].  Equation 
  \eqref{eq:p8-entanglement-of-formation} determines one copy exactly, but it
  does not by itself determine the asymptotic entanglement cost.
