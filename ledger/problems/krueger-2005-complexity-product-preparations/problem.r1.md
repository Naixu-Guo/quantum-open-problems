No general asymptotic formula is known for the weighted Pauli-rotation cost of preparing many copies of a pure state.

The answer decides whether state-preparation complexity is extensive or whether a collective circuit can amortize the cost of producing repeated quantum resources.

## Interpretation

Do not substitute gate count for the source's weighted continuous-angle cost.
