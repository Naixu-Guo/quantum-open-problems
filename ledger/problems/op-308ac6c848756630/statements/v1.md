Does a symmetric informationally complete positive-operator-valued measure exist in every finite dimension $d\ge2$? Equivalently, determine whether for every such $d$ there are $d^2$ unit vectors $\lvert\psi_1\rangle,\ldots,\lvert\psi_{d^2}\rangle\in\mathbb{C}^d$ satisfying

$$
\sum_{j=1}^{d^2}
 \lvert\psi_j\rangle\!\langle\psi_j\rvert=dI_d,
 \qquad
 \left|\langle\psi_j\vert\psi_k\rangle\right|^2
 =\frac{1}{d+1}
 \quad\text{for all }j\ne k.
\tag{1}
$$

When Eq. (1) holds, the effects $\Pi_j=d^{-1}\lvert\psi_j\rangle\!\langle\psi_j\rvert$ form the desired SIC-POVM. No covariance or additional symmetry is required.
