## Source

Renes, Blume-Kohout, Scott, and Caves explicitly conjecture that SIC-POVMs exist in every finite dimension [RBS+04](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1737053).

## Progress

Renes, Blume-Kohout, Scott, and Caves explicitly conjectured existence in every dimension and found Weyl–Heisenberg-covariant numerical solutions through $d=45$. Numerical solutions do not prove the universal quantifier in Eq. (1) [RBS+04](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1737053).

In their $2022$ survey, Horodecki, Rudnicki, and Życzkowski identified construction of SIC-POVMs in an unbounded sequence of finite dimensions as an intermediate open milestone. The universal statement in Eq. (1) implies that milestone [HRZ22](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.3.010101).

Appleby, Bengtsson, Flammia, and Goyeneche reported numerical solutions in every dimension through $d=181$ and in many larger dimensions. The computations provide extensive evidence but no all-dimensional construction [ABFG19](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/ab25ad).

In $2025$, Appleby, Flammia, and Kopp gave a construction of SICs for every $d>3$, conditional on the order-one abelian Stark conjecture for real quadratic fields and a special-value identity for the Shintani–Faddeev modular cocycle [AFK25](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.03970).

A claimed unconditional proof posted in $2026$ was withdrawn after the author stated that the proof was incorrect, so it does not change the status of the problem [Jok26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13475).

In September $2026$, Radchenko and Wheeler proved algebraicity of real-quadratic Stark–Shintani invariants and the rank-one twisted-convolution identity (Theorems 1 and 7) [RW26](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.21892). Appleby, Flammia, and Kopp extended the identity to all admissible tuples and constructed Weyl–Heisenberg-covariant ghost SICs for every $d>3$ (Theorems 1.2–1.3) [AFK26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39192v1). This supplies the special-value identity in the earlier conditional construction; converting the ghost SICs to Hermitian SIC projectors requires the appropriate Galois conjugation.

The manuscript dated 4 October $2026$, prepared and edited by Danylo Radchenko and Campbell Wheeler, reports Stark reciprocity and unitary Galois conjugates (Theorem 3 and Proposition 7). Section 9.2 claims SIC existence in every dimension by combining these results with the earlier construction. [SU26](https://www.danrad.net/papers/stark-reciprocity.pdf). The manuscript has not yet passed peer review. This is recorded as significant reported progress; the catalog status remains Unsolved.

## Comment

The catalog retains Unsolved while documenting the reported progress above; a status change requires a separate maintainer decision. The target is unconditional existence for every integer $d\ge2$ in Eq. (1). The weaker objective of an unbounded sequence of finite dimensions is subsumed here as an intermediate milestone rather than maintained as a separate problem.
