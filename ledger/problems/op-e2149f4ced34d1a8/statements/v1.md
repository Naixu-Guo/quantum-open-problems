Find a closed formula for the relative entropy of entanglement of every two-qubit density operator $\rho$, including an explicit closest separable state. With $\operatorname{Sep}(\mathbb{C}^2:\mathbb{C}^2)$ denoting the two-qubit separable states, the quantity is

$$
E_R(\rho)
 :=\min_{\sigma\in\operatorname{Sep}(\mathbb{C}^2:\mathbb{C}^2)}
 D(\rho\Vert\sigma),
 \qquad
 D(\rho\Vert\sigma)
 :=\begin{cases}
 \operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\rho(\log\rho-\log\sigma)\right],
 &\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\operatorname{supp}\sigma,\\
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\tag{1}
$$

In the finite branch of Eq. (1), the trace is evaluated on $\operatorname{supp}\rho$, with $0\log 0:=0$. The formula sought must determine at least one minimizing state $\sigma_\rho$ for every $\rho$.
