For each integer $d\geq2$, determine the maximum number $\mu(d)$ of pairwise mutually unbiased orthonormal bases of $\mathbb C^d$. Two orthonormal bases $\mathcal B_r=\{|e_i^{(r)}\rangle\}_{i=1}^{d}$ and $\mathcal B_s=\{|e_j^{(s)}\rangle\}_{j=1}^{d}$ are mutually unbiased when

$$
\bigl|\langle e_i^{(r)}|e_j^{(s)}\rangle\bigr|^2=\frac1d
 \qquad\text{for every }i,j\in\{1,\ldots,d\}.
\tag{1}
$$

Thus the extremal quantity defined by Eq. (1) is

$$
\mu(d):=\max\left\{m:\text{there exist $m$ orthonormal bases of
 $\mathbb C^d$ that are pairwise mutually unbiased}\right\}.
\tag{2}
$$

Determine Eq. (2) in the non-prime-power regime, in particular for $d\in\{6,10,12,14,15\}$.
