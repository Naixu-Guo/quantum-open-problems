Define an isometry $V:A\to B\otimes E$, where $A$ and $B$ are qutrits and $E$ is a qubit, by

$$
V\ket{0}=(\ket{00}+\ket{11})/\sqrt2,\qquad
 V\ket{1}=\ket{20},\qquad V\ket{2}=\ket{21}.
\tag{1}
$$

For $V$ in Eq. (1), set $\mathcal{N}(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}_E(V\rho V^\dagger)$ and $\mathcal{N}^c(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}_B(V\rho V^\dagger)$. For $m\geq1$ let

$$
q_m=\max_{\rho}\Big[
 S\bigl(\mathcal{N}^{\otimes m}(\rho)\bigr)-
 S\bigl((\mathcal{N}^c)^{\otimes m}(\rho)\bigr)\Big],
\tag{2}
$$

where $\rho$ ranges over all $m$-qutrit states and $S$ denotes von Neumann entropy in bits. Here $q_m$ in Eq. (2) is the largest coherent information attainable using $m$ copies of the channel jointly. What is the exact value of the unassisted quantum capacity $\mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{N})=\sup_{m\geq1}q_m/m$, in qubits per channel use?
