What is the unassisted quantum capacity of a general qubit Pauli channel? Let $\mathbf p=(p_I,p_X,p_Y,p_Z)$ be a probability vector, with $p_i\geq0$ for $i\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}$ and $\sum_{i\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}}p_i=1$. Define

$$
\Lambda_{\mathbf p}(\rho)
 =p_I\rho+p_XX\rho X+p_YY\rho Y+p_ZZ\rho Z,
\tag{1}
$$

where $\rho$ is a qubit density operator and $X,Y,Z$ are the Pauli matrices. The unassisted quantum capacity $\mathcal Q(\Lambda_{\mathbf p})$ is the supremum of asymptotic qubit transmission rates achievable with vanishing error over independent channel uses. Arbitrary block encodings and joint decoding are allowed, without classical communication assistance or preshared entanglement. Equivalently,

$$
\mathcal Q(\Lambda_{\mathbf p})
 =\sup_{n\geq1}\frac1n\max_{\rho_{A^n}}
 I_c\!\left(\rho_{A^n},\Lambda_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n}\right),
\tag{2}
$$

where $\rho_{A^n}$ ranges over all states of $n$ input qubits [Dev05](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2004.839515), Proposition 7. The coherent information in Eq. (2) is

$$
I_c(\rho,\mathcal N)
 =S(\mathcal N(\rho))-S(\mathcal N^c(\rho)),
\tag{3}
$$

where $\mathcal N^c$ is a complementary channel and $S(\sigma)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\log_2\sigma)$ is the von Neumann entropy used in Eq. (3). Determine $\mathcal Q(\Lambda_{\mathbf p})$ in Eq. (2) for the channel of Eq. (1) throughout the probability simplex, including the exact boundary between zero and positive capacity.
