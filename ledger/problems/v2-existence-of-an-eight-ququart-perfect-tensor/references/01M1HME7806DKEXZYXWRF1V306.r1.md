Wójcik, Makuta, Bruzda, and Augusiak proved that no canonical
  $\mathbb{Z}_{d}$ graph state can be $\operatorname{AME}(N,d)$ when $4$
  divides $N$ and $d$ is even.  This excludes canonical $\mathbb{Z}_{4}$
  graph-state constructions for \eqref{eq:p40-ame-condition}, but their
  theorem does not exclude stabilizer constructions over $\mathbb{F}_{4}$ or
  general non-stabilizer states
  [WMB+26].
