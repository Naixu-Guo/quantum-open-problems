Bernal proved that a minimally supported $\operatorname{AME}(N,4)$
  state exists only for $N\leq 6$.  Thus any solution of
  \eqref{eq:p40-ame-condition} must have strictly more than $4^{4}$ nonzero
  coefficients in every local product basis
  [Ber19].
