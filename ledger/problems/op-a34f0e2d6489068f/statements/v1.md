For every $d\geq3$, do the standard CGLMP Fourier–phase measurements maximize the relative-entropy statistical strength against local realism among all projective $d$-outcome measurements on the fixed state $\lvert\Phi_d\rangle=d^{-1/2}\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\lvert j,j\rangle$, when the setting distribution is also optimized? For a behavior $p(a,b\mid x,y)$, a distribution $\mu(x,y)$ on the four setting pairs, and the local polytope $\mathcal L$, define

$$
S(p;\mu)
 :=\inf_{\ell\in\mathcal L}
 \sum_{a,b,x,y}\mu(x,y)p(a,b\mid x,y)
 \log_2\!\frac{p(a,b\mid x,y)}{\ell(a,b\mid x,y)},
\tag{1}
$$

Set $S^\star(p):=\sup_{\mu\in\Delta(\{0,1\}^2)}S(p;\mu)$; by Eq. (1), this is the optimized asymptotic evidence rate against the best local model. The candidate bases are

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \lvert a;x\rangle
 &=\frac{1}{\sqrt d}\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{2\pi i}{d}j(a+\alpha_x)\right)\lvert j\rangle,\\
 \lvert b;y\rangle
 &=\frac{1}{\sqrt d}\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}
 \exp\!\left(\frac{2\pi i}{d}j(-b+\beta_y)\right)\lvert j\rangle,
 \end{aligned}
\tag{2}
$$

where $\alpha_0=0$, $\alpha_1=-1/2$, $\beta_0=1/4$, and $\beta_1=3/4$. Let $p_{\mathrm{CGLMP}}$ denote the behavior produced on $\lvert\Phi_d\rangle$ by the bases in Eq. (2), and write $p_M$ for the behavior produced by any other measurement choice $M$ on $\lvert\Phi_d\rangle$. The conjectured optimality of Eq. (2) is

$$
S^\star(p_{\mathrm{CGLMP}})
 =\sup_M S^\star(p_M),
\tag{3}
$$

where the supremum is over two projective $d$-outcome measurements per party. Equation (3) is the question to be resolved.
