Does preservation of Gaussian inputs by a trace-decreasing operation imply preservation when a Gaussian reference is attached? Let $\Phi$ be a completely positive trace-nonincreasing linear map on trace-class operators of one bosonic mode. Assume $\Phi(\rho)/\operatorname{Tr}\Phi(\rho)$ is Gaussian for every Gaussian density operator $\rho$ with positive success probability. Let $\operatorname{id}_B$ be the identity channel of a second bosonic mode. Must the state in Eq. (1) be Gaussian for every two-mode Gaussian state $\omega_{AB}$ with positive denominator?

$$
\frac{(\Phi\otimes\operatorname{id}_B)(\omega_{AB})}{\operatorname{Tr}[(\Phi\otimes\operatorname{id}_B)(\omega_{AB})]}.
\tag{1}
$$
