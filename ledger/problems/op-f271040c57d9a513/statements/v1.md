Can pseudorandom-unitary security scale independently of Hilbert-space dimension while construction uses only polynomially many oracle queries?

Question. Can pseudorandom-unitary security be increased independently of Hilbert-space dimension while retaining polynomial construction-query cost?

Use the precise idealized model of [Brakerski26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09957v1), Definitions 4.1 and 4.4. For every dimension $d=2^n$ and security parameter $\kappa$, seek a uniformly generated family $\{U_{n,\kappa,f}\}_f$, indexed by Boolean functions $f:\{0,1\}^{r}\to\{0,1\}$, with

$$
r,\ q\leq\operatorname{poly}(n,\kappa).
\tag{1}
$$

A construction algorithm $\mathcal A^f$ uses at most $q$ oracle queries and satisfies, for every $f$,

$$
\left\|\mathcal A^f-
U_{n,\kappa,f}(\,\cdot\,)U_{n,\kappa,f}^{\dagger}
\right\|_{\diamond}\leq2^{-\kappa}.
\tag{2}
$$

For uniformly random $f$, require

$$
\sup_{\mathcal D:\,\#\mathrm{queries}\leq2^{\kappa}}
\left|\Pr_f[\mathcal D^{U_{n,\kappa,f}}=1]
-\Pr_{V\sim\operatorname{Haar}(d)}[\mathcal D^V=1]\right|
\leq2^{-\kappa}.
\tag{3}
$$

The distinguisher accesses the ideal unitary, not the underlying function $f$. Both algorithms are query-bounded, not necessarily computationally bounded. There must be no imposed scaling relation between $n$ and $\kappa$. [Brakerski26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09957v1)

The displayed definitions, constraints, and target bounds are recorded in Eqs. (1), (2), (3).
