Can every Wigner-negative state in the depolarized qutrit Strange-state family be distilled arbitrarily accurately by catalyst-free stabilizer operations?

Define

$$
|S\rangle=\frac{|1\rangle-|2\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
\rho_\varepsilon=(1-\varepsilon)|S\rangle\langle S|+\varepsilon\frac{I_3}{3}.
\tag{1}
$$

For every $0\leq\varepsilon<3/4$ and $\delta>0$, can finitely many copies of the state in Eq. (1) be converted, with positive success probability, to a state within trace distance $\delta$ of $|S\rangle\langle S|$ using stabilizer ancillas, Clifford unitaries, Pauli measurements, classical feedforward, and discarding, but no magic catalyst?
