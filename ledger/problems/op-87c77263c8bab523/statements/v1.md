Does the ordinary, unrotated Petz map universally recover a tripartite state with fidelity controlled by its conditional mutual information? For a finite-dimensional state $\rho_{ABC}$, define

$$
I(A;B\mid C)_\rho
 :=S(AC)_\rho+S(BC)_\rho-S(C)_\rho-S(ABC)_\rho,
 \qquad
 S(\tau):=-\operatorname{Tr}(\tau\log_2\tau).
\tag{1}
$$

The ordinary Petz map associated with $\rho_{AC}$ and the channel $\operatorname{Tr}_A:AC\to C$ is

$$
\mathcal P^{\rho}_{C\to AC}(X_C)
 :=\rho_{AC}^{1/2}\!\left[
 I_A\otimes\rho_C^{-1/2}X_C\rho_C^{-1/2}
 \right]\rho_{AC}^{1/2},
\tag{2}
$$

where the inverse is taken on $\operatorname{supp}\rho_C$. With squared fidelity $F(\tau,\omega):=\lVert\sqrt\tau\sqrt\omega\rVert_1^2$, determine whether the quantity in Eq. (1) always satisfies

$$
I(A;B\mid C)_\rho
 \stackrel{?}{\geq}
 -\log_2 F\!\left(
 \rho_{ABC},
 (\operatorname{id}_B\otimes\mathcal P^{\rho}_{C\to AC})(\rho_{BC})
 \right),
\tag{3}
$$

with the recovered systems ordered canonically as $ABC$.
