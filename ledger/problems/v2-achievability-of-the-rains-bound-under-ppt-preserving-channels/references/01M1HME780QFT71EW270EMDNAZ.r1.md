Rains showed that the Rains bound $R(\rho_{\mathbf p})$ upper-bounds
  the entanglement distillable from $\rho_{\mathbf p}$ by PPT-preserving
  operations:
  \begin{equation}
    D_{\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho_{\mathbf p})\le R(\rho_{\mathbf p}).
    \label{eq:p4-rains-upper-bound}
  \end{equation}
  Equation \eqref{eq:p4-rains-upper-bound} is the relevant converse bound for
  the operational class in this problem
  [Rai99],
  [Rai01].
