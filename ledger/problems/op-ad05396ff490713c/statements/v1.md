For every integer $d\geq3$, every $x\in(0,1)$, and every $1<p<2$, is the maximal output Schatten $p$-norm of the polarized Werner–Holevo channel multiplicative on two identical copies? Define the Werner–Holevo channel and its polarized interpolation with the identity channel by

$$
\mathcal W_d(X):=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(X)I_d-X^{\mathsf T}}{d-1},
 \qquad
 \Phi_{x,d}:=x\,\operatorname{id}_d+(1-x)\mathcal W_d,
\tag{1}
$$

where the transpose in Eq. (1) is taken in a fixed basis. For a channel $\Phi$ with $d$-dimensional input, set

$$
\lVert A\rVert_p:=\bigl(\operatorname{Tr}\lvert A\rvert^p\bigr)^{1/p},
 \qquad
 \nu_p(\Phi):=\max_{\rho\in\mathcal D(\mathbb C^d)}
 \lVert\Phi(\rho)\rVert_p.
\tag{2}
$$

With the convention in Eq. (2), determine whether

$$
\nu_p(\Phi_{x,d}\otimes\Phi_{x,d})
 =\nu_p(\Phi_{x,d})^2
\tag{3}
$$

holds throughout the stated parameter range.
