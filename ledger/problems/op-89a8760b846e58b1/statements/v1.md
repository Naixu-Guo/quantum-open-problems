Alice, Bob, and Charlie share $n$ copies of the standard tripartite W state. Let $\mathrm{W}_3$ be the projector onto $(\ket{100}+\ket{010}+\ket{001})/\sqrt{3}$.

For a proposed rate $r$, the parties aim to distill $\lfloor rn\rfloor$ copies of the tripartite GHZ state with vanishing trace-distance error on the full target. Let $\mathrm{GHZ}_3$ be the projector onto $(\ket{000}+\ket{111})/\sqrt{2}$.

What is the optimal LOCC distillation rate $R_{\mathrm{W}\to\mathrm{GHZ}}$? The rate is defined by

$$
R_{\mathrm{W}\to\mathrm{GHZ}}=\sup\left\{r\geq 0:\lim_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\Lambda_n\in\mathrm{LOCC}}
 \tfrac12\left\lVert\Lambda_n\bigl(\mathrm{W}_3^{\otimes n}\bigr)
 -\mathrm{GHZ}_3^{\otimes\lfloor rn\rfloor}\right\rVert_1=0\right\}.
\tag{1}
$$

In Eq. (1), the infimum is over LOCC channels with $\lfloor rn\rfloor$ output qubits per party.

Give an achievable rate and a matching converse for arbitrary LOCC, counting GHZ states per W state supplied.
