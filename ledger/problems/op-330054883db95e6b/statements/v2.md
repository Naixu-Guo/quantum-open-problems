Can every balanced RSA semiprime be factored in polynomial time using sublinear logical quantum space?

Let $N=pq$ be an $n$-bit integer whose distinct odd prime factors have bit length $n/2+O(1)$. Does a uniform hybrid algorithm exist that factors every such $N$ with probability at least $2/3$ while satisfying

$$
q(n)=o(n),
\qquad
T_{\mathrm{total}}(n)=n^{O(1)}?
\tag{1}
$$

Here $q(n)$ in Eq. (1) counts all simultaneously live logical qubits, including control, data, workspace, quantum memory, and resource states. Polynomial classical storage, intermediate measurements, and resets are allowed, and total runtime includes repetitions and classical processing.
