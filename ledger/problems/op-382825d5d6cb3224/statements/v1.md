Let $\Phi$ and $\Psi$ be finite-dimensional quantum channels. Write $S(\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\log_2\rho)$, and define the Holevo information of a channel $\mathcal N$ by

$$
\chi(\mathcal N)=\sup_{\{p_x,\rho_x\}}\left[
 S\!\left(\sum_x p_x\mathcal N(\rho_x)\right)
 -\sum_xp_x S\bigl(\mathcal N(\rho_x)\bigr)\right].
\tag{1}
$$

The supremum is over finite ensembles of channel input states. Its unassisted classical capacity is $C(\mathcal N)=\sup_{n\geq1}\chi(\mathcal N^{\otimes n})/n$, using Eq. (1) with arbitrary ensembles on the joint input. Does

$$
C(\Phi\otimes\Psi)=C(\Phi)+C(\Psi)
\tag{2}
$$

hold for every pair of such channels? For the product channel in Eq. (2), one use consists of one use of each factor.
