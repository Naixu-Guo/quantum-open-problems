Block-comb semidefinite programs bound the asymptotic precision under correlated noise, but no theorem shows that the best block bound equals the precision of a physical protocol.

Metrology bounds guide claims about attainable precision under memory noise. Tightness would certify that the block semidefinite programs predict a physical protocol rather than an unattainable relaxation.

## Interpretation

The defensible open problem is the tightness of the asymptotic coefficient. The broader scaling-dichotomy formulation in the lead repository goes beyond the explicit claim in the source paper.
