Data processing proves
  $\mathfrak D\subseteq\mathfrak L_\infty$.  Watanabe showed that regularized
  less-noisy channels have single-letter quantum and private capacities,
  $\mathcal Q(\mathcal T)=\mathcal C_p(\mathcal T)
  =\mathcal Q^{(1)}(\mathcal T)$
  [Wat12].  Hirche and Leditzky further proved
  that superactivation of private capacity would imply
  $\mathfrak D\subsetneq\mathfrak L_\infty$; neither result decides whether
  the inclusion is strict [HL23].
