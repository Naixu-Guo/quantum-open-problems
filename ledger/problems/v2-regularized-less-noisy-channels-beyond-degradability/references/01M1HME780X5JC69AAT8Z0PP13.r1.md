Smith and Wu derived a sufficient condition under which a flagged
  mixture of degradable and antidegradable channels is regularized less noisy
  while remaining nondegradable.  For their amplitude-damping candidates, the
  condition reduces to strict positivity of a multi-copy comparison
  coefficient; they leave that positivity unproved.  Their construction is
  therefore a conditional route to a separation, not a counterexample
  [SW25].
