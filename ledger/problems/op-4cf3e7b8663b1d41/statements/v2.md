Is the Rains bound asymptotically achievable by completely positive-partial-transpose-preserving (completely PPT-preserving) channels for every full-rank two-qubit Bell-diagonal state, and what channel family achieves it? Consider

$$
\rho_{\mathbf p}
 =p_I\lvert\Phi^+\rangle\!\langle\Phi^+\rvert
 +p_X\lvert\Psi^+\rangle\!\langle\Psi^+\rvert
 +p_Y\lvert\Psi^-\rangle\!\langle\Psi^-\rvert
 +p_Z\lvert\Phi^-\rangle\!\langle\Phi^-\rvert,
\tag{1}
$$

where $p_i>0$ and $p_I+p_X+p_Y+p_Z=1$, and $\lvert\Phi^\pm\rangle:=(\lvert00\rangle\pm\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and $\lvert\Psi^\pm\rangle:=(\lvert01\rangle\pm\lvert10\rangle)/\sqrt2$.

A channel $\Lambda_n:A^nB^n\to A'_nB'_n$ is completely PPT-preserving if

$$
\Lambda_n\text{ is CPTP},\qquad
 \Gamma_{B'_n}\circ\Lambda_n\circ\Gamma_{B^n}
 \text{ is completely positive},
\tag{2}
$$

where $\Gamma$ is partial transposition on the indicated subsystem. Equation (2) requires PPT preservation also with arbitrary local ancillas. Write $D(\rho\|\tau)=\operatorname{Tr}\rho(\log_2\rho-\log_2\tau)$ with the standard support convention, and define

$$
R(\rho):=\inf_{\tau\geq0,\,\|\tau^{\Gamma_B}\|_1\leq1}
 D(\rho\|\tau).
\tag{3}
$$

For the state in Eq. (1), achievability of the Rains bound in Eq. (3) means a sequence satisfying Eq. (2) and

$$
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log_2 M_n}{n}
 \geq R(\rho_{\mathbf p}),\qquad
 \big\|\Lambda_n(\rho_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n})
 -\Phi_{M_n}\big\|_1\longrightarrow0,
\tag{4}
$$

where $M_n$ is a positive integer and $\Phi_M:=M^{-1}\sum_{i,j=1}^M|ii\rangle\langle jj|$. Equation (4) imposes no efficiency requirement on the construction.
