## Formal statement

Does there exist a passive self-correcting quantum memory in three spatial
dimensions?  Consider finite-dimensional spins on a three-dimensional lattice
$\Lambda_L$ of linear size $L$ and a Hamiltonian
$H_L=\sum_{X\subseteq\Lambda_L}h_X$ whose interaction range, local strength
$\lVert h_X\rVert$, and number of terms incident on each spin are bounded
independently of $L$.  Let its ground space $\mathcal C_L$ encode a logical
space $\mathcal Q_L$ with $\dim\mathcal Q_L\ge2$, and let
$\mathcal V_L:\mathcal S(\mathcal Q_L)\to\mathcal S(\mathcal C_L)$ be the
channel induced by an isometric encoding into $\mathcal C_L$.

During storage no active control or error correction is permitted.  The
encoded state evolves under a local thermal channel
$\mathcal E_{t,\beta}^{(L)}$ at inverse temperature $\beta$, such as a Davies
semigroup, followed only at readout by a decoder $\mathcal D_L$.  For a fixed
$0<\varepsilon<1$, define the worst-case storage time by
\begin{equation}
  \tau_L(\beta,\varepsilon)
  :=\sup\!\left\{t\ge0:
       \sup_{0\le s\le t}\ \sup_{\rho\in\mathcal S(\mathcal Q_L)}
       \left\|
         \bigl(\mathcal D_L\circ\mathcal E_{s,\beta}^{(L)}
         \circ\mathcal V_L\bigr)(\rho)-\rho
       \right\|_1
       \le\varepsilon\right\},
  \label{eq:p9-memory-time}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal S(\mathcal Q_L)$ is the set of logical states.  The problem is
to construct such a Hamiltonian family with efficient decoders and a finite
critical inverse temperature $\beta_c$ for which
\begin{equation}
  \beta>\beta_c
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \lim_{L\to\infty}\tau_L(\beta,\varepsilon)=\infty.
  \label{eq:p9-self-correction-condition}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:p9-self-correction-condition} requires the lifetime in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p9-memory-time} to diverge at fixed nonzero temperature as
$L\to\infty$; growth that terminates at a temperature-dependent system size is
only partial self-correction.
