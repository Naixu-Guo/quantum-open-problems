## Comment

The 2026 construction resolves the long-standing question under a thermal
syndrome encoding and provides substantially stronger progress than earlier
finite-size candidates.  Under the deliberately stricter formulation in this
section, the remaining issue is to establish the same thermodynamic lifetime
starting from the isometric ground-space encoding $\mathcal V_L$ in
Eq. \eqref{eq:p9-memory-time}, or to prove that the two initialization models
are equivalent for this construction.
