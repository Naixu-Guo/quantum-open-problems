What is the squashed entanglement $E_{\mathrm{sq}}(\Lambda_p)$ of the qubit depolarizing channel, defined for a mixing parameter $p\in[0,1]$ by

$$
\Lambda_p(\rho):=(1-p)\,\rho+p\,\mathrm{Tr}[\rho]\,\frac{I}{2}
 =\bigl(1-\tfrac{3p}{4}\bigr)\rho
 +\tfrac{p}{4}\bigl(X\rho X+Y\rho Y+Z\rho Z\bigr),
\tag{1}
$$

where $\rho$ ranges over qubit states, $I$ is the qubit identity, and $X,Y,Z$ are the Pauli matrices, so that the total Pauli error probability, read off from the second expression in Eq. (1), is $\tfrac{3p}{4}$? Let $U:A'\to BE$ be an isometric extension of $\Lambda_p$, let $\varphi_{RA'}$ range over pure bipartite input states, and let $S:E\to E'$ range over completely positive trace-preserving maps of arbitrary finite output dimension. For $\omega_{RBE'}:=(\mathrm{id}_R\otimes(S\circ U))(\varphi_{RA'})$ the squashed entanglement of the channel is

$$
E_{\mathrm{sq}}(\Lambda_p)
 :=\max_{\varphi_{RA'}}\ \frac{1}{2}\inf_{S}I(R;B|E')_\omega,
\tag{2}
$$

where $I(R;B|E')_\omega$ is the conditional mutual information and all logarithms are base 2 [TGW14](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2014.2330313). The quantity in Eq. (2) is additive over tensor products of channels and upper-bounds the two-way-assisted quantum and private capacities, $Q_2(\Lambda_p)\le E_{\mathrm{sq}}(\Lambda_p)$ and $P_2(\Lambda_p)\le E_{\mathrm{sq}}(\Lambda_p)$ [TGW14](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2014.2330313); this is its operational role, since an exact value would be a single-letter upper bound on secret and quantum transmission over depolarizing noise. Determine $E_{\mathrm{sq}}(\Lambda_p)$ as an explicit function of $p$ on $[0,1]$.
