## Source

Damski and Życzkowski ask for complete isoentangled sets on two equal prime-dimensional systems beyond qubits in Sec. I [DZ26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18232v1). The common-spectrum formulation uses the local-unitary definition in Czartowski et al. [CGGZ20](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.12291).

## Progress

A complete set of five isoentangled bases on two qubits is constructed in Czartowski et al. [CGGZ20](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.12291).

Damski et al. construct nine mutually unbiased Hadamard bases generated by local Weyl–Heisenberg actions on a fiducial vector in the two-qutrit case. Appending the computational basis yields a complete mutually unbiased set whose extra basis consists of product states (Sec. IV.2 and Theorem 2) [DBPCZ26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15550v2).

## Comment

The [unrestricted maximum number of mutually unbiased bases](https://qiqc-op.com/problem/op_c9c62042b15fcb06/) in dimension nine is ten. The question here requires one common entanglement spectrum across that complete set. The catalog and literature were checked through November 26, 2026.
