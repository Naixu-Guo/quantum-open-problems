Known examples are consistent with the proposed completely bounded entropy or coherent-information conditions, but no theorem covers every multiplicativity counterexample.

A universal entropy signature would explain why known output-norm counterexamples work and connect multiplicativity failure to coherent information.

## Interpretation

A statement that coherent information is zero must specify whether this is an input value, the channel maximum, or a capacity.
