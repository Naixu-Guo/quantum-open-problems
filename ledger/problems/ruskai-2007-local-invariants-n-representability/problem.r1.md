Graph-indexed complete invariant families and a free stable invariant algebra are known. A finite-dimensional minimal set for arbitrary mixed two-particle states and the full N-representability constraints remain unknown.

Local invariants provide basis-independent coordinates for two-particle states. Expressing N-representability in those coordinates would connect orbit classification to the quantum marginal problem.
