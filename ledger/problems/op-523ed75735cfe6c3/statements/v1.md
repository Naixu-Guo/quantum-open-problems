Can every finite-dimensional quantum channel be written as the uniform mixture of $d_B$ channels whose Choi ranks are at most the input dimension? Let $A$ and $B$ have dimensions $d_A$ and $d_B$, respectively, and let $\Phi:\mathcal L(A)\to\mathcal L(B)$ be completely positive and trace preserving. In a fixed orthonormal basis of $A$, define its Choi operator by

$$
J(\Phi)
 :=\sum_{i,j=1}^{d_A}
 \lvert i\rangle\!\langle j\rvert_A\otimes
 \Phi\!\left(\lvert i\rangle\!\langle j\rvert_A\right),
\tag{1}
$$

The rank of the operator in Eq. (1) is independent of the chosen basis. Determine whether every $\Phi$ admits completely positive trace-preserving maps $\Phi_1,\ldots,\Phi_{d_B}:\mathcal L(A)\to\mathcal L(B)$ satisfying

$$
\Phi=\frac1{d_B}\sum_{r=1}^{d_B}\Phi_r,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}J(\Phi_r)\leq d_A
 \quad\text{for every }r\in\{1,\ldots,d_B\}.
\tag{2}
$$

Thus Eq. (2) requires both the prescribed input-dimension rank bound and exactly equal mixing weights.
