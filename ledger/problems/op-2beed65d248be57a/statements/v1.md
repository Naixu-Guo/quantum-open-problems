For $0<\eta<1$ and $0<\nu<\infty$, let the single-mode bosonic thermal attenuator be

$$
\Phi_{\eta,\nu}(\rho_A)
 :=\operatorname{Tr}_{E'}\!\left[
 U_\eta(\rho_A\otimes\tau_{\nu,E})U_\eta^\dagger
 \right],
 \qquad
 \tau_{\nu,E}:=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
 \frac{\nu^k}{(\nu+1)^{k+1}}
 |k\rangle_E\!\langle k|_E,
\tag{1}
$$

where $U_\eta:AE\to BE'$ is a beam-splitter unitary and $\tau_{\nu,E}$ acts on the environment mode $E$. For an environment mode of angular frequency $\omega_E$ at temperature $T$, $\nu=(e^{\hbar\omega_E/(k_{\rm B}T)}-1)^{-1}$. Thus $0<T<\infty$ is equivalent to $0<\nu<\infty$ for fixed $\omega_E>0$; $\nu=0$ corresponds to $T=0$, while $\nu\to\infty$ as $T\to\infty$. Define its unassisted quantum capacity and its critical transmissivity by

$$
\mathcal Q(\Phi_{\eta,\nu})
 :=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\sup_{\rho_{A^n}}
 I_{\rm c}(\rho_{A^n},\Phi_{\eta,\nu}^{\otimes n}),
 \qquad
 \eta_{\rm c}(\nu)
 :=\inf\{\eta\in(0,1):\mathcal Q(\Phi_{\eta,\nu})>0\},
\tag{2}
$$

where $I_{\rm c}(\rho,\mathcal N)
:=S(\mathcal N(\rho))-S(\mathcal N^{\rm c}(\rho))$. Determine the exact threshold curve in Eq. (2) for the channel in Eq. (1).
