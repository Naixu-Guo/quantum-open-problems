Can the Pauli periodicity of gates in one fixed level of the qubit Clifford hierarchy above the Clifford group grow at least polynomially with the number of qubits? For $n\geq1$, let $\mathcal{P}_n$ be the $n$-qubit Pauli group, generated by $iI$ and the single-qubit operators $X_j,Y_j,Z_j$, and let $\mathbb{T}:=\{e^{i\theta}:\theta\in\mathbb{R}\}$. Define the hierarchy modulo global phase by

$$
\mathcal{C}_1(n):=\mathbb{T}\mathcal{P}_n,
 \qquad
 \mathcal{C}_{k+1}(n):=
 \bigl\{U\in U(2^n):UPU^\dagger\in\mathcal{C}_k(n)
 \ \text{for every}\ P\in\mathcal{P}_n\bigr\},
 \qquad k\geq1,
\tag{1}
$$

so that $\mathcal{C}_2(n)$ is the Clifford group. For a unitary $U\in U(2^n)$, its projective Pauli periodicity is

$$
m(U):=\min\bigl\{m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq0}:U^{2^m}\in\mathbb{T}\mathcal{P}_n\bigr\},
\tag{2}
$$

with $m(U):=\infty$ when no such $m$ exists. Thus $m(U)$ counts repeated squarings rather than the total number of powers, and scalar phases are ignored, so that $m(e^{i\theta}U)=m(U)$. For a level $k$ of Eq. (1), set

$$
M_k(n):=\sup\bigl\{m(U):U\in\mathcal{C}_k(n),\ m(U)<\infty\bigr\}.
\tag{3}
$$

The question asks whether the quantity in Eq. (3) satisfies

$$
\exists\,k\geq3\ \exists\,a,c>0\ \forall\,N\geq1\ \exists\,n\geq N:
 \qquad M_k(n)\geq a\,n^{c}.
\tag{4}
$$

A negative answer means that $M_k(n)=o(n^{c})$ as $n\to\infty$ for every fixed $k\geq3$ and every $c>0$.
