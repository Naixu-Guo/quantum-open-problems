Is every finite-dimensional extensibly causal process matrix purifiable? Call an $N$-laboratory process matrix $W$ extensibly causal if $W\otimes\rho_R$ generates only causal correlations for every ancillary input system $R$, every joint ancillary state $\rho_R$, and every choice of local instruments. Call $W$ purifiable if there exist auxiliary global past and future systems, a fixed pure state on the auxiliary past, and a pure process $S$ such that inserting the fixed state into $S$ and discarding the auxiliary future yields $W$. If $\mathrm{EC}_N$ and $\mathrm{Pur}_N$ denote the corresponding classes, the question is whether

$$
\mathrm{EC}_N\subseteq\mathrm{Pur}_N
\tag{1}
$$

holds for every $N$ and every choice of finite local dimensions. In particular, does the inclusion in Eq. (1) hold for two laboratories?
