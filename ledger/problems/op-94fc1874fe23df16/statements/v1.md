Does there exist a fixed family of $n$-qubit circuit layouts with $m=\operatorname{poly}(n)$ one- and two-qubit gates for which the following task is $\#\mathrm{P}$-hard? Choose every gate independently from Haar measure, obtaining $C$. Given $C$ and $\epsilon,\delta\in(0,1)$, estimate the all-zero output probability with

$$
p_0(C)=|\langle0^n|C|0^n\rangle|^2,\qquad
 \Pr_C[|\widetilde p(C)-p_0(C)|\leq\epsilon 2^{-n}]\geq1-\delta.
\tag{1}
$$

The guarantee in Eq. (1) must hold for arbitrary $\epsilon,\delta$, with running time measured in $n,1/\epsilon,1/\delta$.
