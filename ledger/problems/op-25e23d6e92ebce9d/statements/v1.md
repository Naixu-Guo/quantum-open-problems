For a density operator $\rho$, write $S(\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}\rho\log_2\rho$. For a bipartite state $\rho_{AB}$, the *entanglement of formation* is

$$
E_F(\rho_{AB})=\inf_{\{p_x,\psi_x\}}\ \sum_x p_x\, S\big(\operatorname{Tr}_B\lvert\psi_x\rangle\!\langle\psi_x\rvert\big),
\tag{1}
$$

where the infimum runs over pure-state ensembles with $\sum_x p_x\lvert\psi_x\rangle\!\langle\psi_x\rvert=\rho_{AB}$; and for a channel $\mathcal N$, the *Holevo capacity* is

$$
\chi(\mathcal N)=\sup_{\{p_x,\rho_x\}}\ \Big[ S\Big(\sum_x p_x\,\mathcal N(\rho_x)\Big)-\sum_x p_x\,S\big(\mathcal N(\rho_x)\big)\Big],
\tag{2}
$$

where the supremum runs over finite input ensembles. Hastings’ random-channel construction, combined with Shor’s equivalence chain and Pomeransky’s reduction, proves that states $\rho,\sigma$ and channels $\mathcal N_1,\mathcal N_2$ *exist* with

$$
E_F(\rho\otimes\sigma)<E_F(\rho)+E_F(\sigma)
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 \chi(\mathcal N_1\otimes\mathcal N_2)>\chi(\mathcal N_1)+\chi(\mathcal N_2),
\tag{3}
$$

and that the two violations in (3) interconvert constructively; since Shor’s reduction passes through the minimum output entropy $H_{\min}(\mathcal N)=\min_\rho S(\mathcal N(\rho))$, equivalently there exist channels with $H_{\min}(\mathcal N\otimes\mathcal N)<2\,H_{\min}(\mathcal N)$. Every known proof at the von Neumann point is probabilistic and produces no concrete instance.

**Problem.** Write down a *feasible* witness for (3) at the von Neumann point: a pair of states or channels specified by simple closed-form data of bounded size — Kraus or Stinespring operators with rational or algebraic entries, or permutations of feasibly small cardinality, with input and output dimensions below any fixed constant (in particular below the randomized existence dimension $183$) — together with a checkable certificate, in exact rational arithmetic or interval bounds, for the strict inequality between $E_F$ and $\chi$ as defined in (1) and (2); or prove that no violation exists below an explicit dimension bound $D$, that is, $E_F$ and $\chi$ are additive on all pairs of channels with input and output dimensions at most $D$, so that any witness in the sense of (3) requires larger dimension.

Two kinds of partial progress do *not* settle the problem: a deterministic algorithm whose output dimensions tend to infinity (asymptotic derandomization), and explicit channel pairs violating additivity only at Rényi orders $p\ne 1$.
