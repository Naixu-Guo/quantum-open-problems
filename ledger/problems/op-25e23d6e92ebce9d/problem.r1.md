## Source

The closed-form question for the Holevo capacity is stated explicitly by Lovitz and Wu[LovWu26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25961), who derive the first derandomized witness and record verbatim that *finding a simple closed-form or practically computable counterexample remains open*. The entanglement-of-formation side is registered, as resolved existentially only, by the formalization of Open Quantum Problem 7[Kre26](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/issues/3413); the attribution there is to the quantum-information open-problem tradition through Shor’s equivalence chain, not to a single posing paper.

## Progress

Hastings constructed random unitary channels whose minimum output entropy is nonadditive in sufficiently large dimension, yielding existentially the violations of (3), with superadditivity of the Holevo capacity under entangled inputs [Has09](https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys1224).

Shor proved that additivity of $\chi$, additivity of $H_{\min}$, additivity of $E_F$, and strong superadditivity of $E_F$ all interreduce, and Pomeransky supplied the reduction from additivity to strong superadditivity [Sho04](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0305035) [Pom03](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.68.032317). The reductions are constructive, so a feasible witness at any point of the chain yields one at every other point, with dimension blow-up.

Belinschi, Collins, and Nechita sharpened the randomized construction to violations approaching one bit at output dimension $183$, the best small-dimension randomized bound [BCN16](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.1567).

Explicit and even computer-certified witnesses exist only away from the von Neumann point: Cubitt, Ruskai, and Smith gave explicit low-dimensional channels violating minimum output $p$-Rényi entropy additivity for $p>4.78$ [CRS08](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-008-0625-z); Derksen and Lovitz gave constructive counterexamples for all $p>1$ [DL26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07547); and Krohn-Grimberghe certified, in integer arithmetic, an explicit pair violating additivity for $0<p\le 1/22$ [KG26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17376).

Leung, Lovitz, and Wu extended the randomized nonadditivity to $p>3/4$ and $0\le p<1/4$ via correlated random-projection constructions; at $p=1$ the abstract confirms only the random-channel line applies [LLW26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15210).

Lovitz and Wu replaced Haar randomness by random permutations and derandomized Hastings’ argument in an asymptotic algorithmic sense: some tuple of $57{,}836{,}025$ permutations on a set of size at most $5.4\times 10^{116216}$ exists, but no simple closed-form or practically computable counterexample is known [LovWu26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25961). Zhen, Zhu, Chen, and Wang gave a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm outputting certified nonadditive mixed-unitary channels whose dimensions tend to infinity [ZZCW26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31081).

For contrast, the quantum-capacity analogue already has explicit small-dimensional examples — Leditzky, Leung, Siddhu, Smith, and Smolin exhibited generic nonadditivity of coherent information using the $50\%$ erasure channel combined with a PPT-binding channel [LLSSS23](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.08377) — underscoring that at the von Neumann point it is smallness and verifiability, not existence, that remain open.

## Comment

What remains unresolved is a witness for (3) that a person can write down and check: no closed-form, bounded-dimension, or feasibly verifiable counterexample is known at the von Neumann point, the smallest input and output dimensions harboring any violation are unknown (in particular whether qubit-channel pairs can violate $H_{\min}$ additivity is open), and the deterministic derandomizations of 2026 output channels whose dimensions tend to infinity. This record merges the Holevo-capacity and entanglement-of-formation formulations because Shor’s and Pomeransky’s reductions convert witnesses constructively. It is distinct from the delayed-onset additivity record (which asks, for Rényi orders, when self-power additivity first breaks) and from the polarized Werner–Holevo multiplicativity record (which asks multiplicativity to *hold* for a specific family); the smallest-dimension subquestion stated above is a natural future sibling record.
