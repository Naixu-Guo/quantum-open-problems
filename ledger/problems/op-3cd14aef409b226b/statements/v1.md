Is Guha’s multimode Strong Conjecture 2 true for every correlated input state? Fix $n\geq1$, $K\geq0$, and $0<\eta<1$. Let the modes $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ be in the vacuum and let $\rho_{B^n}$ be an arbitrary joint $n$-mode state. Apply identical beam splitters whose output annihilation operators satisfy

$$
\hat c_i=\sqrt{\eta}\,\hat a_i
 +\sqrt{1-\eta}\,\hat b_i,
 \qquad i\in\{1,\ldots,n\},
\tag{1}
$$

and denote the resulting joint state of $C_1,\ldots,C_n$ by $\rho_{C^n}$. In the convention of Eq. (1), $\eta$ multiplies the vacuum $A$ port, so the attenuator from the information-bearing $B$ port to $C$ has transmissivity $1-\eta$. Define the thermal entropy function by

$$
g(x):=(x+1)\log_2(x+1)-x\log_2x,
 \qquad x\geq0,
\tag{2}
$$

with $0\log_2 0:=0$. Using the function in Eq. (2), impose the sole input-entropy constraint $S(\rho_{B^n})=ng(K)$. Does the state produced in Eq. (1) always obey

$$
S(\rho_{C^n})\geq ng((1-\eta)K)?
\tag{3}
$$

Equality in Eq. (3) is attained when $\rho_{B^n}$ is a tensor product of $n$ thermal states of mean photon number $K$; the conjecture asserts that arbitrary correlations cannot lower the output entropy further.
