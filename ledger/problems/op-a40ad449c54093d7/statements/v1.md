For every odd integer $d\geq5$, do there exist $r=d(d+1)/2$ unitary operators $U_1,\ldots,U_r$ on $\mathbb C^d$ such that

$$
\Phi_d(X):=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}(X)I_d+X^{\mathsf T}}{d+1}
 =\frac1r\sum_{j=1}^{r}U_j XU_j^\dagger
 \qquad\text{for every }X\in\mathcal L(\mathbb C^d)?
\tag{1}
$$

The transpose in Eq. (1) is taken in a fixed orthonormal basis.
