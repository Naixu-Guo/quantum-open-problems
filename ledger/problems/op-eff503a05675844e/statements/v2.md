Is every fixed-temperature Gibbs state of a bounded finite-range qubit Hamiltonian on the square lattice an exact convex combination of PEPS with system-size-independent bond dimension?

Let $\Lambda$ be a finite rectangular square lattice and $H=\sum_Xh_X$ satisfy $\operatorname{diam}(X)\leq R$ and $\max_{v\in\Lambda}\sum_{X\ni v}\|h_X\|\leq J$. Let $\mathcal P_\chi(\Lambda)$ be the normalized pure PEPS on $\Lambda$ with bond dimension at most $\chi$.

For every fixed $0<\beta<\infty$, does a finite $\chi(\beta,R,J)$ exist, independent of $|\Lambda|$ and $H$, such that

$$
\frac{e^{-\beta H}}{\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-\beta H})}
\in\operatorname{conv}\{|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|:
\psi\in\mathcal P_{\chi(\beta,R,J)}(\Lambda)\}?
\tag{1}
$$

Equation (1) asks for exact finite convex membership, with no efficiency requirement.
