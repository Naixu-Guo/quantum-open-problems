Several nearby measures and one-way distillable entanglement are lockable, but the requested two-way distillable entanglement and arbitrary-state distillable key questions remain unresolved.

An affirmative answer would show that losing one qubit can destroy an unbounded amount of recoverable entanglement or secret key. That behavior sets a sharp limit on the robustness of these resources.

## Interpretation

Alice-or-Bob loss of a subsystem is not the same operational model as changing an eavesdropper's side information.
