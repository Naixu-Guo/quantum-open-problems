What is the entanglement cost of the isotropic state $\sigma_{d,F}$ for every integer $d\geq2$ and every fidelity $1/d<F<1$? On $\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^d$, let $\lvert\Phi_d\rangle:=d^{-1/2}\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\lvert jj\rangle$ and $\Phi_d:=\lvert\Phi_d\rangle\!\langle\Phi_d\rvert$. For $F\in[0,1]$, the isotropic state with fidelity $F$ is

$$
\sigma_{d,F}:=F\,\Phi_d+(1-F)\,\frac{I-\Phi_d}{d^2-1}.
\tag{1}
$$

The state in Eq. (1) is invariant under $U\otimes\overline U$ for every unitary $U$ on $\mathbb C^d$, is separable for $F\leq1/d$, and is entangled for $F>1/d$. The entanglement cost $E_C(\rho)$ of a bipartite state $\rho$ is the infimum of the rates $R$ for which local operations and classical communication (LOCC) transform $\lceil nR\rceil$ ebits into states whose trace distance from $\rho^{\otimes n}$ tends to zero as $n\to\infty$. Determine $E_C(\sigma_{d,F})$ for all integers $d\geq2$ and all $1/d<F<1$, including the qubit case $d=2$.
