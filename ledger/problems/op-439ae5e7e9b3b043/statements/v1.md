For which of the parameter pairs specified below does an absolutely maximally entangled state exist? A normalized vector $|\psi\rangle\in(\mathbb C^d)^{\otimes n}$ is an $\operatorname{AME}(n,d)$ state when

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_{S^c}\!\left(|\psi\rangle\!\langle\psi|\right)
 =\frac{I_{d^{|S|}}}{d^{|S|}}
 \quad\text{for every }S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}
 \text{ with }|S|\leq\left\lfloor\frac n2\right\rfloor.
\tag{1}
$$

Determine whether a state satisfying Eq. (1) exists for every pair in

$$
\mathcal U:=\bigl\{(8,6),(8,10),(9,6),(9,10),(10,6),(10,10),
 (11,3),(11,6),(11,10),(12,6),(12,10)\bigr\}.
\tag{2}
$$

Thus the task is to classify every pair in Eq. (2) by existence or nonexistence, without restricting to stabilizer or minimal-support states.
