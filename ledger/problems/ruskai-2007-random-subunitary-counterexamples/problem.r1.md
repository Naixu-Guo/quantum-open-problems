General random channels violate multiplicativity, but no verified new counterexample has been found inside the intended cyclic sub-unitary family.

The problem tests whether the rigid cyclic Kraus structure can support the multiplicativity violations known for less structured random channels.

## Interpretation

The source's displayed Kraus operators do not define a trace-preserving map as printed; the status concerns the intended corrected family.
