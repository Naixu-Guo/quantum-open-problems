For any given probability vector $\mathbf p=(p_I,p_X,p_Y,p_Z)$, does there exist a family of quantum low-density parity-check (LDPC) stabilizer codes that reliably transmits quantum information through the corresponding Pauli channel at a rate at least $R_{\mathrm{hash}}(\mathbf p)$?

The qubit Pauli channel is

$$
\Lambda_{\mathbf p}(\rho)
 =p_I\rho+p_XX\rho X+p_YY\rho Y+p_ZZ\rho Z,
 \qquad p_I+p_X+p_Y+p_Z=1,
\tag{1}
$$

where every $p_i\ge0$, $I$ is the identity, and $X,Y,Z$ are the Pauli operators. The qubit depolarizing channel is the specialization of Eq. (1) given by

$$
\mathcal D_p(\rho)
 =(1-p)\rho+\frac p3\bigl(X\rho X+Y\rho Y+Z\rho Z\bigr),
 \qquad 0\le p\le1.
\tag{2}
$$

Thus Eq. (2) has $\mathbf p=(1-p,p/3,p/3,p/3)$. For the general channel in Eq. (1), define the hashing rate by

$$
R_{\mathrm{hash}}(\mathbf p)
 :=\max\{0,1-H(\mathbf p)\},
 \qquad
 H(\mathbf p):=-\sum_{i\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}}p_i\log_2p_i,
 \qquad 0\log_2 0:=0.
\tag{3}
$$

A family of stabilizer codes $[[n,k_n]]$ is quantum LDPC if it has stabilizer generators of uniformly bounded weight and each qubit participates in a uniformly bounded number of generators.

To specify reliable quantum transmission, let $\mathcal E_n$ be an isometric encoding channel from $k_n$ logical qubits into the code space on $n$ physical qubits, and let $\mathcal D_n$ be a completely positive trace-preserving decoding channel back to the $k_n$ logical qubits. Transmission is unassisted: no preshared entanglement or auxiliary communication is available. Let $\lvert\Phi_n\rangle$ be a maximally entangled state of the logical register and a reference register $A_n$, each of dimension $2^{k_n}$. Define the entanglement fidelity of the encoded, transmitted, and decoded state by

$$
F_n:=\langle\Phi_n\rvert
 \Bigl[\operatorname{id}_{A_n}\otimes
 \bigl(\mathcal D_n\circ\Lambda_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n}
 \circ\mathcal E_n\bigr)\Bigr]
 \bigl(\lvert\Phi_n\rangle\langle\Phi_n\rvert\bigr)
 \lvert\Phi_n\rangle.
\tag{4}
$$

A rate $R\ge0$ is reliably achievable by this family with these encoders and decoders if both

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}F_n=1,
 \qquad
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{k_n}{n}\ge R.
\tag{5}
$$

Thus $k_n/n$ is the code rate, while reliable transmission in Eq. (5) also requires the entanglement fidelity in Eq. (4) to tend to one.

The question requires such a family and encoders and decoders for every $\mathbf p$, with $R\ge R_{\mathrm{hash}}(\mathbf p)$ from Eq. (3). The family, encoders, decoders, and LDPC bounds may depend on $\mathbf p$, but the weight and degree bounds must be independent of $n$. When $R_{\mathrm{hash}}(\mathbf p)=0$, transmitting no logical qubits is allowed; the positive-rate requirement concerns channels with $H(\mathbf p)<1$.
