Exact antidegradability gives the lower bound
  \begin{equation}
    \eta_{\rm c}(\nu)\geq\eta_{\rm AD}(\nu)
    :=\frac{\nu+\frac12}{\nu+1}.
    \label{eq:p50-antidegradable-lower-bound}
  \end{equation}
  The capacity vanishes at and below the right-hand side of
  Eq. \eqref{eq:p50-antidegradable-lower-bound}
  [LKA+19].
