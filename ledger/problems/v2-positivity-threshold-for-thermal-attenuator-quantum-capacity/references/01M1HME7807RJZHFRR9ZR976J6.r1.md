Thermal input states give the upper bound
  \begin{equation}
    \eta_{\rm c}(\nu)\leq\eta_{\rm G}(\nu)
    :=\frac{2^{g(\nu)}}{1+2^{g(\nu)}},
    \qquad
    g(x):=(x+1)\log_2(x+1)-x\log_2x.
    \label{eq:p50-gaussian-upper-bound}
  \end{equation}
  Above the right-hand side of Eq. \eqref{eq:p50-gaussian-upper-bound}, the
  thermal-state coherent information is positive
  [HW01].
