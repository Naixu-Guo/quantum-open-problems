Does the pure-loss bosonic channel admit the following candidate second-order classical converse under a maximum-photon-number occupation constraint? Let $\mathcal N_\eta$ be the single-mode pure-loss channel with transmissivity $0<\eta<1$, defined in the Heisenberg picture by

$$
\hat b=\sqrt{\eta}\,\hat a+\sqrt{1-\eta}\,\hat e,
\tag{1}
$$

where the environment mode $E$ is in the vacuum. In an $n$-use code, the channel in Eq. (1) is used to transmit one of $M$ input states $\rho_m^{A^n}$, with a decoding POVM $\{\Lambda_m^{B^n}\}_{m=1}^M$. Write $\overline\rho_{A^n}:=M^{-1}\sum_m\rho_m^{A^n}$ and let $\Pi_{\lceil nN_S\rceil}$ project onto the $n$-mode subspace of total photon number at most $\lceil nN_S\rceil$. For fixed $N_S>0$, $\varepsilon\in(0,1)$, and $c>0$, impose

$$
\frac1M\sum_{m=1}^M
 \operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
 \Lambda_m\mathcal N_\eta^{\otimes n}(\rho_m)
 \right]
 \geq1-\varepsilon,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
 \Pi_{\lceil nN_S\rceil}\overline\rho_{A^n}
 \right]
 \geq1-\delta_n,
 \qquad
 0\leq\delta_n\leq2^{-cn}.
\tag{2}
$$

Let $M^*_{\rm occ}(n,\eta,N_S,\varepsilon,c)$ be the largest $M$ satisfying Eq. (2). Define the thermal entropy and its entropy variance by

$$
g(x):=(x+1)\log_2(x+1)-x\log_2x,
 \qquad
 v(x):=x(x+1)
 \left[\log_2(x+1)-\log_2x\right]^2,
\tag{3}
$$

where $0\log_2 0:=0$. With the functions in Eq. (3), is the following upper bound valid as $n\to\infty$?

$$
\log_2 M^*_{\rm occ}(n,\eta,N_S,\varepsilon,c)
 \leq ng(\eta N_S)
 +\sqrt{n\,v(\eta N_S)}\,\Phi^{-1}(\varepsilon)
 +O(\log n),
\tag{4}
$$

Here $\Phi^{-1}$ in Eq. (4) is the inverse standard-normal cumulative distribution function, and the implicit constant may depend on $\eta,N_S,\varepsilon,$ and $c$, but not on $n$.
