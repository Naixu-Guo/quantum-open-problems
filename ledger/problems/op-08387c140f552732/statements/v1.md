Let $\mathcal N,\mathcal M:\mathcal L(A)\to\mathcal L(B)$ be quantum channels on finite-dimensional systems. For states $\rho$ and $\sigma$, define the relative entropy and the hypothesis-testing divergence by

$$
\begin{aligned}
 D(\rho\|\sigma)
 &:={\rm Tr}\!\left[\rho(\log_2\rho-\log_2\sigma)\right],\\
 D_H^\varepsilon(\rho\|\sigma)
 &:=-\log_2\inf_{\substack{0\leq Q\leq I\\
 {\rm Tr}(Q\rho)\geq1-\varepsilon}}
 {\rm Tr}(Q\sigma),
 \end{aligned}
\tag{1}
$$

where $D(\rho\|\sigma)=+\infty$ unless $\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\operatorname{supp}\sigma$, and $\varepsilon\in(0,1)$. For either divergence $\mathbf D$ in Eq. (1), its stabilized channel extension is

$$
\mathbf D_{\rm ch}(\mathcal N\|\mathcal M)
 :=\sup_{\psi_{RA}\in\mathcal D(R\otimes A)}
 \mathbf D\!\left(
 (\operatorname{id}_R\otimes\mathcal N)(\psi)
 \middle\|
 (\operatorname{id}_R\otimes\mathcal M)(\psi)
 \right),
 \qquad R\simeq A.
\tag{2}
$$

Using Eq. (2), define the regularized channel relative entropy by

$$
D_{\rm ch}^{\infty}(\mathcal N\|\mathcal M)
 :=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n
 D_{\rm ch}(\mathcal N^{\otimes n}\|\mathcal M^{\otimes n}).
\tag{3}
$$

Whenever Eq. (3) is finite, does the fixed-error parallel Stein limit exist and satisfy

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n
 D_{H,{\rm ch}}^\varepsilon
 (\mathcal N^{\otimes n}\|\mathcal M^{\otimes n})
 =D_{\rm ch}^{\infty}(\mathcal N\|\mathcal M)
 \qquad\text{for every }\varepsilon\in(0,1)?
\tag{4}
$$
