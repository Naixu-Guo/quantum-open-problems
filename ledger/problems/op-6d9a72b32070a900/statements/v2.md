Is every $n$-qubit state of purity at most $1/(2^n-1/2)$ a convex mixture of stabilizer states?

Let $d=2^n$ and

$$
\mathrm{STAB}_n=\operatorname{conv}\{|s\rangle\langle s|:|s\rangle\text{ is an }n\text{-qubit stabilizer state}\}.
\tag{1}
$$

Is the following implication valid for every $n\geq1$?

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^2)\leq\frac1{d-1/2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\rho\in\mathrm{STAB}_n.
\tag{2}
$$

Equivalently, does the Hilbert–Schmidt ball about $I/d$ contained in Eq. (1) have radius $1/\sqrt{d(2d-1)}$? This is the threshold asserted by Eq. (2).
