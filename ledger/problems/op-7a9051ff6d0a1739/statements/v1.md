What is the entanglement cost of the Choi state of the qubit amplitude-damping channel

$$
\mathcal A_p(\rho)=A_0\rho A_0^\dagger+A_1\rho A_1^\dagger,
 \qquad 0\le p\le1?
\tag{1}
$$

The Kraus operators in Eq. (1) are

$$
\begin{aligned}
 A_0&=\lvert0\rangle\!\langle0\rvert
 +\sqrt{1-p}\,\lvert1\rangle\!\langle1\rvert
 =\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&\sqrt{1-p}\end{pmatrix},\\
 A_1&=\sqrt p\,\lvert0\rangle\!\langle1\rvert
 =\begin{pmatrix}0&\sqrt p\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{2}
$$

In Eq. (2), $p$ is the decay probability of the excited state. Let $\lvert\Phi^+\rangle_{RA}=(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The normalized Choi state of the channel in Eq. (1) is

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \omega_p^{RB}
 &:=(\operatorname{id}_R\otimes\mathcal A_p)
 (\lvert\Phi^+\rangle\!\langle\Phi^+\rvert_{RA})\\
 &=\frac12\Bigl[
 \lvert00\rangle\!\langle00\rvert
 +\sqrt{1-p}\bigl(\lvert00\rangle\!\langle11\rvert
 +\lvert11\rangle\!\langle00\rvert\bigr)
 +(1-p)\lvert11\rangle\!\langle11\rvert
 +p\lvert10\rangle\!\langle10\rvert
 \Bigr].
 \end{aligned}
\tag{3}
$$

Here the first and second entries in each ket in Eq. (3) label $R$ and $B$, respectively. Thus the question is to determine $E_C(\omega_p)$, the asymptotic number of ebits per copy required to prepare many copies of $\omega_p$ by local operations and classical communication.
