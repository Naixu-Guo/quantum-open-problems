Does Graph Collision on every known $n$-vertex graph admit a bounded-error quantum algorithm using $\widetilde O(\sqrt n)$ queries to the marked-vertex bits? Let $G=([n],E)$ be a simple undirected graph given in full, and let $x\in\{0,1\}^n$ be accessible through the quantum oracle $O_x|v,b\rangle=|v,b\oplus x_v\rangle$. The task is to compute

$$
f_G(x)=\bigvee_{\{u,v\}\in E}(x_u\wedge x_v).
\tag{1}
$$

Only calls to $O_x$ are counted. All computation involving the known graph is free, and the algorithm must return the value in (1) with probability at least $2/3$ for every $x$.

Let $Q_2(f_G)$ be the minimum worst-case query count under this guarantee, and let $Q_{\mathrm{GC}}(n)=\max_{G:\,|V(G)|=n}Q_2(f_G)$. The question is whether

$$
Q_{\mathrm{GC}}(n)=\widetilde O(\sqrt n).
\tag{2}
$$

In (2), $\widetilde O$ hides only factors polynomial in $\log n$ as $n\to\infty$.
