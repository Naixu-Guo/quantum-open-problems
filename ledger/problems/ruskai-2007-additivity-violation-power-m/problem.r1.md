No channel is known to be additive for every tensor power below a chosen m at least three, then first become nonadditive at m copies.

A delayed violation would show that testing one or two copies cannot certify an additive channel law. It would expose a new level of regularization in channel-output entropy.

## Interpretation

The intended problem must require m at least three. Otherwise the one-copy equality is tautological and any self-channel two-copy violation answers it.
