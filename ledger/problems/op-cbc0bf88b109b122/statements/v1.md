Does the ordinary Petz recovery map give a universal fidelity remainder for monotonicity of quantum relative entropy? Let $\mathcal N:\mathcal L(A)\to\mathcal L(B)$ be a finite-dimensional quantum channel, and let $\rho,\sigma\in\mathcal D(A)$ satisfy $\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\operatorname{supp}\sigma$. With inverses taken on the relevant supports, define the Petz map by

$$
\mathcal P_{\sigma,\mathcal N}(X)
 :=\sigma^{1/2}\mathcal N^{\dagger}\!\left(
 \mathcal N(\sigma)^{-1/2}X
 \mathcal N(\sigma)^{-1/2}
 \right)\sigma^{1/2},
\tag{1}
$$

where $\mathcal N^{\dagger}$ is the Hilbert–Schmidt adjoint. Write $D(\tau\Vert\omega):=\operatorname{Tr}[\tau(\log_2\tau-
\log_2\omega)]$ and use squared fidelity $F(\tau,\omega):=\lVert\sqrt\tau\sqrt\omega\rVert_1^2$. The proposed remainder bound for the map in Eq. (1) is

$$
D(\rho\Vert\sigma)
 -D\!\left(\mathcal N(\rho)\middle\Vert\mathcal N(\sigma)\right)
 \stackrel{?}{\geq}
 -\log_2 F\!\left(
 \rho,
 \mathcal P_{\sigma,\mathcal N}(\mathcal N(\rho))
 \right).
\tag{2}
$$

Determine whether Eq. (2) holds for every such triple $(\rho,\sigma,\mathcal N)$.
