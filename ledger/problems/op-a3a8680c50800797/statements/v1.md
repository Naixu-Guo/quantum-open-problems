For every local dimension $d\geq3$, determine the entanglement of formation of an arbitrary Weyl–Bell-diagonal state. Let $\omega_d:=\exp(2\pi i/d)$ and define the generalized Pauli operators and their associated Bell basis by

$$
X\lvert j\rangle:=\lvert j+1\!\!\pmod d\rangle,
 \qquad
 Z\lvert j\rangle:=\omega_d^j\lvert j\rangle,
 \qquad
 \lvert\Phi_{a,b}\rangle
 :=(I\otimes X^aZ^b)\lvert\Phi_d\rangle,
 \qquad
 \lvert\Phi_d\rangle:=\frac1{\sqrt d}\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\lvert j,j\rangle,
\tag{1}
$$

where $a,b\in\mathbb Z_d$. In this problem, “Pauli-diagonal” means diagonal in the generalized Bell basis in Eq. (1). Thus the state is

$$
\rho_{\mathbf p}
 :=\sum_{a,b\in\mathbb Z_d}p_{a,b}
 \lvert\Phi_{a,b}\rangle\!\langle\Phi_{a,b}\rvert,
 \qquad
 p_{a,b}\geq0,
 \qquad
 \sum_{a,b\in\mathbb Z_d}p_{a,b}=1.
\tag{2}
$$

For every probability array $\mathbf p$ in Eq. (2), determine an evaluable exact formula and an optimal pure-state ensemble for

$$
E_F(\rho_{\mathbf p})
 :=\inf_{\rho_{\mathbf p}=\sum_i q_i
 \lvert\psi_i\rangle\!\langle\psi_i\rvert}
 \sum_i q_i\,
 S\!\left(\operatorname{Tr}_B
 \lvert\psi_i\rangle\!\langle\psi_i\rvert\right),
 \qquad
 S(\sigma):=-\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\log_2\sigma).
\tag{3}
$$

The infimum in Eq. (3) is over finite pure-state ensembles with $q_i\geq0$ and $\sum_iq_i=1$.
