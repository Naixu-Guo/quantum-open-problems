What is the constructive quantum code for achieving the quantum capacity $\mathcal{Q}(\mathcal A_p)$ of the qubit amplitude-damping channel

$$
\mathcal A_p(\rho)=A_0\rho A_0^\dagger+A_1\rho A_1^\dagger,
 \qquad 0\le p\le1?
\tag{1}
$$

The operators appearing in Eq. (1) are

$$
\begin{aligned}
 A_0&=\lvert0\rangle\!\langle0\rvert
 +\sqrt{1-p}\,\lvert1\rangle\!\langle1\rvert
 =\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&\sqrt{1-p}\end{pmatrix},\\
 A_1&=\sqrt p\,\lvert0\rangle\!\langle1\rvert
 =\begin{pmatrix}0&\sqrt p\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{2}
$$

Equation (2) uses $p$ as the decay probability of the excited state.
