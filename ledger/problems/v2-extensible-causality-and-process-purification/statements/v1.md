## Formal statement

Is every finite-dimensional extensibly causal process matrix purifiable?
Call an $N$-laboratory process matrix $W$ extensibly causal if
$W\otimes\rho_R$ generates only causal correlations for every ancillary
input system $R$, every joint ancillary state $\rho_R$, and every choice of
local instruments.  Call $W$ purifiable if there exist auxiliary global past
and future systems, a fixed pure state on the auxiliary past, and a pure
process $S$ such that inserting the fixed state into $S$ and discarding the
auxiliary future yields $W$.  If $\mathrm{EC}_N$ and $\mathrm{Pur}_N$ denote
the corresponding classes, the question is whether
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{EC}_N\subseteq\mathrm{Pur}_N
  \label{eq:p35-ec-pur-inclusion}
\end{equation}
holds for every $N$ and every choice of finite local dimensions.  In
particular, does the inclusion in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p35-ec-pur-inclusion} hold for
two laboratories?
