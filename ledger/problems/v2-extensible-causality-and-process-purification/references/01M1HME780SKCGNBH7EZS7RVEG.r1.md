For two laboratories, every purifiable process is extensibly causal,
  so $\mathrm{Pur}_2\subseteq\mathrm{EC}_2$
  [YQS+21].  This is the converse of the open
  implication in Eq. \eqref{eq:p35-ec-pur-inclusion}; it neither proves the
  inclusion nor establishes equality.
