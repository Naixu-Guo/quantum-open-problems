Determine complete facet descriptions for local-behavior polytopes beyond the presently solved Bell scenarios, either for a specified unresolved finite scenario or for a nontrivial infinite family with additional structure. For positive integers $N$, $M$, and $K$, let $\mathbf{x}\in\{1,\ldots,M\}^{N}$ denote the measurement settings and $\mathbf{a}\in\{1,\ldots,K\}^{N}$ the outcomes. The relevant local polytope is

$$
\mathcal{L}_{N,M,K}
 :=\operatorname{conv}\!\left\{
 p_{\mathbf f}:p_{\mathbf f}(\mathbf a\mid\mathbf x)
 =\prod_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf{1}\!\left\{a_i=f_i(x_i)\right\},\quad
 f_i:\{1,\ldots,M\}\to\{1,\ldots,K\}
 \right\}.
\tag{1}
$$

Here $\mathbf 1\{\cdot\}$ is the indicator function. The task is to characterize all facet-defining inequalities of the polytope in Eq. (1) in a chosen unresolved regime, modulo permutations of parties, settings, and outcomes and modulo liftings obtained by adjoining redundant settings or outcomes. A solution must carry a proof of completeness, not merely generate a large collection of facets.
