## Source

Appleby states the all-dimensional existence of a Zauner-symmetric Weyl–Heisenberg SIC fiducial as Conjecture B [App05](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1896384).

## Progress

Appleby stated Eq. (3) as Conjecture B and verified it for the numerical fiducials then known, through $d=45$ [App05](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1896384).

Larger exact and numerical data sets continue to exhibit the Zauner symmetry, including Weyl–Heisenberg SIC solutions in every dimension through $d=181$. Finite computations do not prove the universal quantifier in Eq. (3) [ABFG19](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/ab25ad).

In $2025$, Appleby, Flammia, and Kopp gave a construction of Zauner-symmetric SICs for every $d>3$, conditional on the order-one abelian Stark conjecture for real quadratic fields and a special-value identity for the Shintani–Faddeev modular cocycle [AFK25](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.03970).

In September $2026$, Radchenko and Wheeler proved algebraicity of real-quadratic Stark–Shintani invariants and the rank-one twisted-convolution identity (Theorems 1 and 7) [RW26](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.21892). Appleby, Flammia, and Kopp extended the identity to all admissible tuples and constructed Weyl–Heisenberg-covariant ghost SICs for every $d>3$ (Theorems 1.2–1.3) [AFK26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39192v1). This supplies the special-value identity in the earlier conditional construction; converting the ghost SICs to Hermitian SIC projectors requires the appropriate Galois conjugation.

The manuscript dated 4 October $2026$, prepared and edited by Danylo Radchenko and Campbell Wheeler, reports Stark reciprocity and unitary Galois conjugates (Theorem 3 and Proposition 7). Section 9.2 claims the existence part of Zauner’s conjecture, providing proposed arithmetic input for the Zauner-symmetric construction. [SU26](https://www.danrad.net/papers/stark-reciprocity.pdf). The manuscript has not yet passed peer review. This is recorded as significant reported progress; the catalog status remains Unsolved.

## Comment

This record requires both the SIC overlap equations and the specified Zauner Clifford symmetry in Eq. (3) for every $d\ge2$. The catalog retains Unsolved while documenting the reported arithmetic progress; a status change requires a separate maintainer decision. The symmetry requirement is stronger than [Weyl–Heisenberg-covariant SIC existence in every dimension](https://qiqc-op.com/problem/op_6ba929179cc40c0a/).
