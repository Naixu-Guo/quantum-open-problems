Does the semidefinite hierarchy for exact PPT entanglement cost collapse at its second level for every finite-dimensional bipartite density operator $\rho_{AB}$? Let $\Gamma=\operatorname{id}_A\otimes T_B$ denote partial transpose. For an integer $p\geq0$, define

$$
E_{\chi,p}(\rho):=\log_2\min_{S_0,\ldots,S_p}\left\{\operatorname{Tr}S_p:\ -S_i\leq S_{i-1}^{\Gamma}\leq S_i\ (0\leq i\leq p),\ S_{-1}=\rho\right\},
\tag{1}
$$

where the variables in Eq. (1) are Hermitian operators on $A\otimes B$ and the inequalities are in the positive-semidefinite order. Prove or disprove

$$
E_{\chi,3}(\rho)=E_{\chi,2}(\rho)\qquad\text{for every }\rho_{AB}.
\tag{2}
$$

A counterexample to Eq. (2) must establish a strict gap.
