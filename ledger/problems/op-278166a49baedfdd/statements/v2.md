Does the quantum Mrs. Gerber lower bound hold for two independent uniform bits with arbitrary quantum side information?

Let

$$
\rho_{X_iB_i}=\frac12\sum_{x=0}^1|x\rangle\langle x|\otimes\rho_x^{(i)},
\qquad
\rho_{X_1B_1X_2B_2}=\rho_{X_1B_1}\otimes\rho_{X_2B_2},
\tag{1}
$$

where $X_1,X_2$ are uniform bits. Put $Z=X_1\oplus X_2$ and $s_i=H(X_i\mid B_i)$, using base-two von Neumann entropy. Define

$$
h_2(p)=-p\log_2p-(1-p)\log_2(1-p),\quad 0\log_2 0=0,
\qquad
F(s,t)=h_2\!\left(h_2^{-1}(s)*h_2^{-1}(t)\right),
\tag{2}
$$

where $p*q=p(1-q)+(1-p)q$ and $h_2^{-1}$ takes values in $[0,1/2]$. Is

$$
H(Z\mid B_1B_2)\ge
\begin{cases}
F(s_1,s_2), & s_1+s_2\leq1,\\
s_1+s_2-1+F(1-s_1,1-s_2), & s_1+s_2\geq1
\end{cases}
\tag{3}
$$

for every state in Eq. (1)? Equation (2) fixes the function used in the bound (3).
