Does the proposed quantum Mrs. Gerber lower information-combining bound hold for two independent uniform bits with arbitrary quantum side information?

Take independent uniform bits $X_1,X_2$. Each bit is encoded into a quantum system:

$$
\rho_{X_iB_i}=\frac12\sum_{x=0}^1|x\rangle\langle x|\otimes\rho_x^{(i)},
\qquad
\rho_{X_1B_1X_2B_2}=\rho_{X_1B_1}\otimes\rho_{X_2B_2}.
\tag{1}
$$

Put $Z=X_1\oplus X_2$ and $s_i=H(X_i\mid B_i)\in[0,1]$, using base-two von Neumann conditional entropy. Define

$$
h_2(p)=-p\log_2p-(1-p)\log_2(1-p),\qquad 0\log_2 0:=0,\qquad
p*q=p(1-q)+(1-p)q,
\tag{2}
$$

$$
F(s,t)=h_2\!\left(h_2^{-1}(s)*h_2^{-1}(t)\right),
\tag{3}
$$

with $h_2^{-1}$ taking values in $[0,1/2]$.

For classical side information $Y_1,Y_2$, the sharp information-combining bounds are

$$
F(s_1,s_2)\le H(Z\mid Y_1Y_2)
\le s_1+s_2-s_1s_2.
\tag{4}
$$

The lower bound is the Mrs. Gerber information-combining bound; the upper bound is attained by binary erasure channels. The classical bounds are reviewed in Section II of [Hirche20](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14408); the quantum setup is studied in [Hirche18](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.09752v2), [Hirche23](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02589).

Conjecture:

$$
H(Z\mid B_1B_2)\ge
\begin{cases}
F(s_1,s_2), & s_1+s_2\le1,\\[2mm]
s_1+s_2-1+F(1-s_1,1-s_2), & s_1+s_2\ge1.
\end{cases}
\tag{5}
$$

This is Conjecture VII.1 of Hirche and Reeb. The two branches agree on their boundary. The unmodified classical lower bound is not the proposed universal quantum theorem: pure-state output channels necessitate the reflected branch. [Hirche18](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.09752v2)

The displayed definitions, constraints, and target bounds are recorded in Eqs. (1), (2), (3), (4), (5).
