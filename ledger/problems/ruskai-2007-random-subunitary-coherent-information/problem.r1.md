No complete degradability, coherent-information or capacity analysis is known for the intended normalized cyclic sub-unitary family.

The cyclic low-rank Kraus structure offers a controlled testbed for degradability and superadditive coherent information. A solution would give exact capacities for a nontrivial noisy-channel family.

## Interpretation

The printed Kraus formula is not trace preserving. The research question is interpreted for the intended normalized cyclic family with varying input supports.
