Renes closed the remaining asymmetric-input and explicit-decoder
  gaps.  The construction uses asymmetric classical polar coding for the
  amplitude $Z$-channel with the capacity-achieving biased input, together
  with a coherent quantum-message-passing decoder for the phase channel.
  It achieves the optimized rate in Eq. \eqref{eq:p2-capacity} without
  preshared entanglement and gives an explicit decoding circuit with
  $O(N^2)$ gates for block length $N$
  [Ren17].
