Is the exact Haar-mixing spectral gap of uniformly random Pauli rotations equal to $2^n(2^n-3)/(8(4^n-1))$ for every $n\geq4$?

Let $\mathcal P_n=\{I,X,Y,Z\}^{\otimes n}\setminus\{I^{\otimes n}\}$. One step chooses $P\in\mathcal P_n$ and $\theta\in[0,2\pi)$ uniformly and applies $e^{i\theta P}$. On $L^2(\mathrm{SU}(2^n),\mathrm{Haar})$, define

$$
(K_nf)(U)=\mathbb E_{P,\theta}f(e^{i\theta P}U),
\qquad
\Delta_n=1-\|K_n|_{L^2_0}\|_{2\to2},
\tag{1}
$$

where $L^2_0$ is the zero-Haar-mean subspace. The conjectured value of the gap in Eq. (1) is

$$
\Delta_n=\frac{2^n(2^n-3)}{8(4^n-1)}
\tag{2}
$$

Does Eq. (2) hold for every $n\geq4$?
