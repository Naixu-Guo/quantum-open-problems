Do there exist triples of qubit CSS LDPC codes with linear combined rate and linear distance on which the transversal CCZ gate induces a non-Clifford logical operation? Precisely, do there exist constants $r,\delta>0$ and $w\in\mathbb N$, and triples, with $n\to\infty$, of qubit CSS codes $\mathcal Q_{a,n}$ with parameters $[[n,k_{a,n},d_{a,n}]]$, indexed by $a\in\{1,2,3\}$, such that each code has $k_{a,n}\ge1$ and $X$- and $Z$-check matrices whose row weights (check weights) and column weights (qubit degrees) are at most $w$,

$$
\sum_{a=1}^{3}k_{a,n}\ge rn,\qquad
 \min_{a\in\{1,2,3\}}d_{a,n}\ge\delta n,
\tag{1}
$$

and the transversal gate

$$
U_n:=\prod_{j=1}^{n}\operatorname{CCZ}_{(1,j),(2,j),(3,j)},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{CCZ}|x,y,z\rangle=(-1)^{xyz}|x,y,z\rangle
 \quad\text{for }x,y,z\in\{0,1\},
\tag{2}
$$

preserves $\mathcal Q_{1,n}\otimes\mathcal Q_{2,n}\otimes\mathcal Q_{3,n}$ and induces a logical unitary that does not normalize its encoded Pauli group? Here $(a,j)$ denotes physical qubit $j$ of block $a$, so Eq. (2) applies one CCZ gate to each aligned triple of physical qubits.
