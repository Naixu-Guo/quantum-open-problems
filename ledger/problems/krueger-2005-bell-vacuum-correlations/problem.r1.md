Vacuum entanglement persists at arbitrary separation, but direct CHSH violation by bounded distant regions has not been proved or excluded.

The problem separates long-range vacuum entanglement from Bell nonlocality that localized observers can test. It probes the operational content of correlations in algebraic quantum field theory.

## Interpretation

Entanglement, failure of a PPT analogue and postselected hidden nonlocality are weaker than the direct bounded-region CHSH statement in the archived problem.
