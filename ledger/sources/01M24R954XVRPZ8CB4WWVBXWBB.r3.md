László Babai, “Canonical Form for Graphs in Quasipolynomial Time: Preliminary Report,” in *Proceedings of the 51st Annual ACM SIGACT Symposium on Theory of Computing (STOC 2019)*, 1237–1246 (2019). [doi:10.1145/3313276.3316356](https://doi.org/10.1145/3313276.3316356). This STOC 2019 paper extends the earlier Graph Isomorphism test to canonical-form construction; it is not a corrected republication of the STOC 2016 result. Babai’s January 2017 update separately documents the correction to the running-time analysis of the original arXiv:1512.03547 preprint.
