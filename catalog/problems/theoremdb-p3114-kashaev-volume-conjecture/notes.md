# Kashaev volume conjecture for hyperbolic knots

## Background

Kashaev defined a sequence of knot invariants from the quantum dilogarithm. For a hyperbolic knot, he conjectured that their exponential growth recovers the hyperbolic volume of the knot complement. Murakami and Murakami identified Kashaev's invariant with a root-of-unity specialization of the colored Jones polynomial, which connects the conjecture to quantum topology.

## Status and known progress

**Status: open.**

- Kashaev formulated the volume asymptotic in 1997. Murakami and Murakami recast it using colored Jones polynomials.
- Researchers have proved the conjecture for selected knots and knot families. These theorems do not cover every hyperbolic knot.
- TheoremDB checked the universal statement on 1 August 2026 and found no proof or counterexample. Searches through 31 August found later computations and refinements, but no universal theorem.

**Last verified:** 2026-08-31.
