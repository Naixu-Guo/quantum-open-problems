## Notation

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $n$ | Number of qubits / vertices of the graph |
| $G$ | A simple undirected graph on $n$ vertices |
| $\vert\psi_G\rangle$ | The graph state associated with $G$, a state of $n$ qubits |
| $q_i\in\{0,1\}$ | The computational-basis label at vertex $i$ |
| $X^{(i)},Z^{(i)}$ | Pauli $X$, $Z$ operators acting on the qubit at vertex $i$ |
| $K_i$ | Stabiliser generator $K_i = X^{(i)}\prod_{j:\,ij\in E(G)} Z^{(j)}$ for vertex $i$ |
| Stabiliser group | Abelian Pauli subgroup leaving $\vert\psi_G\rangle$ invariant, generated by $\{K_i\}_{i=1}^n$ |
| Pauli group | Group generated by tensor products of Pauli operators (with phases $\pm 1, \pm i$) |
| Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_n$ | Group of unitaries $U\in U(2^n)$ for which $UPU^*$ is a Pauli operator for every Pauli $P$ |
| Single-qubit Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_1$ | The Clifford group on one qubit, generated by the Hadamard matrix $H=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$, the phase gate $S=\mathrm{diag}(1,i)$, and the Pauli matrices |
| LU equivalence | Two states $\vert\psi\rangle,\vert\phi\rangle$ on $n$ qubits are LU-equivalent if there exist single-qubit unitaries $U_1,\dots,U_n$ with $\vert\phi\rangle = U_1\otimes\cdots\otimes U_n\,\vert\psi\rangle$ |
| LC equivalence | Same as LU but with each $U_i$ required to lie in the single-qubit Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_1$ |
| Local complementation | Graph operation: at a chosen vertex $v$, complement the edges within $N(v)$; LC equivalence of graph states corresponds (Van den Nest et al.) to a sequence of local complementations |

## Formal statement

Decide the following decision problem.

**Input:** Two simple graphs $G$ and $G'$ on the same vertex set of size $n$, presenting two graph states $|\psi_G\rangle$ and $|\psi_{G'}\rangle$ on $n$ qubits.

**Question (LU $\Rightarrow$ LC ?):** Suppose there exist single-qubit unitaries $U_1,\dots,U_n\in U(2)$ such that
$$|\psi_{G'}\rangle = U_1\otimes U_2\otimes\cdots\otimes U_n\,|\psi_G\rangle .$$
Does it then follow that there also exist *Clifford* single-qubit unitaries $V_1,\dots,V_n\in\mathcal{C}_1$ such that
$$|\psi_{G'}\rangle = V_1\otimes V_2\otimes\cdots\otimes V_n\,|\psi_G\rangle\,?$$

Equivalently, are LU-equivalent graph states necessarily LC-equivalent?
