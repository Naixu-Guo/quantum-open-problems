# Quantum PCP conjecture

## Background

The $k$-local Hamiltonian problem is the quantum analogue of a classical constraint-satisfaction problem. An inverse-polynomial promise gap gives a QMA-complete problem. The quantum PCP conjecture asks whether the problem remains QMA-hard when the promise gap is a fixed fraction of the number of local terms.

The site classifies this problem under quantum computation because the unresolved statement concerns QMA-hardness. The Hamiltonian supplies the physical model but does not move the older quantum-information catalog into a many-body field.

## Status and known progress

**Status: open.**

- Aharonov, Arad, and Vidick give the standard constant-gap formulation and review the relation between quantum PCPs and local Hamiltonians.
- Anshu, Breuckmann, and Nirkhe construct NLTS Hamiltonians from good quantum LDPC codes. NLTS establishes the required low-energy entanglement phenomenon but does not prove QMA-hardness at constant gap.
- Buhrman, Helsen, and Weggemans prove reductions and restrictions for several quantum-PCP formulations. Their 2025 paper describes constant-gap Local Hamiltonian hardness as open and shows that a proof needs non-relativizing ingredients.

No primary paper located by 31 August 2026 proves or refutes the constant-gap Local Hamiltonian statement.

**Last verified:** 2026-08-31.
