# Local Equivalence of Graph States

> **Audit status (2026-08-12): SOLVED**

## Notation

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $n$ | Number of qubits / vertices of the graph |
| $G$ | A simple undirected graph on $n$ vertices |
| $\vert\psi_G\rangle$ | The graph state associated with $G$, a state of $n$ qubits |
| $q_i\in\{0,1\}$ | The computational-basis label at vertex $i$ |
| $X^{(i)},Z^{(i)}$ | Pauli $X$, $Z$ operators acting on the qubit at vertex $i$ |
| $K_i$ | Stabiliser generator $K_i = X^{(i)}\prod_{j:\,ij\in E(G)} Z^{(j)}$ for vertex $i$ |
| Stabiliser group | Abelian Pauli subgroup leaving $\vert\psi_G\rangle$ invariant, generated by $\{K_i\}_{i=1}^n$ |
| Pauli group | Group generated by tensor products of Pauli operators (with phases $\pm 1, \pm i$) |
| Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_n$ | Group of unitaries $U\in U(2^n)$ for which $UPU^*$ is a Pauli operator for every Pauli $P$ |
| Single-qubit Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_1$ | The Clifford group on one qubit, generated by the Hadamard matrix $H=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$, the phase gate $S=\mathrm{diag}(1,i)$, and the Pauli matrices |
| LU equivalence | Two states $\vert\psi\rangle,\vert\phi\rangle$ on $n$ qubits are LU-equivalent if there exist single-qubit unitaries $U_1,\dots,U_n$ with $\vert\phi\rangle = U_1\otimes\cdots\otimes U_n\,\vert\psi\rangle$ |
| LC equivalence | Same as LU but with each $U_i$ required to lie in the single-qubit Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_1$ |
| Local complementation | Graph operation: at a chosen vertex $v$, complement the edges within $N(v)$; LC equivalence of graph states corresponds (Van den Nest et al.) to a sequence of local complementations |

## Background

A graph state $|\psi_G\rangle$ is a multi-qubit pure state attached to a simple undirected graph $G$ with $n$ vertices; each vertex carries a qubit and each edge encodes a controlled-phase entangling interaction. Equivalently, in the computational basis,
$$\langle q_1,q_2,\dots,q_n|\psi_G\rangle = 2^{-n/2}\,\prod_{(i,j)\in E(G)}(-1)^{q_i q_j} ,$$
and $|\psi_G\rangle$ can be characterised as the unique joint $+1$ eigenstate of the $n$ stabiliser generators
$$K_i = X^{(i)}\!\!\prod_{j:\,(i,j)\in E(G)}\!\!Z^{(j)},\qquad i=1,\dots,n .$$
Graph states are central in measurement-based quantum computation and quantum error correction; the Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_n$ is the natural symmetry group of stabiliser states.

Two natural equivalence relations on $n$-qubit states are *local unitary* (LU) equivalence — both states are connected by a product of arbitrary single-qubit unitaries — and *local Clifford* (LC) equivalence — the same with each single-qubit unitary restricted to the single-qubit Clifford group $\mathcal{C}_1$. LC is the equivalence preserving the stabiliser-state structure, and admits a clean combinatorial description (Van den Nest, Dehaene, De Moor 2004): two graph states are LC-equivalent iff their underlying graphs are related by a sequence of *local complementations*. Decision and enumeration of LC equivalence are therefore well understood and computable.

LU equivalence is a finer notion. Trivially LC equivalence implies LU equivalence; whether the converse holds for graph states — equivalently, whether *every* LU-equivalence among graph states can already be realised by LC operations — is the LU=LC conjecture. A positive answer would mean that the easy-to-handle combinatorial classification of stabiliser states by local complementations is the *complete* classification of graph states by single-qubit unitaries; a negative answer would force a richer, non-combinatorial theory of LU classes. This is the open problem under consideration.

## Formal statement

Decide the following decision problem.

**Input:** Two simple graphs $G$ and $G'$ on the same vertex set of size $n$, presenting two graph states $|\psi_G\rangle$ and $|\psi_{G'}\rangle$ on $n$ qubits.

**Question (LU $\Rightarrow$ LC ?):** Suppose there exist single-qubit unitaries $U_1,\dots,U_n\in U(2)$ such that
$$|\psi_{G'}\rangle = U_1\otimes U_2\otimes\cdots\otimes U_n\,|\psi_G\rangle .$$
Does it then follow that there also exist *Clifford* single-qubit unitaries $V_1,\dots,V_n\in\mathcal{C}_1$ such that
$$|\psi_{G'}\rangle = V_1\otimes V_2\otimes\cdots\otimes V_n\,|\psi_G\rangle\,?$$

Equivalently, are LU-equivalent graph states necessarily LC-equivalent?

## Status and known progress

The problem is **solved (negatively)**: the LU = LC conjecture is **false** in general.

- For qubit graph states on $n\le 7$ vertices, numerical work of Hein, Eisert and Briegel (and Hein et al.) verified that LC equivalence coincides with LU equivalence on all connected graphs with up to 7 vertices.
- Van den Nest, Dehaene and De Moor (arXiv:quant-ph/0411115, 2004) proved LU $\Rightarrow$ LC for a substantial class of graph states, namely those whose stabiliser group has a particular structure; this class includes all stabiliser states derived from $GL(4)$-linear codes, and (in a separate paper) also generalised GHZ / star-graph states.
- The general LU=LC conjecture was **disproved** by Z. Ji, J. Chen, Z. Wei and M. Ying, *The LU-LC conjecture is false*, Quantum Information & Computation **10**, 97 (2010); arXiv:0709.1266 (2007/2008). They exhibited explicit counterexamples on sufficiently large numbers of qubits (the smallest counterexamples produced in their construction have 27 qubits) — pairs of graph states that are LU-equivalent but not LC-equivalent.
- A March 2026 preprint by Claudet, arXiv:2603.25219v1, proves that the 27-qubit counterexample is minimal, so LU and LC equivalence coincide through 26 qubits. This refinement had not passed peer review by the verification date. Cabello et al., arXiv:1010.5638, classify small graph states using compact invariants; that paper is not a source of additional counterexamples.

Even with this disproof, several refined questions remain (e.g. small-vertex exhaustive bounds; minimum $n$ for which LU $\neq$ LC; classification of LU classes within an LC class for arbitrary $n$). The original problem as posed, however, is settled.
