# Yang-Mills existence and mass gap

> **Audit status (2026-08-31): OPEN**

## Background

Quantum Yang-Mills theory underlies the Standard Model, yet mathematics lacks a nonperturbative four-dimensional construction satisfying the required field-theory axioms. The Clay problem joins that existence question to a spectral one: the reconstructed theory must have a positive energy gap above the vacuum.

## Formal statement

For each compact simple gauge group $G$, construct a nontrivial quantum Yang-Mills theory on $\mathbb R^4$ that satisfies axioms at least as strong as those in the official Jaffe-Witten formulation and agrees with the classical Yang-Mills theory in the required sense.

Let $H$ be the Hamiltonian of the reconstructed quantum theory and let the vacuum energy be zero. Prove that there is a constant $\Delta>0$ such that
$$
\operatorname{spec}(H)\cap(0,\Delta)=\varnothing.
$$

A complete solution must establish both the quantum theory and the positive mass gap for the gauge groups and axioms in the official statement.

## Status and known progress

**Status: open.**

- The Clay Mathematics Institute lists the problem as unsolved and says that experiments and simulations do not supply the required proof.
- Constructive and lattice gauge theory prove substantial results in lower dimensions, finite volume, or regulated settings. Those results do not give the required continuum theory on $\mathbb R^4$.
- A June 2026 preprint, arXiv:2606.19362, claims a four-dimensional $SU(N)$ construction with a mass gap. The manuscript has not completed peer review or the Clay evaluation process, and Clay lists the problem as unsolved. This catalog does not treat the claim as a resolution.
