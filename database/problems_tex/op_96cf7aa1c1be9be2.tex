\section{Polynomial shared-resource lower bounds for routing}
\label{sec:op_96cf7aa1c1be9be2}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does an explicit total Boolean family require polynomial shared-state cost for
bounded-error one-round $f$-routing?  More precisely, do there exist constants
$c>0$ and $\varepsilon>0$ and a sequence
\begin{equation}
  f_n:\{0,1\}^n\times\{0,1\}^n\longrightarrow\{0,1\}
  \label{eq:p38-boolean-family}
\end{equation}
such that one uniform deterministic algorithm computes $f_n(x,y)$ from
$(n,x,y)$ in time polynomial in $n$, and every routing protocol for the map in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p38-boolean-family} with worst-case diamond-norm error at most
$\varepsilon$ has cost at least $n^c$?

In an $f$-routing protocol, Alice receives $x$ and an unknown qubit $Q$, Bob
receives $y$, and they may share an arbitrary state $\rho_{LR}$ before the
inputs arrive.  After one simultaneous message in each direction, Alice must
recover $Q$ when $f(x,y)=0$, and Bob must recover it when $f(x,y)=1$.  Message
sizes and local operations are unrestricted.  Measure only the shared state
by
\begin{equation}
  E_{\mathrm{dim}}(\rho_{LR})
  :=\log_2\min\!\left\{
    \operatorname{rank}\rho_L,\operatorname{rank}\rho_R
  \right\}.
  \label{eq:p38-dimension-cost}
\end{equation}
The target is a family in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p38-boolean-family} for which every
valid protocol satisfies $E_{\mathrm{dim}}(\rho_{LR})\geq n^c$, with the cost
defined in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p38-dimension-cost}.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Bogner explicitly asks for polynomial, or even superlogarithmic for an
explicit total family, shared-resource lower bounds for $f$-routing
\sourcecite{ref:p38-bogner}{Bog26}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Every Boolean function admits an $f$-routing protocol with worst-case
  shared-resource cost at most
  \begin{equation}
    2^{O(\sqrt{n\log n})}.
    \label{eq:p38-general-upper-bound}
  \end{equation}
  The construction underlying Eq.~\eqref{eq:p38-general-upper-bound} also
  relates routing quantitatively to conditional disclosure of secrets
  \sourcecite{ref:p38-allerstorfer}{ABM+24}.

  \item Rank methods give nontrivial lower bounds for explicit functions when
  one routing case is required to be perfectly correct, but do not establish
  a polynomial lower bound on Eq.~\eqref{eq:p38-dimension-cost} in the
  two-sided bounded-error model \sourcecite{ref:p38-asadi}{ACM24}.

  \item For the explicit inner-product function, the best robust result is
  \begin{equation}
    f_n(x,y)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n x_i y_i,
    \qquad
    d\log_2(2d)=\Omega(n),
    \qquad
    E_{\mathrm{dim}}\geq
      \log_2n-\log_2\log_2n-O(1),
    \label{eq:p38-inner-product-lower-bound}
  \end{equation}
  where $d=\min\{\operatorname{rank}\rho_L,
  \operatorname{rank}\rho_R\}$ and the worst-case error is at most $0.09$
  \sourcecite{ref:p38-bogner}{Bog26}.
  Equation~\eqref{eq:p38-inner-product-lower-bound} is logarithmic rather than
  polynomial in the cost measure of Eq.~\eqref{eq:p38-dimension-cost}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

Bogner explicitly leaves a polynomial lower bound on
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p38-dimension-cost} open and notes that even a superlogarithmic
bound for an explicit total family is unknown.  The quantity
$E_{\mathrm{dim}}$ is a rank-based shared-resource cost for arbitrary mixed
states, not an entanglement monotone.
