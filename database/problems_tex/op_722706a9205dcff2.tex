\section{Loss of mixed unitarity after a positive time in a quantum dynamical semigroup}
\label{sec:op_722706a9205dcff2}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does there exist a norm-continuous semigroup $(\Phi_t)_{t\geq0}$ of
unital completely positive trace-preserving maps on $\mathcal L(\mathbb C^d)$,
for some finite integer $d\geq3$, such that $\Phi_s$ is mixed unitary and
$\Phi_t$ is not mixed unitary for some $0<s<t$?
Here $\Phi_0=\operatorname{id}$ and $\Phi_{u+v}=\Phi_u\circ\Phi_v$ for
$u,v\geq0$. A map $\Psi$ is mixed unitary if it admits
\begin{equation}
  \Psi(X)=\sum_{j=1}^{N}p_j U_j XU_j^\dagger,
  \qquad p_j\geq0,\qquad\sum_{j=1}^{N}p_j=1,
  \label{eq:72270-mixed-unitary}
\end{equation}
for every $X\in\mathcal L(\mathbb C^d)$, where $N$ is finite and the
$U_j$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:72270-mixed-unitary} are unitary.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Bhat and Devendra explicitly ask whether the first positive mixed-unitary
time can precede the eventual threshold; see the unnumbered remark after
Theorem~5.4, p.~21 of version~2 \sourcecite{ref:72270-bhat}{BD26}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Every such semigroup is mixed unitary at all sufficiently large times
(Theorem~4.12). Unless it is mixed unitary at every time, Theorem~5.4 gives
a first positive mixed-unitary time $t_0$ and the least eventual threshold
$t_1\geq t_0$. The authors leave $t_0<t_1$ open
\sourcecite{ref:72270-bhat}{BD26}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The question is equivalent to $t_0<t_1$; eventual mixed unitarity alone
does not settle it. Literature audit: 9 September 2026. The latest source
version remains a preprint explicitly leaving this gap open. No later
resolution was located in indexed literature; unindexed work cannot be excluded.
