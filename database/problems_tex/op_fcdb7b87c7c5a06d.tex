\section{Distillable tripartite W states from tripartite GHZ states by LOCC}
\label{sec:op_fcdb7b87c7c5a06d}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Alice, Bob, and Charlie share $n$ copies of the standard tripartite GHZ state. Let $\mathrm{GHZ}_3$ be the projector onto $(\ket{000}+\ket{111})/\sqrt{2}$.

For a proposed rate $r$, the parties aim to distill $\lfloor rn\rfloor$ copies of the tripartite W state with vanishing trace-distance error on the full target. Let $\mathrm{W}_3$ be the projector onto $(\ket{100}+\ket{010}+\ket{001})/\sqrt{3}$.

What is the optimal LOCC distillation rate $R_{\mathrm{GHZ}\to\mathrm{W}}$? The rate is defined by
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{GHZ}\to\mathrm{W}}=\sup\left\{r\geq 0:\lim_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\Lambda_n\in\mathrm{LOCC}}
  \tfrac12\left\lVert\Lambda_n\bigl(\mathrm{GHZ}_3^{\otimes n}\bigr)
  -\mathrm{W}_3^{\otimes\lfloor rn\rfloor}\right\rVert_1=0\right\}.
  \label{eq:ghz-w-rate}
\end{equation}
In Eq.~\eqref{eq:ghz-w-rate}, the infimum is over LOCC channels with $\lfloor rn\rfloor$ output qubits per party.

Give an achievable rate and a matching converse for arbitrary LOCC, counting W states per GHZ state supplied.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Yang and Jeong give bounds on the GHZ entanglement cost of the W state in Section~IV~A. The rate $R_{\mathrm{GHZ}\to\mathrm{W}}$ is the reciprocal of this cost \sourcecite{ref:c5a06d-yj15}{YJ15}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The bounds are given in Eq.~\eqref{eq:ghz-w-bounds}.
\begin{equation}
  \frac{1}{\log_2 3}\leq R_{\mathrm{GHZ}\to\mathrm{W}}\leq\frac{1}{\log_2(9/4)}.
  \label{eq:ghz-w-bounds}
\end{equation}
For the lower bound, the preparation protocol of Yang and Jeong consumes GHZ states at a rate bounded above by the entropy of the three equally likely computational-basis terms of the W state \sourcecite{ref:c5a06d-yj15}{YJ15} (Sections III and IV A).

For the upper bound, the fully separable relative entropy of entanglement is additive on GHZ and W tensor powers grouped by party, with respective single-copy values $1$ and $\log_2(9/4)$ \sourcecite{ref:c5a06d-yj15}{YJ15} (Section IV A), \sourcecite{ref:c5a06d-zch10}{ZCH10} (Proposition 12).
\end{itemize}
