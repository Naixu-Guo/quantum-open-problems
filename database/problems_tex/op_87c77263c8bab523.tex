\section{Ordinary-Petz recovery bound for conditional mutual information}
\label{sec:op_87c77263c8bab523}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does the ordinary, unrotated Petz map universally recover a tripartite state
with fidelity controlled by its conditional mutual information?  For a
finite-dimensional state $\rho_{ABC}$, define
\begin{equation}
  I(A;B\mid C)_\rho
  :=S(AC)_\rho+S(BC)_\rho-S(C)_\rho-S(ABC)_\rho,
  \qquad
  S(\tau):=-\operatorname{Tr}(\tau\log_2\tau).
  \label{eq:p70-cmi}
\end{equation}
The ordinary Petz map associated with $\rho_{AC}$ and the channel
$\operatorname{Tr}_A:AC\to C$ is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal P^{\rho}_{C\to AC}(X_C)
  :=\rho_{AC}^{1/2}\!\left[
      I_A\otimes\rho_C^{-1/2}X_C\rho_C^{-1/2}
    \right]\rho_{AC}^{1/2},
  \label{eq:p70-petz-map}
\end{equation}
where the inverse is taken on $\operatorname{supp}\rho_C$.  With squared
fidelity $F(\tau,\omega):=\lVert\sqrt\tau\sqrt\omega\rVert_1^2$, determine
whether the quantity in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-cmi} always satisfies
\begin{equation}
  I(A;B\mid C)_\rho
  \stackrel{?}{\geq}
  -\log_2 F\!\left(
    \rho_{ABC},
    (\operatorname{id}_B\otimes\mathcal P^{\rho}_{C\to AC})(\rho_{BC})
  \right),
  \label{eq:p70-ordinary-petz-cmi}
\end{equation}
with the recovered systems ordered canonically as $ABC$.

\subsection*{Status}

Solved

\subsection*{Source}

Berta, Seshadreesan, and Wilde state the R\'enyi-monotonicity conjecture whose
$\alpha=1/2$ and $\alpha=1$ endpoints give
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-ordinary-petz-cmi}; Wilde records this ordinary-Petz
inequality explicitly in Section~12.7
\sourcecite{ref:p70-berta-seshadreesan-wilde}{BSW15},
\sourcecite{ref:p70-wilde}{Wil17}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Peter supplied two explicit three-qubit counterexamples to
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-ordinary-petz-cmi} through the project submission inbox
on 10 September 2026. For the rank-three example, in computational
$ABC$ order, set
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    v_1&=4\lvert111\rangle,\qquad
    v_2=3(\lvert011\rangle-\lvert101\rangle),\\
    v_3&=5\lvert001\rangle+\lvert010\rangle+\lvert100\rangle,\qquad
    \rho_{ABC}=\frac1{61}\sum_{j=1}^3\lvert v_j\rangle\!\langle v_j\rvert.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:p70-counterexample}
\end{equation}
The vectors in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-counterexample} are orthogonal with
squared norms $16,18,27$, so they define a rank-three density operator.
Its marginals satisfy
\begin{equation}
  \rho_{AC}=\rho_{BC}=\frac1{61}
  \begin{pmatrix}1&0&0&0\\0&34&5&0\\0&5&1&0\\0&0&0&25\end{pmatrix},
  \qquad \rho_C=\frac1{61}\operatorname{diag}(2,59).
  \label{eq:p70-counterexample-marginals}
\end{equation}
Thus the inverse in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-petz-map} is an ordinary inverse.
For $\sigma_{ABC}=(\operatorname{id}_B\otimes\mathcal P^{\rho}_{C\to AC})(\rho_{BC})$,
independent exact symbolic reconstruction and rational interval arithmetic give
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    I(A;B\mid C)_\rho&=0.48665355975796833566\ldots,\\
    -\log_2F(\rho_{ABC},\sigma_{ABC})&=0.49106481978485687802\ldots,\\
    -0.004412&<I(A;B\mid C)_\rho+\log_2F(\rho_{ABC},\sigma_{ABC})<-0.0044<0.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:p70-certified-gap}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:p70-certified-gap} disproves the universal inequality
with exactly the map and squared-fidelity convention in the statement.
A second, rank-two example has a separately certified gap between
$-0.000881$ and $-0.000880$. Both constructions, their exact fidelity
matrices, and an independently written verifier are recorded in the
\href{https://github.com/Naixu-Guo/quantum-open-problems/blob/main/database/proofs/ordinary-petz.md}{counterexample note}
\sourcecite{ref:p70-peter-counterexamples}{Pet26}.

  \item Sutter, Tomamichel, and Harrow proved a strengthened data-processing
  inequality using a pinched Petz map, equivalently a convex combination of
  rotated Petz maps.  Their result yields a conditional-mutual-information
  recovery bound of the form
  \begin{equation}
    I(A;B\mid C)_\rho
    \geq-\log_2 F\!\left(
      \rho_{ABC},
      (\operatorname{id}_B\otimes\mathcal R_{C\to AC})(\rho_{BC})
    \right),
    \label{eq:p70-rotated-recovery}
  \end{equation}
  for an explicitly averaged recovery map $\mathcal R_{C\to AC}$.
  Equation~\eqref{eq:p70-rotated-recovery} does not establish
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-ordinary-petz-cmi}, because the averaging need not reduce
  to the unrotated map in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-petz-map}
  \sourcecite{ref:p70-sutter-tomamichel-harrow}{STH16}.

  \item Junge, Renner, Sutter, Wilde, and Winter constructed a universal
  recovery map depending only on the reference state and the channel, and
  proved a fidelity remainder of the form in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-rotated-recovery}.  Their universal map is an average of
  rotated Petz maps, so universality alone does not settle the ordinary-map
  requirement in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-ordinary-petz-cmi}
  \sourcecite{ref:p70-junge-et-al}{JRS+18}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The ordinary-map requirement in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p70-petz-map} is now
settled negatively by the compatible-marginal counterexamples above.
The results using rotations, pinching, averaging, or optimized recovery
channels remain valid. The counterexamples were submitted by Peter and
verified for this catalog with exact symbolic identities and rigorous
rational intervals; no external peer review, proof-assistant kernel check,
or historical-priority claim is asserted
\sourcecite{ref:p70-peter-counterexamples}{Pet26}.
