\section{Distillable tripartite GHZ states from tripartite W states by LOCC}
\label{sec:op_89a8760b846e58b1}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Alice, Bob, and Charlie share $n$ copies of the standard tripartite W state. Let $\mathrm{W}_3$ be the projector onto $(\ket{100}+\ket{010}+\ket{001})/\sqrt{3}$.

For a proposed rate $r$, the parties aim to distill $\lfloor rn\rfloor$ copies of the tripartite GHZ state with vanishing trace-distance error on the full target. Let $\mathrm{GHZ}_3$ be the projector onto $(\ket{000}+\ket{111})/\sqrt{2}$.

What is the optimal LOCC distillation rate $R_{\mathrm{W}\to\mathrm{GHZ}}$? The rate is defined by
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{W}\to\mathrm{GHZ}}=\sup\left\{r\geq 0:\lim_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\Lambda_n\in\mathrm{LOCC}}
  \tfrac12\left\lVert\Lambda_n\bigl(\mathrm{W}_3^{\otimes n}\bigr)
  -\mathrm{GHZ}_3^{\otimes\lfloor rn\rfloor}\right\rVert_1=0\right\}.
  \label{eq:w-ghz-rate}
\end{equation}
In Eq.~\eqref{eq:w-ghz-rate}, the infimum is over LOCC channels with $\lfloor rn\rfloor$ output qubits per party.

Give an achievable rate and a matching converse for arbitrary LOCC, counting GHZ states per W state supplied.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Vrana and Christandl identify the unknown LOCC distillation rate in Example~4.8 \sourcecite{ref:6e58b1-vc19}{VC19}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Let $h_2$ denote the binary entropy in bits. The bounds are given in Eq.~\eqref{eq:w-ghz-bounds}.
\begin{equation}
  h_2(1/3)-\tfrac12 h_2\bigl((3-\sqrt{5})/6\bigr)
  \ \leq\ R_{\mathrm{W}\to\mathrm{GHZ}}\ \leq\ h_2(1/3).
  \label{eq:w-ghz-bounds}
\end{equation}
For the lower bound, Smolin, Verstraete, and Winter obtain both GHZ states
and Bell pairs, then combine the Bell pairs to obtain more GHZ states \sourcecite{ref:6e58b1-svw05}{SVW05} (Example~11). The resulting rate is about $0.64327$.

For the upper bound, group Bob and Charlie into one laboratory. A W state has
entanglement entropy $h_2(1/3)$ across this cut and a GHZ state has one ebit. The
pure-state bipartite distillation converse therefore gives the upper bound \sourcecite{ref:6e58b1-bbps96}{BBPS96}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The stochastic conversion rate is $h_2(1/3)$ in the model allowing postselection
with arbitrarily small nonzero success probability \sourcecite{ref:6e58b1-vc15}{VC15} (Theorem~11).
Equation~\eqref{eq:w-ghz-rate} uses the full output of a trace-preserving LOCC channel.
