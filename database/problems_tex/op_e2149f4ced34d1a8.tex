\section{Relative entropy of entanglement for two qubits}
\label{sec:op_e2149f4ced34d1a8}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Find a closed formula for the relative entropy of entanglement of every
two-qubit density operator $\rho$, including an explicit closest separable
state.  With $\operatorname{Sep}(\mathbb{C}^2:\mathbb{C}^2)$ denoting the
two-qubit separable states, the quantity is
\begin{equation}
  E_R(\rho)
  :=\min_{\sigma\in\operatorname{Sep}(\mathbb{C}^2:\mathbb{C}^2)}
       D(\rho\Vert\sigma),
  \qquad
  D(\rho\Vert\sigma)
  :=\begin{cases}
    \operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\rho(\log\rho-\log\sigma)\right],
      &\operatorname{supp}\rho\subseteq\operatorname{supp}\sigma,\\
    +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:p12-relative-entropy-entanglement}
\end{equation}
In the finite branch of Eq.~\eqref{eq:p12-relative-entropy-entanglement}, the
trace is evaluated on $\operatorname{supp}\rho$, with $0\log 0:=0$.  The
formula sought must determine at least one minimizing state $\sigma_\rho$ for
every $\rho$.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

The general two-qubit formula is listed by Kr\"uger and Werner, and
Miranowicz and Ishizaka explicitly distinguish this unresolved forward
problem from their solved inverse construction
\sourcecite{ref:p12-krueger-werner}{KW05},
\sourcecite{ref:p12-miranowicz-ishizaka}{MI08}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item For two qubits, separability is equivalent to positivity under partial
  transpose.  Thus Eq.~\eqref{eq:p12-relative-entropy-entanglement} is a convex
  optimization over the positive-partial-transpose set, but this equivalence
  does not produce a closed optimizer
  \sourcecite{ref:p12-horodecki}{HHH96}.

  \item Miranowicz and Ishizaka solved the inverse problem: from a boundary
  separable state $\sigma$, they parameterized entangled states for which
  $\sigma$ is closest.  Their construction yields formulas for special
  families but does not invert to a closed map $\rho\mapsto\sigma_\rho$ for an
  arbitrary input
  \sourcecite{ref:p12-miranowicz-ishizaka}{MI08}.

  \item Friedland and Gour extended the inverse optimality characterization to
  general dimensions and proved uniqueness of the closest separable state for
  full-rank entangled inputs.  These structural results still do not evaluate
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p12-relative-entropy-entanglement} in closed form for every
  two-qubit state
  \sourcecite{ref:p12-friedland-gour}{FG11}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The source collection and the full discussion of the inverse construction
explicitly identify the forward formula as open
\sourcecite{ref:p12-krueger-werner}{KW05},
\sourcecite{ref:p12-miranowicz-ishizaka}{MI08}.  The unresolved step is a
closed determination of $\sigma_\rho$ from arbitrary input data $\rho$;
special-state formulas and an inverse parameterization do not supply it.
