\section{Strict stationary squeezing limit for one-mode thermal diffusion}
\label{sec:op_71f84253f425fc7e}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does every stable one-mode thermal Gaussian diffusion obey the sharp stationary squeezing bound $\lambda_{\min}(V_\infty)>\nu/2$?
Use $R=(q,p)^{\mathsf T}$, $[q,p]=i$, and covariance $V_{jk}=\langle\{R_j-\langle R_j\rangle,R_k-\langle R_k\rangle\}\rangle$.
Let $G$ be any real symmetric $2\times2$ matrix, $\kappa>0$, and $\nu=2\bar n+1$ with $\bar n\geq0$.
Define the drift and diffusion in Eq.~\eqref{eq:sr51-drift}.
\begin{equation}
A=\Omega G-\frac\kappa2I_2,\qquad D=\kappa\nu I_2,\qquad \Omega=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\label{eq:sr51-drift}
\end{equation}
Assume $A$ is Hurwitz stable: both eigenvalues have negative real part.
The stationary covariance $V_\infty$ solves Eq.~\eqref{eq:sr51-lyapunov}.
\begin{equation}
AV_\infty+V_\infty A^{\mathsf T}+D=0.
\label{eq:sr51-lyapunov}
\end{equation}
Sharpness means that the infimum of $\lambda_{\min}(V_\infty)$ over stable choices of $G$ is $\nu/2$.

\subsection*{Status}

Solved

\subsection*{Source}

This is the single-mode isotropic-diffusion form of the stationary squeezing limit in Harwood and Serafini, Eq.~(15) \sourcecite{ref:sr51-harwood}{HS20}. Their physical setting is passive interferometric coherent feedback with rotating-wave coupling to equal-temperature Markov baths.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Stability implies the unique solution $V_\infty=\int_0^\infty e^{At}D e^{A^{\mathsf T}t}\,dt$ is positive definite. Multiplying Eq.~\eqref{eq:sr51-lyapunov} by $V_\infty^{-1}$ and taking the trace gives Eq.~\eqref{eq:sr51-proof}, since $\operatorname{tr}A=-\kappa$.
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{tr}(V_\infty^{-1})=\frac2\nu,\qquad \frac1{\lambda_{\min}(V_\infty)}<\operatorname{tr}(V_\infty^{-1}).
\label{eq:sr51-proof}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:sr51-proof} proves the strict bound directly and agrees with the published result \sourcecite{ref:sr51-harwood}{HS20}.

  \item For $0\leq s<\kappa$, choose $G_s=\begin{pmatrix}0&-s/2\\-s/2&0\end{pmatrix}$. The stable drift and covariance in Eq.~\eqref{eq:sr51-sharp} show sharpness.
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 A_s&=\frac12\operatorname{diag}(-\kappa-s,-\kappa+s),\\
 V_\infty(s)&=\nu\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac\kappa{\kappa+s},\frac\kappa{\kappa-s}\right),\\
 \lim_{s\uparrow\kappa}\lambda_{\min}(V_\infty(s))&=\frac\nu2.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:sr51-sharp}
\end{equation}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The question is completely resolved, with a peer-reviewed source and a direct verification above. The limit is an infimum approached at instability, not a stable minimum. Isotropic noise and one mode are essential hypotheses; the statement does not cover arbitrary multimode feedback or anisotropic baths.
