\section{Umegaki relative entropy of local recovery}
\label{sec:op_8c6e6d0cc3d28e86}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does the conditional mutual information of every finite-dimensional
tripartite state dominate its Umegaki relative entropy of local recovery?
For a state $\rho_{ABC}$, define
\begin{equation}
  I(A:C\mid B)_\rho
  :=S(AB)_\rho+S(BC)_\rho-S(B)_\rho-S(ABC)_\rho.
  \label{eq:p46-cmi}
\end{equation}
With $D(\tau\Vert\omega):=\operatorname{Tr}[\tau(\log\tau-\log\omega)]$
when $\operatorname{supp}\tau\subseteq\operatorname{supp}\omega$, the
question is whether the quantity in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p46-cmi} always satisfies
\begin{equation}
  I(A:C\mid B)_\rho
  \stackrel{?}{\ge}
  \min_{\mathcal R_{B\to BC}}
  D\!\left(
    \rho_{ABC}
    \middle\Vert
    (\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\mathcal R_{B\to BC})(\rho_{AB})
  \right),
  \label{eq:p46-recovery-bound}
\end{equation}
where the minimum in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p46-recovery-bound} is over all completely
positive trace-preserving recovery maps $\mathcal R_{B\to BC}$.

\subsection*{Status}

Solved

\subsection*{Source}

The local Umegaki-recovery inequality was formulated by Li and Winter
\sourcecite{ref:p46-li-winter}{LW18}; the associated universal recovery-map
proposal is recorded as Eq.~(12.153) of Wilde's text
\sourcecite{ref:p46-wilde}{Wil17}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Li and Winter formulated the local Umegaki-recovery inequality in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p46-recovery-bound} and related it to a proposed universal,
  functorial recovery-map strengthening of relative-entropy monotonicity
  \sourcecite{ref:p46-li-winter}{LW18}.  Wilde recorded the latter proposal as
  Eq.~(12.153) in his text \sourcecite{ref:p46-wilde}{Wil17}.

  \item Fawzi and Fawzi disproved Eq.~\eqref{eq:p46-recovery-bound}.  Their
  explicit family consists of the pure three-qubit states
  \begin{equation}
    \begin{aligned}
      \rho^{(\theta)}_{ABC}
      &=\lvert\psi_\theta\rangle\!\langle\psi_\theta\rvert,\\
      \lvert\psi_\theta\rangle_{ABC}
      &=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\lvert0\rangle_B\lvert00\rangle_{AC}
        +\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\lvert1\rangle_B
        \bigl(\cos\theta\,\lvert01\rangle_{AC}
              +\sin\theta\,\lvert10\rangle_{AC}\bigr).
    \end{aligned}
    \label{eq:p46-counterexample-family}
  \end{equation}
  For sufficiently small positive $\theta$, the states in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p46-counterexample-family} violate
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p46-recovery-bound}.  The violation is certified by optimizing
  a semidefinite-representable Petz--R\'enyi-divergence lower bound on the
  relative entropy of recovery \sourcecite{ref:p46-fawzi}{FF18}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The answer to the local-recovery question in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p46-recovery-bound} is negative.  This is the conditional-mutual-
information consequence of the recovery proposal associated with
Eq.~(12.153) of \sourcecite{ref:p46-wilde}{Wil17}.  The archived statement is
deliberately restricted to local recovery: it does not assert that the same
counterexample rules out every nonfunctorial recovery channel allowed to act
jointly on an otherwise spectator system.
