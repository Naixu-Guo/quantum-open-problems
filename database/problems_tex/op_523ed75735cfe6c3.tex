\section{Equal-weight low-Choi-rank decompositions of quantum channels}
\label{sec:op_523ed75735cfe6c3}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Can every finite-dimensional quantum channel be written as the uniform
mixture of $d_B$ channels whose Choi ranks are at most the input dimension?
Let $A$ and $B$ have dimensions $d_A$ and $d_B$, respectively, and let
$\Phi:\mathcal L(A)\to\mathcal L(B)$ be completely positive and trace
preserving.  In a fixed orthonormal basis of $A$, define its Choi operator by
\begin{equation}
  J(\Phi)
  :=\sum_{i,j=1}^{d_A}
      \lvert i\rangle\!\langle j\rvert_A\otimes
      \Phi\!\left(\lvert i\rangle\!\langle j\rvert_A\right),
  \label{eq:p58-choi-operator}
\end{equation}
The rank of the operator in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p58-choi-operator} is independent
of the chosen basis.  Determine whether every $\Phi$ admits completely
positive trace-preserving maps
$\Phi_1,\ldots,\Phi_{d_B}:\mathcal L(A)\to\mathcal L(B)$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
  \Phi=\frac1{d_B}\sum_{r=1}^{d_B}\Phi_r,
  \qquad
  \operatorname{rank}J(\Phi_r)\leq d_A
  \quad\text{for every }r\in\{1,\ldots,d_B\}.
  \label{eq:p58-equal-weight-decomposition}
\end{equation}
Thus Eq.~\eqref{eq:p58-equal-weight-decomposition} requires both the
prescribed input-dimension rank bound and exactly equal mixing weights.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Ruskai records the Audenaert--Ruskai channel-decomposition conjecture and its
unital and positive-block-matrix formulations explicitly
\sourcecite{ref:p58-ruskai}{Rus07}.  The Choi-rank bound $d_A$ in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p58-equal-weight-decomposition} is the audited correction of
the inconsistent $d_B$ bound printed in the source's channel formulation;
it agrees with the companion formulations and the modern statement studied
by Kumar and Wolf \sourcecite{ref:p58-kumar-wolf}{KW26}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The stronger positive-block-matrix formulation is known when
  $d_B=2$: a contraction can be expressed as the midpoint of two unitaries,
  yielding two positive summands of rank at most $d_A$ with the prescribed
  diagonal-block sum.  Because that formulation implies the channel
  decomposition, Eq.~\eqref{eq:p58-equal-weight-decomposition} holds for
  every qubit-output channel
  \sourcecite{ref:p58-ruskai-szarek-werner}{RSW02},
  \sourcecite{ref:p58-ruskai}{Rus07}.

  \item Kumar and Wolf prove the equal-weight decomposition in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p58-equal-weight-decomposition} for every qubit-input
  channel, for all classical-to-quantum and quantum-to-classical channels,
  and for a nonzero-measure family in every pair of dimensions.  They also
  prove an unequal-weight version for $3\to3$ channels; that weaker result
  does not establish the equal weights required here.  Their first-version
  preprint therefore settles substantial subclasses but not arbitrary
  $(d_A,d_B)$ \sourcecite{ref:p58-kumar-wolf}{KW26}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The remaining problem is the equal-weight decomposition in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p58-equal-weight-decomposition} for arbitrary input and output
dimensions outside the proved subclasses.  At the source audit cutoff, the
broad 2026 partial result was an unrefereed first-version preprint.
