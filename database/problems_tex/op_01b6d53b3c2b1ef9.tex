\section{Complete isoentangled mutually unbiased bases of two qutrits}
\label{sec:op_01b6d53b3c2b1ef9}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Do there exist ten orthonormal bases of $\mathbb C^3\otimes\mathbb C^3$ that are pairwise mutually unbiased and have identical entanglement spectra for all their vectors?

Mutual unbiasedness means $|\langle u|v\rangle|^2=1/9$ for every pair of vectors $u,v$ in different bases. The entanglement spectrum of a unit vector $u$ is the unordered list of eigenvalues of $\operatorname{Tr}_B(\ket{u}\!\langle u\rvert)$, where $B$ is the second qutrit. Require the same spectrum across all ninety vectors.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Damski and Życzkowski ask for complete isoentangled sets on two equal prime-dimensional systems beyond qubits in Sec.~I \sourcecite{ref:d8-02-dz26}{DZ26}. The common-spectrum formulation uses the local-unitary definition in Czartowski et al. \sourcecite{ref:d8-02-cggz20}{CGGZ20}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item A complete set of five isoentangled bases on two qubits is constructed in Czartowski et al. \sourcecite{ref:d8-02-cggz20}{CGGZ20}.

  \item Damski et al. construct nine mutually unbiased Hadamard bases generated by local Weyl--Heisenberg actions on a fiducial vector in the two-qutrit case. Appending the computational basis yields a complete mutually unbiased set whose extra basis consists of product states (Sec.~IV.2 and Theorem~2) \sourcecite{ref:d8-02-dbpcz26}{DBPCZ26}.
\end{itemize}
