\section{Distillation rate of W states from a Bell triangle}
\label{sec:op_da0450f40da1eea8}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Alice, Bob, and Charlie share $n$ perfect Bell pairs on each of the edges $AB$, $BC$, and $CA$. Let $\Phi_2$ be the projector onto $(\ket{00}+\ket{11})/\sqrt{2}$. The state of one triangle is $\tau_\triangle=\Phi_2^{AB}\otimes\Phi_2^{BC}\otimes\Phi_2^{CA}$.

For a proposed rate $r$, the parties aim to distill $\lfloor rn\rfloor$ copies of the tripartite W state with vanishing trace-distance error on the full target. Let $\mathrm{W}_3$ be the projector onto $(\ket{100}+\ket{010}+\ket{001})/\sqrt{3}$.

What is the optimal LOCC distillation rate $R_{\triangle\to\mathrm{W}}$? The rate is defined by
\begin{equation}
  R_{\triangle\to\mathrm{W}}=\sup\left\{r\geq 0:\lim_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\Lambda_n\in\mathrm{LOCC}}
  \tfrac12\left\lVert\Lambda_n\bigl(\tau_\triangle^{\otimes n}\bigr)
  -\mathrm{W}_3^{\otimes\lfloor rn\rfloor}\right\rVert_1=0\right\}.
  \label{eq:triangle-w-rate}
\end{equation}
In Eq.~\eqref{eq:triangle-w-rate}, the infimum is over LOCC channels with $\lfloor rn\rfloor$ output qubits per party.

Give an achievable rate and a matching converse for arbitrary LOCC, counting W states per triangle.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Eisert asks for the minimum total Bell-pair cost of forming W states asymptotically \sourcecite{ref:a1eea8-eisert01}{Eisert01} (Example 2.28). For this permutation-invariant target, the cost and the equal-edge rate ask the same question. A protocol using unequal numbers of pairs can be run on three blocks with cyclically permuted party roles, balancing the total consumption across the edges.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Let $h=h_2(1/3)$, where $h_2$ is the binary entropy in bits. Each one-qubit W marginal has entropy $h$. The bounds are given in Eq.~\eqref{eq:triangle-w-bounds}.
\begin{equation}
  \frac{3}{2h}\;\leq\; R_{\triangle\to\mathrm{W}}\;\leq\;\frac{2}{h}.
  \label{eq:triangle-w-bounds}
\end{equation}
For the lower bound, one party prepares a block locally, applies Schumacher compression separately to the two remote shares, and teleports them at a cost approaching $h$ Bell pairs per output on each incident edge. Using the three choices of preparing party on equal-size blocks balances the edge costs \sourcecite{ref:a1eea8-streltsovmeignanteisert20}{StreltsovMeignantEisert20} (Appendix B).

For the upper bound, group Bob and Charlie into one laboratory. The input has two ebits per triangle across this cut and each output W state has $h$ ebits. The bipartite pure-state conversion converse applies to the enlarged class of protocols \sourcecite{ref:a1eea8-streltsovmeignanteisert20}{StreltsovMeignantEisert20} (Eq.~(3)).
\end{itemize}
