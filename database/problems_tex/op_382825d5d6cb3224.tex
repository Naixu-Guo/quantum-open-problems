\section{Additivity of unassisted classical capacity}
\label{sec:op_382825d5d6cb3224}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Let $\Phi$ and $\Psi$ be finite-dimensional quantum channels. Write $S(\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\log_2\rho)$, and define the Holevo information of a channel $\mathcal N$ by
\begin{equation}
  \chi(\mathcal N)=\sup_{\{p_x,\rho_x\}}\left[
    S\!\left(\sum_x p_x\mathcal N(\rho_x)\right)
    -\sum_xp_x S\bigl(\mathcal N(\rho_x)\bigr)\right].
  \label{eq:rca-holevo}
\end{equation}
The supremum is over finite ensembles of channel input states. Its unassisted classical capacity is $C(\mathcal N)=\sup_{n\geq1}\chi(\mathcal N^{\otimes n})/n$, using Eq.~\eqref{eq:rca-holevo} with arbitrary ensembles on the joint input. Does
\begin{equation}
 C(\Phi\otimes\Psi)=C(\Phi)+C(\Psi)
 \label{eq:rca-additivity}
\end{equation}
hold for every pair of such channels? For the product channel in Eq.~\eqref{eq:rca-additivity}, one use consists of one use of each factor.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Lovitz and Wu, Section~1.3, Eq.~(33), and the outlook of Zhu and Wang explicitly ask whether the fully regularized classical capacity can be superadditive \sourcecite{ref:rca-lovitz-wu}{LW26} \sourcecite{ref:rca-zhu-wang}{ZW26}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Using independent encodings establishes $C(\Phi\otimes\Psi)\geq C(\Phi)+C(\Psi)$. Shor's additivity theorem gives equality when one channel is entanglement breaking, meaning its output is separable from every reference system, because the same theorem applies to every tensor power of the channel pair \sourcecite{ref:rca-shor}{Sho02}.

  \item Hastings exhibited a strict Holevo information violation for a pair at one use of each factor. Lovitz and Wu provide further deterministic and random examples and leave Eq.~\eqref{eq:rca-additivity} open \sourcecite{ref:rca-hastings}{Has09} \sourcecite{ref:rca-lovitz-wu}{LW26}.

  \item Collins and Youn show nonadditivity of regularized minimum output entropy in an infinite-dimensional commuting-operator model. Kalantar and Shobeiri extend this line of examples within the same model. Equation~\eqref{eq:rca-additivity} concerns finite-dimensional channels \sourcecite{ref:rca-collins-youn}{CY22} \sourcecite{ref:rca-kalantar-shobeiri}{KS25}.
\end{itemize}
