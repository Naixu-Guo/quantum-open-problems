\section{Multimode constrained output entropy of a pure-loss channel}
\label{sec:op_3cd14aef409b226b}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Is Guha's multimode Strong Conjecture~2 true for every correlated input state?
Fix $n\geq1$, $K\geq0$, and $0<\eta<1$.  Let the modes
$A_1,\ldots,A_n$ be in the vacuum and let $\rho_{B^n}$ be an arbitrary joint
$n$-mode state.  Apply identical beam splitters whose output annihilation
operators satisfy
\begin{equation}
  \hat c_i=\sqrt{\eta}\,\hat a_i
    +\sqrt{1-\eta}\,\hat b_i,
  \qquad i\in\{1,\ldots,n\},
  \label{eq:p75-beamsplitter-output}
\end{equation}
and denote the resulting joint state of $C_1,\ldots,C_n$ by
$\rho_{C^n}$.  In the convention of Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-beamsplitter-output},
$\eta$ multiplies the vacuum $A$ port, so the attenuator from the
information-bearing $B$ port to $C$ has transmissivity $1-\eta$.  Define the
thermal entropy function by
\begin{equation}
  g(x):=(x+1)\log_2(x+1)-x\log_2x,
  \qquad x\geq0,
  \label{eq:p75-thermal-entropy}
\end{equation}
with $0\log_2 0:=0$.  Using the function in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-thermal-entropy}, impose the sole
input-entropy constraint $S(\rho_{B^n})=ng(K)$.  Does the state produced in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-beamsplitter-output} always obey
\begin{equation}
  S(\rho_{C^n})\geq ng((1-\eta)K)?
  \label{eq:p75-strong-conjecture-two}
\end{equation}
Equality in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-strong-conjecture-two} is attained when
$\rho_{B^n}$ is a tensor product of $n$ thermal states of mean photon number
$K$; the conjecture asserts that arbitrary correlations cannot lower the
output entropy further.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Guha, Shapiro, and Erkmen explicitly formulated
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-strong-conjecture-two} as Strong Conjecture~2
\sourcecite{ref:p75-guha-shapiro-erkmen}{GSE07}.  Wilde records the same
multimode statement as the hypothesis needed for the converse in the
pure-loss dynamic-capacity theorem
\sourcecite{ref:p75-wilde}{Wil17}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item De Palma, Trevisan, and Giovannetti proved
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-strong-conjecture-two} for one mode and every input state
  of fixed entropy.  Their theorem leaves the arbitrary correlated case
  $n\geq2$ open
  \sourcecite{ref:p75-de-palma-one-mode}{DTG17}.

  \item For arbitrary $n$, thermal product states are proved optimal when the
  input is restricted to states diagonal in some product basis
  \sourcecite{ref:p75-de-palma-multimode}{DTG17b}.  Related unrestricted-input
  multimode optimality is known for entanglement-breaking Gaussian
  attenuators, but those noise regimes do not settle the nontrivial
  quantum-limited attenuator in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-beamsplitter-output}
  \sourcecite{ref:p75-de-palma-eb}{DP19}.

  \item Falco and De Palma proved a multimode conditional quantum entropy
  power inequality for general linear mixing.  Its resulting exponential-
  entropy lower bound is weaker than the thermal $g$-function bound in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-strong-conjecture-two}, so it does not close Strong
  Conjecture~2
  \sourcecite{ref:p75-falco-de-palma}{FDP25}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The unresolved regime is $n\geq2$ with an arbitrary, possibly entangled and
non-Gaussian, joint input of fixed entropy.  Unconstrained Gaussian
minimum-output-entropy theorems and one-mode constrained theorems are not
equivalent to Eq.~\eqref{eq:p75-strong-conjecture-two}.
