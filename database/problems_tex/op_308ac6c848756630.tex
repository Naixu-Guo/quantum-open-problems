\section{SIC-POVM existence in every dimension}
\label{sec:op_308ac6c848756630}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does a symmetric informationally complete positive-operator-valued measure
exist in every finite dimension $d\ge2$?  Equivalently, determine whether for
every such $d$ there are $d^2$ unit vectors
$\lvert\psi_1\rangle,\ldots,\lvert\psi_{d^2}\rangle\in\mathbb{C}^d$
satisfying
\begin{equation}
  \sum_{j=1}^{d^2}
    \lvert\psi_j\rangle\!\langle\psi_j\rvert=dI_d,
  \qquad
  \left|\langle\psi_j\vert\psi_k\rangle\right|^2
    =\frac{1}{d+1}
  \quad\text{for all }j\ne k.
  \label{eq:p17-sic-conditions}
\end{equation}
When Eq.~\eqref{eq:p17-sic-conditions} holds, the effects
$\Pi_j=d^{-1}\lvert\psi_j\rangle\!\langle\psi_j\rvert$ form the desired
SIC-POVM.  No covariance or additional symmetry is required.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Renes, Blume-Kohout, Scott, and Caves explicitly conjecture that SIC-POVMs
exist in every finite dimension \sourcecite{ref:p17-renes-et-al}{RBS+04}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Renes, Blume-Kohout, Scott, and Caves explicitly conjectured existence
  in every dimension and found Weyl--Heisenberg-covariant numerical solutions
  through $d=45$.  Numerical solutions do not prove the universal quantifier
  in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p17-sic-conditions}
  \sourcecite{ref:p17-renes-et-al}{RBS+04}.

  \item In their $2022$ survey, Horodecki, Rudnicki, and \.{Z}yczkowski
  identified construction of SIC-POVMs in an unbounded sequence of finite
  dimensions as an intermediate open milestone. The universal statement in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p17-sic-conditions} implies that milestone
  \sourcecite{ref:p17-horodecki-et-al}{HRZ22}.

  \item Appleby, Bengtsson, Flammia, and Goyeneche reported numerical solutions
  in every dimension through $d=181$ and in many larger dimensions.  The
  computations provide extensive evidence but no all-dimensional construction
  \sourcecite{ref:p17-appleby-et-al}{ABFG19}.

  \item In $2025$, Appleby, Flammia, and Kopp gave a construction of
  SICs for every $d>3$, conditional on the order-one abelian
  Stark conjecture for real quadratic fields and a special-value identity
  for the Shintani--Faddeev modular cocycle
  \sourcecite{ref:p17-appleby-flammia-kopp}{AFK25}.

  \item A claimed unconditional proof posted in $2026$ was withdrawn after
  the author stated that the proof was incorrect, so it does not change the
  status of the problem
  \sourcecite{ref:p17-joka}{Jok26}.

  \item In September $2026$, Radchenko and Wheeler proved algebraicity of
  real-quadratic Stark--Shintani invariants and the rank-one
  twisted-convolution identity (Theorems~1 and~7)
  \sourcecite{ref:p17-radchenko-wheeler}{RW26}.
  Appleby, Flammia, and Kopp extended the identity to all admissible tuples
  and constructed Weyl--Heisenberg-covariant ghost SICs for every $d>3$
  (Theorems~1.2--1.3) \sourcecite{ref:p17-twisted-convolution}{AFK26}.
  This supplies the special-value identity in the earlier conditional
  construction; converting the ghost SICs to Hermitian SIC projectors
  requires the appropriate Galois conjugation.

  \item The manuscript dated 4 October $2026$, prepared and edited by Danylo
  Radchenko and Campbell Wheeler, reports Stark reciprocity and unitary
  Galois conjugates (Theorem~3 and Proposition~7). Section~9.2 claims SIC existence in every dimension by combining
  these results with the earlier construction.
  \sourcecite{ref:p17-stark-reciprocity}{SU26}.
  The manuscript has not yet passed peer review. This is recorded as
  significant reported progress; the catalog status remains Unsolved.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The catalog retains Unsolved while documenting the reported progress above;
  a status change requires a separate maintainer decision. The target is
  unconditional existence for every integer $d\ge2$ in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p17-sic-conditions}. The weaker objective of an unbounded
  sequence of finite dimensions is subsumed here as an intermediate milestone
  rather than maintained as a separate problem.
