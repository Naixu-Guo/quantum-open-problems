\section{Zauner-symmetric Weyl--Heisenberg SIC fiducials}
\label{sec:op_ff2e80b425aebb86}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does every finite dimension admit a Weyl--Heisenberg SIC fiducial that is an
eigenvector of a Zauner Clifford unitary?  For $d\geq2$, define the displacement
operators $D_{\mathbf p}:=D_{p,q}:=X_d^pZ_d^q$ for
$\mathbf p=(p,q)^{\mathsf T}\in\mathbb Z_d^2$, using
\begin{equation}
  X_d\lvert j\rangle=\lvert j+1\!\!\pmod d\rangle,
  \qquad
  Z_d\lvert j\rangle=e^{2\pi i j/d}\lvert j\rangle,
  \qquad \mathbf p=(p,q)^{\mathsf T}\in\mathbb Z_d^2.
  \label{eq:p19-displacements}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:p19-displacements} fixes the Weyl--Heisenberg orbit up to
irrelevant phases.  Let $U_Z$ be a Clifford unitary whose action on displacement
operators is
\begin{equation}
  U_ZD_{\mathbf p}U_Z^\dagger\doteq D_{F_Z\mathbf p},
  \qquad
  F_Z=
  \begin{pmatrix}
    0&-1\\
    1&-1
  \end{pmatrix},
  \label{eq:p19-zauner-action}
\end{equation}
where indices are reduced modulo $d$ and $\doteq$ denotes equality up to phase.
Equation~\eqref{eq:p19-zauner-action} fixes the distinguished order-three
Clifford symmetry.
The question is whether, for every $d\geq2$, there are a unit vector
$\lvert\phi\rangle$ and a phase $e^{i\theta}$ such that
\begin{equation}
  U_Z\lvert\phi\rangle=e^{i\theta}\lvert\phi\rangle,
  \qquad
  \bigl|\langle\phi\rvert D_{\mathbf p}\lvert\phi\rangle\bigr|^2
  =\frac{1}{d+1}
  \quad\text{for every }\mathbf p\in\mathbb Z_d^2\setminus\{\mathbf0\}.
  \label{eq:p19-zauner-sic-condition}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:p19-zauner-sic-condition} simultaneously imposes Zauner
symmetry and the SIC overlap equations.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Appleby stated Eq.~\eqref{eq:p19-zauner-sic-condition} as
  Conjecture~B and verified it for the numerical fiducials then known, through
  $d=45$ \sourcecite{ref:p19-appleby-clifford}{App05}.

  \item Larger exact and numerical data sets continue to exhibit the Zauner
  symmetry, including Weyl--Heisenberg SIC solutions in every dimension
  through $d=181$.  Finite computations do not prove the universal
  quantifier in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p19-zauner-sic-condition}
  \sourcecite{ref:p19-appleby-data}{ABFG19}.

  \item In $2025$, Appleby, Flammia, and Kopp gave a construction of
  Zauner-symmetric SICs for every $d>3$, conditional on the order-one abelian
  Stark conjecture for real quadratic fields and a special-value identity
  for the Shintani--Faddeev modular cocycle
  \sourcecite{ref:p19-appleby-stark}{AFK25}.

  \item In September $2026$, Radchenko and Wheeler proved algebraicity of
  real-quadratic Stark--Shintani invariants and the rank-one
  twisted-convolution identity (Theorems~1 and~7)
  \sourcecite{ref:p19-radchenko-wheeler}{RW26}.
  Appleby, Flammia, and Kopp extended the identity to all admissible tuples
  and constructed Weyl--Heisenberg-covariant ghost SICs for every $d>3$
  (Theorems~1.2--1.3) \sourcecite{ref:p19-twisted-convolution}{AFK26}.
  This supplies the special-value identity in the earlier conditional
  construction; converting the ghost SICs to Hermitian SIC projectors
  requires the appropriate Galois conjugation.

  \item The manuscript dated 4 October $2026$, prepared and edited by Danylo
  Radchenko and Campbell Wheeler, reports Stark reciprocity and unitary
  Galois conjugates (Theorem~3 and Proposition~7). Section~9.2 claims the existence part of Zauner's conjecture,
  providing proposed arithmetic input for the Zauner-symmetric construction.
  \sourcecite{ref:p19-stark-reciprocity}{SU26}.
  The manuscript has not yet passed peer review. This is recorded as
  significant reported progress; the catalog status remains Unsolved.
\end{itemize}
