\section{Exponential strong converse for transpose-degradable channels}
\label{sec:op_414fbcc7d5dc0c64}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does every finite-dimensional transpose-degradable channel satisfy an
exponential strong converse for quantum communication at its single-letter
quantum capacity?  Let $V:A\to B\otimes E$ be an isometry and suppose that
\begin{equation}
  \Phi(X):=\operatorname{Tr}_E(VXV^\dagger),
  \qquad
  \Phi^c(X):=\operatorname{Tr}_B(VXV^\dagger),
  \qquad
  \mathsf T_E\circ\Phi^c=\mathcal G\circ\Phi,
  \label{eq:p55-transpose-degradable}
\end{equation}
where $\mathsf T_E$ is transpose in a fixed basis of $E$ and
$\mathcal G:\mathcal L(B)\to\mathcal L(E)$ is completely positive and
trace preserving.  For the channel in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p55-transpose-degradable}, transpose degradability gives
\begin{equation}
  Q(\Phi)=Q^{(1)}(\Phi)
  :=\max_{\rho_A}
     \left[S(\Phi(\rho_A))-S(\Phi^c(\rho_A))\right],
  \qquad
  S(\sigma):=-\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\log_2\sigma).
  \label{eq:p55-single-letter-capacity}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:p55-single-letter-capacity} fixes the rate threshold.

At blocklength $n$, let
$\mathcal E_n:\mathcal L(S_n)\to\mathcal L(A^{\otimes n})$ and
$\mathcal R_n:\mathcal L(B^{\otimes n})\to\mathcal L(\widehat S_n)$ be
arbitrary encoder and decoder channels, with
$\dim R_n=\dim S_n=\dim\widehat S_n=M_n$.  Define the maximally entangled
target by
\begin{equation}
  \varphi_{M_n}:=
  |\varphi_{M_n}\rangle\!\langle\varphi_{M_n}|,
  \qquad
  |\varphi_{M_n}\rangle
  :=\frac1{\sqrt{M_n}}\sum_{i=1}^{M_n}|i\rangle_{R_n}|i\rangle_{S_n}.
  \label{eq:p55-maximally-entangled-target}
\end{equation}
Using the target in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p55-maximally-entangled-target}, let
\begin{equation}
  \omega_n:=
  \left(\operatorname{id}_{R_n}\otimes
  \mathcal R_n\circ\Phi^{\otimes n}\circ\mathcal E_n\right)(\varphi_{M_n}),
  \qquad
  r_n:=\frac1n\log_2M_n,
  \qquad
  F_n:=\operatorname{Tr}(\varphi_{M_n}^{R_n\widehat S_n}\omega_n).
  \label{eq:p55-code-fidelity}
\end{equation}
The problem is whether, for every channel in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p55-transpose-degradable}, the quantities in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p55-code-fidelity} satisfy
\begin{equation}
  \forall R>Q(\Phi)\ \exists\,\gamma_R>0,\ n_R\in\mathbb N:
  \quad
  r_n\geq R,\ n\geq n_R
  \ \Longrightarrow\
  F_n\leq2^{-\gamma_R n}
  \label{eq:p55-exponential-strong-converse}
\end{equation}
for every encoder--decoder sequence.  Equation~
\eqref{eq:p55-exponential-strong-converse} is the all-code exponential
strong-converse property at the threshold in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p55-single-letter-capacity}.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Singh and Datta supply the complex-linear transpose-degradable formulation and
its single-letter capacity
\sourcecite{ref:p55-singh-datta}{SD22}.  Morgan and Winter explicitly note
that extending their degradable-channel converse method to the corresponding
conjugate-degradable class requires arguments not provided there; together
these papers pose the present class-wide question implicitly
\sourcecite{ref:p55-morgan-winter}{MW14}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Transpose degradability proves the tensor-power identity
  \begin{equation}
    Q^{(1)}(\Phi^{\otimes n})=nQ^{(1)}(\Phi)
    \qquad(n\geq1).
    \label{eq:p55-coherent-information-additivity}
  \end{equation}
  Equation~\eqref{eq:p55-coherent-information-additivity} establishes the
  capacity formula in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p55-single-letter-capacity}, but gives no
  finite-block upper bound on $F_n$
  \sourcecite{ref:p55-singh-datta}{SD22}.

  \item For every ordinarily degradable channel $\mathcal N$ and fixed
  purified-distance error $\varepsilon<1/\sqrt2$, Morgan and Winter proved
  \begin{equation}
    \log_2 N_E(n,\varepsilon\mid\mathcal N)
      \leq nQ^{(1)}(\mathcal N)+O(\sqrt{n\log n}),
    \label{eq:p55-pretty-strong-converse}
  \end{equation}
  where $N_E$ is the largest entanglement-generation code dimension.
  Equation~\eqref{eq:p55-pretty-strong-converse} is a pretty-strong converse
  for ordinary degradability, and their proof does not extend to
  transpose-degradable channels solely from coherent-information additivity
  \sourcecite{ref:p55-morgan-winter}{MW14}.

  \item Kondra et al. proved in 2026 that every finite-dimensional ordinarily
  degradable or antidegradable channel $\mathcal N$ obeys, for every rate above
  capacity,
  \begin{equation}
    r_n\geq R>Q(\mathcal N)
    \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
    F_n\leq2^{-\gamma_R n}
    \quad\text{for all sufficiently large }n.
    \label{eq:p55-ordinary-exponential-converse}
  \end{equation}
  Equation~\eqref{eq:p55-ordinary-exponential-converse} settles the known
  transpose-degradable examples that are also degradable, but its theorem does
  not cover a hypothetical strict transpose-degradable channel
  \sourcecite{ref:p55-kondra-et-al}{KBK+26}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

If every transpose-degradable channel is ordinarily degradable, then
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p55-ordinary-exponential-converse} resolves the problem.  If a
strict channel exists as asked in Problem~53, the exponential converse for
that case remains unproved.
