\section{Achievability of the Rains bound under PPT-preserving channels}
\label{sec:op_4cf3e7b8663b1d41}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Consider distilling the bipartite Bell-diagonal state
\begin{equation}
  \rho_{\mathbf p}
  =p_I\lvert\Phi^+\rangle\!\langle\Phi^+\rvert
  +p_X\lvert\Psi^+\rangle\!\langle\Psi^+\rvert
  +p_Y\lvert\Psi^-\rangle\!\langle\Psi^-\rvert
  +p_Z\lvert\Phi^-\rangle\!\langle\Phi^-\rvert,
  \label{eq:p4-bell-diagonal-state}
\end{equation}
where $p_i>0$ and $p_I+p_X+p_Y+p_Z=1$, and
$\lvert\Phi^\pm\rangle:=(\lvert00\rangle\pm\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and
$\lvert\Psi^\pm\rangle:=(\lvert01\rangle\pm\lvert10\rangle)/\sqrt2$.
Is the Rains bound of the state in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-bell-diagonal-state}
achievable by a positive-partial-transpose-state-preserving
(PPT-state-preserving, or PPT-preserving) quantum channel?  If so, what is the
constructive quantum channel?  A channel is PPT-state-preserving if every PPT
input state is mapped to a PPT output state.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Rains introduced the PPT-preserving distillation framework and its
semidefinite-programming bound; the present Bell-diagonal achievability
question is an implicit specialization of that work
\sourcecite{ref:p4-rains-1999}{Rai99},
\sourcecite{ref:p4-rains-2001}{Rai01}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Rains showed that the Rains bound $R(\rho_{\mathbf p})$ upper-bounds
  the entanglement distillable from $\rho_{\mathbf p}$ by PPT-preserving
  operations:
  \begin{equation}
    D_{\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho_{\mathbf p})\le R(\rho_{\mathbf p}).
    \label{eq:p4-rains-upper-bound}
  \end{equation}
  Equation~\eqref{eq:p4-rains-upper-bound} is the relevant converse bound for
  the operational class in this problem
  \sourcecite{ref:p4-rains-1999}{Rai99},
  \sourcecite{ref:p4-rains-2001}{Rai01}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The problem asks whether the upper bound in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-rains-upper-bound} is achievable for the Bell-diagonal family
in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-bell-diagonal-state}, and, if so, for an explicit
construction of a PPT-preserving channel attaining it.
