\section{Achievability of the Rains bound under completely PPT-preserving channels}
\label{sec:op_4cf3e7b8663b1d41}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Is the Rains bound asymptotically achievable by completely
positive-partial-transpose-preserving (completely PPT-preserving) channels
for every full-rank two-qubit Bell-diagonal state, and what channel family
achieves it? Consider
\begin{equation}
  \rho_{\mathbf p}
  =p_I\lvert\Phi^+\rangle\!\langle\Phi^+\rvert
  +p_X\lvert\Psi^+\rangle\!\langle\Psi^+\rvert
  +p_Y\lvert\Psi^-\rangle\!\langle\Psi^-\rvert
  +p_Z\lvert\Phi^-\rangle\!\langle\Phi^-\rvert,
  \label{eq:p4-bell-diagonal-state}
\end{equation}
where $p_i>0$ and $p_I+p_X+p_Y+p_Z=1$, and
$\lvert\Phi^\pm\rangle:=(\lvert00\rangle\pm\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$ and
$\lvert\Psi^\pm\rangle:=(\lvert01\rangle\pm\lvert10\rangle)/\sqrt2$.

A channel $\Lambda_n:A^nB^n\to A'_nB'_n$ is completely PPT-preserving if
\begin{equation}
  \Lambda_n\text{ is CPTP},\qquad
  \Gamma_{B'_n}\circ\Lambda_n\circ\Gamma_{B^n}
     \text{ is completely positive},
  \label{eq:p4-complete-ppt}
\end{equation}
where $\Gamma$ is partial transposition on the indicated subsystem.
Equation~\eqref{eq:p4-complete-ppt} requires PPT preservation also with
arbitrary local ancillas.
Write $D(\rho\|\tau)=\operatorname{Tr}\rho(\log_2\rho-\log_2\tau)$
with the standard support convention, and define
\begin{equation}
  R(\rho):=\inf_{\tau\geq0,\,\|\tau^{\Gamma_B}\|_1\leq1}
                  D(\rho\|\tau).
  \label{eq:p4-rains-definition}
\end{equation}
For the state in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-bell-diagonal-state}, achievability of
the Rains bound in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-rains-definition} means a sequence satisfying Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-complete-ppt} and
\begin{equation}
  \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{\log_2 M_n}{n}
      \geq R(\rho_{\mathbf p}),\qquad
  \big\|\Lambda_n(\rho_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n})
             -\Phi_{M_n}\big\|_1\longrightarrow0,
  \label{eq:p4-achievability}
\end{equation}
where $M_n$ is a positive integer and
$\Phi_M:=M^{-1}\sum_{i,j=1}^M|ii\rangle\langle jj|$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:p4-achievability} imposes no efficiency requirement on
the construction.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Rains introduced the distillation framework and converse bound for
completely PPT-preserving channels; this Bell-diagonal achievability question
is an implicit specialization of that work
\sourcecite{ref:p4-rains-1999}{Rai99},
\sourcecite{ref:p4-rains-2001}{Rai01}.
The operation class is explicitly the one in Definition 6 of
Regula, Fang, Wang, and Gu \sourcecite{ref:p4-regula-et-al}{RFWG19}.
This corrects the earlier, weaker requirement of preserving PPT input
states without ancillary extensions.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item For the completely PPT-preserving operation class, Rains' converse is
\begin{equation}
  D_{\mathrm{cPPT}}(\rho_{\mathbf p})\leq R(\rho_{\mathbf p}),
  \label{eq:p4-rains-upper-bound}
\end{equation}
where $D_{\mathrm{cPPT}}$ is the supremum of asymptotically achievable
distillation rates. Equation~\eqref{eq:p4-rains-upper-bound} gives the upper
bound whose attainability is asked in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-achievability}
\sourcecite{ref:p4-rains-1999}{Rai99},
\sourcecite{ref:p4-rains-2001}{Rai01}.

  \item For this state family, the Rains bound is additive. Let
$p=\max_i p_i$ and $h_2(x)=-x\log_2x-(1-x)\log_2(1-x)$.
If $p\leq1/2$, the state is PPT and the bound is zero. If $p>1/2$, let
$\Phi$ be its largest Bell component, and let $\sigma$ have Bell
probabilities $q_{\max}=1/2$ and $q_i=p_i/[2(1-p)]$ otherwise.
This normalized state is PPT. Its relative-entropy supporting functional is
$A=\rho_{\mathbf p}\sigma^{-1}=2(1-p)I+2(2p-1)\Phi$.
The eigenvalues of $A^{\Gamma_B}$ are $1$ and $3-4p$, hence
$\|A^{\Gamma_B}\|_\infty=1$ and
$\operatorname{Tr}A\tau\leq1=\operatorname{Tr}A\sigma$ for every
Rains-feasible $\tau$. This is the convex first-order optimality condition
for $\sigma$. The same argument uses $A^{\otimes n}$ for every tensor
power, since partial transpose and the operator norm are multiplicative.
Consequently,
\begin{equation}
  R(\rho_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n})
   =E_{R,\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n})
   =\begin{cases}0,&p\leq1/2,\\ n[1-h_2(p)],&p>1/2,
     \end{cases}
  \label{eq:p4-bell-value}
\end{equation}
where $E_{R,\mathrm{PPT}}$ minimizes $D$ over normalized PPT states.
Equation~\eqref{eq:p4-bell-value} evaluates the converse but does not
construct a completely PPT-preserving distillation protocol.

  \item The weaker class of channels preserving PPT states without ancillary
extensions is $\mathrm{PPTP}_+$ in Definition 7 of Regula, Fang, Wang,
and Gu. Their Corollary 10, Eq.~(33), gives its distillation rate as
$E_{R,\mathrm{PPT}}^\infty$, so Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-bell-value} solves
achievability for that weaker class
\sourcecite{ref:p4-regula-et-al}{RFWG19}.
The generalized quantum Stein lemma used in this result has a complete
proof in Lami's Theorem 1 \sourcecite{ref:p4-lami}{Lam25}.
For $p>1/2$ and $0<r<1-h_2(p)$, it supplies effects $0\leq T_n\leq I$
with $\operatorname{Tr}T_n\rho_{\mathbf p}^{\otimes n}\to1$ and
$\sup_{\omega\in\mathrm{PPT}}\operatorname{Tr}T_n\omega\leq1/M_n$,
where $M_n=\lfloor2^{nr}\rfloor\geq2$ for sufficiently large $n$.
An optimization-defined channel family is
\begin{equation}
  \Lambda_n(X)=\operatorname{Tr}(T_nX)\Phi_{M_n}
   +\operatorname{Tr}[(I-T_n)X]
        \frac{I-\Phi_{M_n}}{M_n^2-1}.
  \label{eq:p4-weak-protocol}
\end{equation}
Each PPT input to Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-weak-protocol} yields an isotropic state
with maximally entangled weight at most $1/M_n$, so the map is
PPT-state-preserving. Letting $r$ approach the value in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-bell-value} gives that asymptotic rate. This does not
establish the complete PPT condition in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-complete-ppt}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The open question uses completely PPT-preserving channels as in Rains'
framework. Its former PPT-state-preserving wording described a strictly
larger class and is corrected explicitly here. The weaker-class result in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p4-weak-protocol} does not settle the intended stronger
operation class. The zero-rate case $p\leq1/2$ is immediate; the remaining
question concerns entangled full-rank Bell-diagonal states.
