\section{Entanglement cost of an amplitude-damping-channel Choi state}
\label{sec:op_7a9051ff6d0a1739}

\paragraph{Problem.}
What is the entanglement cost of the Choi state of the qubit
amplitude-damping channel
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal A_p(\rho)=A_0\rho A_0^\dagger+A_1\rho A_1^\dagger,
  \qquad 0\le p\le1?
  \label{eq:p8-amplitude-damping}
\end{equation}
The Kraus operators in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p8-amplitude-damping} are
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    A_0&=\lvert0\rangle\!\langle0\rvert
         +\sqrt{1-p}\,\lvert1\rangle\!\langle1\rvert
       =\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&\sqrt{1-p}\end{pmatrix},\\
    A_1&=\sqrt p\,\lvert0\rangle\!\langle1\rvert
       =\begin{pmatrix}0&\sqrt p\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:p8-kraus-operators}
\end{equation}
In Eq.~\eqref{eq:p8-kraus-operators}, $p$ is the decay probability of the
excited state.  Let
$\lvert\Phi^+\rangle_{RA}=(\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle)/\sqrt2$.
The normalized Choi state of the channel in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p8-amplitude-damping} is
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    \omega_p^{RB}
      &:=(\operatorname{id}_R\otimes\mathcal A_p)
          (\lvert\Phi^+\rangle\!\langle\Phi^+\rvert_{RA})\\
      &=\frac12\Bigl[
          \lvert00\rangle\!\langle00\rvert
          +\sqrt{1-p}\bigl(\lvert00\rangle\!\langle11\rvert
                           +\lvert11\rangle\!\langle00\rvert\bigr)
          +(1-p)\lvert11\rangle\!\langle11\rvert
          +p\lvert10\rangle\!\langle10\rvert
        \Bigr].
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:p8-choi-state}
\end{equation}
Here the first and second entries in each ket in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:p8-choi-state} label $R$ and $B$, respectively.  Thus the
question is to determine $E_C(\omega_p)$, the asymptotic number of ebits per
copy required to prepare many copies of $\omega_p$ by local operations and
classical communication.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

The question is implicit in the identity between entanglement cost and
regularized entanglement of formation, together with Wootters' single-copy
formula applied to this Choi state
\sourcecite{ref:p8-hayden-horodecki-terhal}{HHT01},
\sourcecite{ref:p8-wootters}{Woo98}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Wootters' two-qubit formula, together with the concurrence of
  $\omega_p$, gives the exact single-copy entanglement of formation
  \begin{equation}
    C(\omega_p)=\sqrt{1-p},
    \qquad
    E_F(\omega_p)=h_2\!\left(\frac{1+\sqrt p}{2}\right),
    \label{eq:p8-entanglement-of-formation}
  \end{equation}
  where $h_2(x):=-x\log_2x-(1-x)\log_2(1-x)$, with $0\log_2 0:=0$,
  \sourcecite{ref:p8-wootters}{Woo98}.  Equation~
  \eqref{eq:p8-entanglement-of-formation} determines one copy exactly, but it
  does not by itself determine the asymptotic entanglement cost.

  \item The entanglement cost equals the regularized entanglement of formation
  \begin{equation}
    E_C(\omega_p)
      =\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n E_F(\omega_p^{\otimes n})
      \le E_F(\omega_p)
      =h_2\!\left(\frac{1+\sqrt p}{2}\right)
    \label{eq:p8-regularized-entanglement-of-formation}
  \end{equation}
  \sourcecite{ref:p8-hayden-horodecki-terhal}{HHT01}.  Consequently,
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p8-regularized-entanglement-of-formation} reduces the problem
  to evaluating the regularization for this particular family of Choi
  states.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The unresolved step is to decide whether regularization lowers the
single-copy value in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p8-entanglement-of-formation}; equivalently,
one must evaluate the entanglement of formation on tensor powers of this Choi
state.  Problems~2 and~5 concern the same channel family but ask about channel
capacities, whereas the present problem asks about the entanglement cost of a
bipartite state associated with the channel.
