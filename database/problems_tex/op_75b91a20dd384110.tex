\section{Closed-form exact PPT distillable entanglement}
\label{sec:op_75b91a20dd384110}

\paragraph{Problem.}
What computable expression, if any, equals the regularized exact PPT
distillable entanglement of a bipartite state?  Let $\rho_{AB}$ be a state
on $\mathbb C^{d_A}\otimes\mathbb C^{d_B}$ with support projector
$P:=\Pi_{\operatorname{supp}(\rho)}$, and let $\Gamma$ denote partial
transposition on $B$.  Exact (zero-error) distillation under
PPT-preserving operations converts $\rho^{\otimes n}$ into a maximally
entangled state of Schmidt rank $M_n$ with unit fidelity.  The largest
one-shot rate is governed by the semidefinite program
\begin{equation}
  W_0(P):=\min\bigl\{\|E^{\Gamma}\|_\infty:\ P\leq E\leq\mathbb 1\bigr\},
  \qquad
  E^{(1)}_{0,\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho):=-\log_2W_0(P),
  \label{eq:75b9-one-shot}
\end{equation}
which depends on $\rho$ only through its support.  The regularized exact
PPT distillable entanglement is
\begin{equation}
  E^{\infty}_{0,\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho)
  :=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n E^{(1)}_{0,\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho^{\otimes n})
  =\lim_{n\to\infty}-\frac1n\log_2W_0(P^{\otimes n}).
  \label{eq:75b9-regularized}
\end{equation}
Is there a single-letter, efficiently computable formula, for example a
semidefinite program in $P$ alone, that equals
Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-regularized} for every bipartite state?

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Zhu and Wang disprove the previously conjectured formula and state that
determining the closed form of the exact PPT distillable entanglement
remains open \sourcecite{ref:75b9-zhu-wang}{ZW26}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Wang and Duan characterize one-copy deterministic PPT distillation
  by a semidefinite program: a maximally entangled state of integer
  Schmidt rank $M$ can be distilled exactly from $\rho$ if and only if
  $M\leq W_0(P)^{-1}$, which gives the one-shot rate in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-one-shot} \sourcecite{ref:75b9-wang-duan-sdp}{WD16}.

  \item Dropping the constraint $E\leq\mathbb 1$ in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-one-shot} yields the min-Rains relative entropy
  \begin{equation}
    R_{\min}(\rho):=-\log_2M(P),
    \qquad
    M(P):=\min\bigl\{\|R^{\Gamma}\|_\infty:\ R\geq P\bigr\},
    \label{eq:75b9-min-rains}
  \end{equation}
  which is multiplicative, $M(P\otimes Q)=M(P)M(Q)$, and therefore an
  additive single-letter upper bound
  $E^{\infty}_{0,\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho)\leq R_{\min}(\rho)$.  It is attained
  for all pure states and for some classes of mixed states, which made
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-min-rains} the candidate closed form
  \sourcecite{ref:75b9-wang-duan-nonadditivity}{WD17}.

  \item Every feasible effect in Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-one-shot} must act as
  the identity on the support of $\rho$, a constraint absent from
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-min-rains}.  Exploiting it, Zhu and Wang construct a
  rank-three qutrit--qutrit support $P$ for which every state supported on
  $P$ satisfies
  \begin{equation}
    E^{\infty}_{0,\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho)
    <\log_2\frac{391}{250}
    <-\log_2\frac{6393}{10000}
    \leq R_{\min}(\rho),
    \label{eq:75b9-separation}
  \end{equation}
  so the min-Rains relative entropy is not the exact rate.  The improved
  bound in Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-separation} comes from a non-Hermitian,
  range-supported witness and is not known to be additive or tight
  \sourcecite{ref:75b9-zhu-wang}{ZW26}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

The former candidate formula is disproved, but the support quantity whose
regularization gives the exact zero-error rate in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-regularized} has no known closed form.  The remaining
task is to find a tensor-stable relaxation of Eq.~\eqref{eq:75b9-one-shot}
that retains the identity-on-support constraint and equals the regularized
rate, or to show that no single-letter formula exists.
