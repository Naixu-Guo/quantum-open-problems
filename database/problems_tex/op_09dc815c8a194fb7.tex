\section{Distillable entanglement of the antisymmetric state}
\label{sec:op_09dc815c8a194fb7}

\paragraph{Problem.}
What is the exact LOCC distillable entanglement
$E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})$ of the $d\times d$ antisymmetric state, for
each integer $d\ge3$ --- as a closed form, or asymptotically through the
existence and value of $\lim_{d\to\infty}d\,E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})$?

Let $F$ be the swap operator on $\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^d$, acting by
$F\,|ij\rangle=|ji\rangle$ on the computational basis.  Define the
antisymmetric projector and the normalized antisymmetric state
\begin{equation}
  P_{\mathrm{as}}:=\tfrac12(\mathbb 1-F),
  \qquad
  \rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)}:=\frac{2}{d(d-1)}\,P_{\mathrm{as}},
  \label{eq:09dc-antisymmetric-state}
\end{equation}
so Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-antisymmetric-state} is the extreme point $\alpha=-1$
of the Werner family $\rho_\alpha\propto\mathbb 1+\alpha F$ and is not of
positive partial transpose; for $d=2$ it is the singlet, with $E_D=1$, and
the question concerns $d\ge3$.

Let $\Phi_2$ be a maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank two.  The LOCC
distillable entanglement of the state in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-antisymmetric-state} is
\begin{equation}
  E_D\bigl(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)}\bigr):=\sup\Bigl\{r\ge0:\ \exists\
  \mathrm{LOCC}\ \Lambda_n\ \mathrm{with}\
  \lim_{n\to\infty}\bigl\|\Lambda_n\bigl((\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})^{\otimes n}\bigr)
  -\Phi_2^{\otimes\lfloor rn\rfloor}\bigr\|_1=0\Bigr\},
  \label{eq:09dc-distillable-entanglement}
\end{equation}
and replacing LOCC in Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-distillable-entanglement} by
completely PPT-preserving operations defines the relaxation
$E_{D,\mathrm{PPT}}(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})$, which is exactly known ---
see Progress --- so the open quantity is the LOCC rate itself.  Determine
$E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})$ exactly for every $d\ge3$: as a closed form,
or asymptotically through the existence and value of
$\lim_{d\to\infty}d\,E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})$.  A complete answer
consists of an explicit LOCC achievability protocol together with a
matching converse bound.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Implicit in Christandl, Schuch, and Winter's analysis of the antisymmetric
state: they bound its distillable key, and hence its distillable entanglement,
by $O(1/d)$ while its entanglement cost stays above a $d$-independent
constant, leaving the exact rate open
\sourcecite{ref:09dc-christandl-schuch-winter}{CSW12}; announced in
\sourcecite{ref:09dc-csw-prl}{CSW10}.  Yura's exact evaluation of the
entanglement cost at $d=3$ settles the cost side of the same question
\sourcecite{ref:09dc-yura}{Yur03}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item DiVincenzo, Shor, Smolin, Terhal, and Thapliyal determined the
  one-copy-distillable region of the Werner family: a Schmidt-rank-two
  projection witnesses entanglement in a single copy precisely on one side of
  an explicit threshold, and the state in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-antisymmetric-state} lies in that region, so
  $E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})>0$
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-divincenzo-et-al}{DST+00}.

  \item A direct LOCC protocol quantifies the achievability side.  Partition the
  local computational basis $\{1,\dots,d\}$ into $\lfloor d/2\rfloor$
  disjoint pairs, together with one unpaired letter when $d$ is odd; both
  parties perform the local coarse measurement that projects onto the
  two-dimensional support $\operatorname{span}\{\lvert i\rangle,\lvert j\rangle\}$ of each
  pair, together with the unpaired letter when $d$ is odd --- an outcome
  identifies the pair but not which of its two letters --- and they keep
  the runs in which both obtain the same pair.  Conditioned on a common pair, the post-measurement state is the
  singlet $(\lvert ij\rangle-\lvert ji\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$ on that pair, so every kept
  run is one ebit, and the $2\lfloor d/2\rfloor$ ordered same-pair
  outcomes, each of probability $1/(d(d-1))$, occur with total probability
  \begin{equation}
    p_d:=\frac{2\lfloor d/2\rfloor}{d(d-1)}
    =\begin{cases}
       \dfrac{1}{d-1}, & d\ \text{even},\\[4pt]
       \dfrac{1}{d}, & d\ \text{odd}.
     \end{cases}
    \label{eq:09dc-pairing-probability}
  \end{equation}
  Independent repetition converts each copy into a singlet with probability
  $p_d$, so
  \begin{equation}
    E_D\bigl(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)}\bigr)\ \ge\ p_d=\Omega(1/d),
    \label{eq:09dc-pairing-bound}
  \end{equation}
  an $\Omega(1/d)$ achievable LOCC rate that coincides with the $1/d$ lower
  bound on $E_D$ and $K_D$ recorded in
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-christandl-schuch-winter}{CSW12} for odd $d$ and
  strengthens it to $1/(d-1)$ for even $d$.  The protocol extends the
  one-copy Schmidt-rank-two projection witnessing distillability in
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-divincenzo-et-al}{DST+00}, which retains a single
  basis pair and succeeds with probability $2/(d(d-1))$, to all
  $\lfloor d/2\rfloor$ disjoint basis pairs at once; the quantitative bound
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-pairing-probability} was recorded by Naixu Guo in the
  review discussion of this catalog entry (September 2026) and is not stated
  in the references above.

  \item The logarithmic negativity upper-bounds distillable entanglement
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-vidal-werner}{VW02}, and the Rains bound
  upper-bounds distillation by completely PPT-preserving operations
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-rains}{Rai01}.  The partial transpose of the state
  in Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-antisymmetric-state} has the single negative
  eigenvalue $-1/d$ and the eigenvalue $1/(d(d-1))$ with multiplicity
  $d^2-1$,
  \begin{equation}
    \operatorname{spec}\bigl(\bigl(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)}\bigr)^{\Gamma}\bigr)
    =\bigl\{-\tfrac1d\bigr\}\cup\bigl\{\tfrac{1}{d(d-1)}\ \text{with
    multiplicity}\ d^2-1\bigr\},
    \label{eq:09dc-pt-spectrum}
  \end{equation}
  so its trace norm is $(d+2)/d$.  The PPT relaxation itself is exactly
  solved: Rains's semidefinite program evaluates distillation under the
  completely PPT-preserving operations defined in Sec.~2 of his paper, and
  his Corollary~5.6 (Eq.~(5.40)) determines, with matching achievability
  and converse, the distillable entanglement
  $D_\Gamma(W_d(1))=\log_2\bigl(\frac{d+2}{d}\bigr)$ of the antisymmetric
  Werner state $W_d(1)$ --- his Eq.~(5.34) at $p=1$, which is exactly the
  state of Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-antisymmetric-state} --- with one-shot
  achievability $F_\Gamma(W_d(1);\frac{d+2}{d})=1$ (his Eq.~(5.39)) and the
  trace-norm converse $D_\Gamma\le
  \log_2\operatorname{Tr}\lvert\rho^\Gamma\rvert$ evaluated through
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-pt-spectrum}.  Combining with
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-pairing-bound}, the known rates are bracketed by
  \begin{equation}
    p_d\ \le\ E_D\bigl(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)}\bigr)\ \le\
    E_{D,\mathrm{PPT}}\bigl(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)}\bigr)
    =\log_2\bigl(1+\tfrac2d\bigr),
    \label{eq:09dc-bracket}
  \end{equation}
  whose final inequality is Rains's exact equality for every $d$, including
  $d=3$; the open gap is the middle one, LOCC versus completely
  PPT-preserving distillation.  The same value was obtained independently
  as the regularized PPT relative entropy of entanglement:
  $E_{R,\mathrm{PPT}}^{\infty}(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})
  =\log_2\bigl(1+\tfrac2d\bigr)$
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-audenaert-et-al}{AEA+01}, and the chain
  $E_{R,\mathrm{PPT}}^{\infty}=E_{\mathrm{Rains}}=E_N
  =\log_2\bigl(1+\tfrac2d\bigr)$, with $E_{\mathrm{Rains}}$ the single-copy
  Rains bound, is recorded in Sec.~III of
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-christandl-schuch-winter}{CSW12}.

  \item On the cost side, Yura computed $E_C(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(3)})=1$ ebit
  exactly \sourcecite{ref:09dc-yura}{Yur03}.  Christandl, Schuch, and Winter
  then showed that the distillable key obeys
  $K_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})=O(1/d)$, hence
  $E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})\le K_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})\to0$ as
  $d\to\infty$, while the entanglement cost stays above a $d$-independent
  constant, making asymptotic manipulation of the family irreversible in
  the limit $d\to\infty$ --- the cited bounds separate $E_D$ from $E_C$
  only for $d\ge7$ (and at $d=3$, via Yura's $E_C=1$), leaving $d=4$,
  $5$, and $6$ open.  Together with the lower bound
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-pairing-bound} and the $1/d$ bound on $E_D$ and
  $K_D$ recorded there, the $O(1/d)$ upper bound is optimal up to a
  constant; the exact distillable entanglement is left open
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-christandl-schuch-winter}{CSW12}.

  \item For any rank-two mixed state supported on the $3\otimes3$ antisymmetric
  subspace, Wang and Duan showed that the distillable entanglement under
  completely PPT-preserving operations is strictly smaller than one ebit,
  while the PPT entanglement cost is exactly one ebit, a strict separation
  between the two PPT measures; the inequality does not evaluate the rate in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-distillable-entanglement}
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-wang-duan}{WD17}.

  \item Beyond LOCC and its PPT relaxation, other operation classes admit
  exact general characterizations, none of which evaluates the LOCC rate
  posed here.  Lami and Regula proved that the distillable entanglement
  under dually non-entangling operations equals the separably measured
  regularized relative entropy of entanglement for every state --- the
  second exact regularized distillation formula after the Devetak--Winter
  formula for one-way LOCC --- and in particular is strictly positive for
  every entangled state \sourcecite{ref:09dc-lami-regula}{LR24}.  Dually
  non-entangling operations do not contain the completely PPT-preserving
  class, nor vice versa: a constant channel preparing a PPT entangled state
  is completely PPT-preserving and CPTP yet not non-entangling, since it
  outputs entanglement on separable inputs, while completely PPT-preserving
  operations distill at zero rate from every PPT state
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-rains}{Rai01}, excluding the strict positivity that
  holds under dually non-entangling operations.  The Lami--Regula formula
  therefore determines neither rate in Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-bracket}.  Chen,
  Wang, Zhang, and Zhu established reversible exact state transfer under
  trace-preserving PPT-preserving maps that need not be completely
  positive, governed by logarithmic negativity, a zero-error non-CPTP
  setting distinct from the vanishing-error rates posed here
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-chen-et-al}{CWZ+25}.

  \item Kondra, Brinster, Kampermann, Bru{\ss}, and Wyderka constructed
  analytically solvable families built from antisymmetric states exhibiting
  exponential strong-converse irreversibility under completely
  PPT-preserving operations \sourcecite{ref:09dc-kondra-et-al}{KBK+26}.
  The results concern cost and error exponents rather than the
  vanishing-error rate in Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-distillable-entanglement},
  and the LOCC-side analogues are stated there as open.

  \item Sen and Lami computed the main entanglement measures of the
  flower-state family, whose distillable entanglement is exactly one ebit
  independent of local dimension, citing the antisymmetric state as the
  standard example of vanishing distillable entanglement at nonvanishing
  cost; the value for the antisymmetric state itself is not computed
  \sourcecite{ref:09dc-sen-lami}{SL26}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

Even at $d=3$ the exact LOCC value is unknown:
Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-bracket} leaves
$\tfrac13\le E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(3)})\le\log_2(5/3)$, while the LOCC
entanglement cost equals one ebit, so both the rate and the sharpness of
the irreversibility are undetermined.  Asymptotically, the sharp question
is the leading constant: $E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})=O(1/d)$ by the key
bound of \sourcecite{ref:09dc-christandl-schuch-winter}{CSW12} and
$E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})\ge p_d$ by
Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-pairing-probability}, while the PPT relaxation is
exactly $\log_2\bigl(1+\tfrac2d\bigr)\sim 2/(d\ln2)$, so neither the
existence nor the value of
$\lim_{d\to\infty}d\,E_D(\rho_{\mathrm{as}}^{(d)})$ is established; in
particular whether the LOCC rate attains the PPT constant $2/\ln2$ or
falls below it by a definite factor is open.  A proof that LOCC attains
the PPT rate of Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-bracket} on this family, or a positive
separation between the two rates, would settle the asymptotic
alternative; the PPT side itself is closed.  This record asks for the
value of a rate on one explicit family, unlike
Problem~\ref{sec:op_b93eb7197c926d9e}, the LOCC distillable entanglement
of Bell-diagonal qubit states, and
Problem~\ref{sec:op_4cf3e7b8663b1d41}, whether the Rains bound of
Bell-diagonal states is attained by a PPT-preserving channel;
Problem~\ref{sec:op_75b91a20dd384110} asks for a closed form of the
regularized exact zero-error PPT distillable entanglement of an arbitrary
support, a different quantity from the vanishing-error rate in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:09dc-distillable-entanglement}.
