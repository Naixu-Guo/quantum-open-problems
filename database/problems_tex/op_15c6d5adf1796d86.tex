\section{Good quantum LDPC codes with a non-Clifford transversal CCZ gate}
\label{sec:op_15c6d5adf1796d86}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Do there exist triples of qubit CSS LDPC codes with linear combined rate and
linear distance on which the transversal CCZ gate induces a non-Clifford
logical operation?  Precisely, do there exist constants $r,\delta>0$ and
$w\in\mathbb N$, and triples, with $n\to\infty$, of qubit CSS codes $\mathcal Q_{a,n}$ with parameters
$[[n,k_{a,n},d_{a,n}]]$, indexed by $a\in\{1,2,3\}$, such that each code has
$k_{a,n}\ge1$ and $X$- and $Z$-check matrices whose row weights (check
weights) and column weights (qubit degrees) are at most $w$,
\begin{equation}
  \sum_{a=1}^{3}k_{a,n}\ge rn,\qquad
  \min_{a\in\{1,2,3\}}d_{a,n}\ge\delta n,
  \label{eq:15c6-good}
\end{equation}
and the transversal gate
\begin{equation}
  U_n:=\prod_{j=1}^{n}\operatorname{CCZ}_{(1,j),(2,j),(3,j)},
  \qquad
  \operatorname{CCZ}|x,y,z\rangle=(-1)^{xyz}|x,y,z\rangle
  \quad\text{for }x,y,z\in\{0,1\},
  \label{eq:15c6-transversal}
\end{equation}
preserves $\mathcal Q_{1,n}\otimes\mathcal Q_{2,n}\otimes\mathcal Q_{3,n}$
and induces a logical unitary that does not normalize its encoded Pauli group?
Here $(a,j)$ denotes physical qubit $j$ of block $a$, so
Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-transversal} applies one CCZ gate to each aligned triple of
physical qubits.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Golowich and Guruswami pose the construction of LDPC codes with asymptotically
optimal dimension and distance and transversal non-Clifford gates as an open
problem \sourcecite{ref:15c6-gg-stoc}{GG25a}, Section~1.1.  The formulation
here specializes it to qubit CSS code triples with linear combined rate,
linear distance, bounded check weights and qubit degrees, and a non-Clifford
logical action of the full transversal CCZ product in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-transversal}.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Li, Li, and Liu construct quantum LDPC codes supporting nontrivial
transversal logical CCZ gates with parameters
\begin{equation}
  [[N,\Theta(N),\Theta(N/(\log N)^2)]]
  \label{eq:15c6-almost-good}
\end{equation}
\sourcecite{ref:15c6-lll}{LLL26}, Theorem~1.1 with $r=3$ and
Corollary~5.7.  A constant-depth logical CCZ circuit is made transversal at a
constant-factor loss in parameters (Lemma~5.6).  At least one of the three
blocks is only known to have a constant number of logical qubits, so linear
rate is established for the combined blocks rather than for each block
(Section~5.3 and Section~6); the combined-rate condition in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-good} accommodates this.  The distance in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-almost-good} is a factor $(\log N)^2$ short of linear.
The revision of 2 July 2026 replaces the argument of the first version, whose
cap product did not satisfy the Leibniz rule required for the gate argument,
by one based on cup products and two-way product-expanding punctured
Reed--Solomon local codes; the results cited here are those of the revised
version.

  \item Golowich and Guruswami construct, for every fixed prime power $q$
including $q=2$, CSS codes with dimension and distance linear in the block
length that support a transversal CCZ gate
\sourcecite{ref:15c6-gg-stoc}{GG25a}, Theorem~1.1.  Their stabilizers have
high weight, so these codes are not LDPC (Section~1.1).

  \item An intermediate locality regime is reached by subsystem codes supporting
transversal CCZ with dimension and distance $\Theta(N)$ and locality
$O(\sqrt N)$ over alphabets of size $O(\sqrt N)$; reducing to qubits gives
$[[N,\tilde\Theta(N),\tilde\Theta(N)]]_2$ codes of locality
$\tilde O(\sqrt N)$, where the tilde hides polylogarithmic factors
\sourcecite{ref:15c6-gg-focs}{GG25b}, Theorem~1.1.  Neither the locality nor,
over qubits, the distance meets Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-good}.

  \item Golowich, Tamo, and Zhu construct, for every $0<\varepsilon<1$ and every
prime power alphabet size including qubits, subsystem codes with
constant-weight stabilizers whose depth-one transversal CCZ circuit induces
logical CCZ gates on at least $N^{1-\varepsilon}$ disjoint triples of logical
qubits, with distance at least $N^{(1-\varepsilon)/3}$
\sourcecite{ref:15c6-gtz}{GTZ26}, Theorem~1.1 with $r=3$ (informal statement
of Corollary~5.1).  Appendix~A extends this to addressable gates.  These
results increase the number and addressability of logical CCZ gates at
constant stabilizer weight, but the distance is polynomially sublinear.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

Literature checked through 15 September 2026: no family meeting
Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-good} with bounded check weights and qubit degrees and a
non-Clifford logical action of Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-transversal} was found.
Distance $\Theta(N/(\log N)^2)$ as in Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-almost-good} is not
a resolution.

The combined code has $N=3n$ physical qubits.  Only the total number of
logical qubits must be linear, which makes the rate accounting explicit
without requiring linearly many logical qubits in every block; each block must
still encode at least one qubit and have linear distance.  The question asks
only for a non-Clifford logical action, not for linearly many independent or
addressable logical CCZ gates.  A physical gate that preserves the code space
but acts trivially or as a logical Clifford operation does not qualify.  The
statement fixes the full product $U_n$; some cited constructions use more
general transversal circuits, such as constant-depth circuits or gates on
selected physical triples, which would need an additional argument to meet
Eq.~\eqref{eq:15c6-transversal}.

The locally testable analogue for good quantum LDPC codes is
\href{https://qiqc-op.com/problem/op_0c091a6b5a55a8fd/}{asymptotically good
quantum locally testable codes}; the construction of
\sourcecite{ref:15c6-lll}{LLL26} also gives almost-good quantum locally
testable codes with transversal non-Clifford gates.
