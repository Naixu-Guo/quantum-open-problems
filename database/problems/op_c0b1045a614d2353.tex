\section{Delayed-onset additivity violation for minimum output R\'enyi entropy}
\label{sec:problem-57}

\paragraph{Problem.}
Does there exist a finite-dimensional quantum channel whose minimum output
R\'enyi entropy is additive for every tensor power below some order and first
becomes strictly subadditive at that order?  Let
$\Phi:\mathcal L(A)\to\mathcal L(B)$ be completely positive and trace
preserving.  For $p>0$, define the R\'enyi entropy and the corresponding
minimum output entropy by
\begin{equation}
  S_p(\sigma)
  :=\begin{cases}
    \displaystyle\frac{1}{1-p}\log_2\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma^p),
      &p\neq1,\\[2mm]
    -\operatorname{Tr}(\sigma\log_2\sigma),&p=1,
  \end{cases}
  \qquad
  S_{p,\min}(\Phi)
  :=\min_{\substack{\rho\succeq0\\\operatorname{Tr}\rho=1}}
       S_p\!\left(\Phi(\rho)\right).
  \label{eq:p57-minimum-output-renyi}
\end{equation}
With the quantities in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p57-minimum-output-renyi}, determine
whether there are $p>0$, an integer $m\geq3$, and a channel $\Phi$ such that
\begin{equation}
  S_{p,\min}(\Phi^{\otimes n})
    =nS_{p,\min}(\Phi)
    \quad\text{for every }1\leq n<m,
  \qquad
  S_{p,\min}(\Phi^{\otimes m})
    <mS_{p,\min}(\Phi).
  \label{eq:p57-delayed-onset}
\end{equation}
The restriction $m\geq3$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p57-delayed-onset} makes the
lower-power requirement nontrivial: the equality at $n=1$ is automatic,
whereas equality at $n=2$ is required.

\subsection*{Status}

Unsolved

\subsection*{Source}

Ruskai explicitly asks whether additivity can hold for all tensor powers
below an integer $m$ and fail at the $m$th power
\sourcecite{ref:p57-ruskai}{Rus07}.  Equation~\eqref{eq:p57-delayed-onset}
uses the intended delayed-onset formulation $m\geq3$, excluding the
tautological one-copy-only condition obtained when $m=2$.

\subsection*{Progress}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Derksen and Lovitz construct explicit finite-dimensional
  self-channel violations
  $S_{p,\min}(\Phi^{\otimes2})<2S_{p,\min}(\Phi)$ for every $p>1$.
  These examples resolve the literal $m=2$ reading, but fail the required
  two-copy equality in Eq.~\eqref{eq:p57-delayed-onset} for every
  $m\geq3$ \sourcecite{ref:p57-derksen-lovitz}{DL26}.

  \item Hastings disproved minimum-output von Neumann entropy additivity at
  $p=1$ using finite-dimensional random channels.  The violation is already
  witnessed at a two-channel tensor product and therefore does not provide a
  channel that remains additive through every lower nontrivial power in
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:p57-delayed-onset}
  \sourcecite{ref:p57-hastings}{Has09}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Comment}

No channel is known to satisfy Eq.~\eqref{eq:p57-delayed-onset}, and no
general theorem is known that promotes two-copy additivity to additivity of
all self-tensor powers.  The unresolved issue is therefore the existence or
impossibility of a genuinely delayed first violation at some $m\geq3$.
