SIC POVMs and Zauner's conjecture

Unconditional SIC existence, Weyl-Heisenberg-covariant existence and Zauner-symmetric existence remain unproved in arbitrary dimension.

On this page Statement What remains Progress Interfaces Cite

Why it matters

SICs give minimal tomography and optimal projective designs. Zauner symmetry links their existence to finite groups and algebraic number theory, offering a route to exact constructions.

The problem is stated in three nested variants, each strictly stronger than its predecessor.

Problem 1 (SIC-POVMs). For every dimension \(d\ge 2\), decide whether there exists a family \(\{|\phi_i\rangle\}_{i=1}^{d^2}\) of normalised vectors in \(\mathbb{C}^d\) such that

\[|\langle\phi_i|\phi_j\rangle|^2 \;=\; \text{const} \qquad \text{for all } i\neq j .\]

Equivalently, decide whether SIC-POVMs—the POVMs \(\{|\phi_i\rangle\langle\phi_i|/d\}_{i=1}^{d^2}\) associated with such equiangular line configurations—exist in every dimension \(d\).

Problem 2 (Covariant SIC-POVMs). With \(X|q\rangle = |q+1\rangle\), \(Z|q\rangle = e^{i 2\pi q/d}|q\rangle\) (arithmetic mod \(d\)), and Weyl operators

\[w(p,q) \;=\; Z(p)\,X(q), \qquad p,q\in\mathbf{Z}_d , \tag{23.1}\]

decide for which \(d\) there exists a fiducial vector \(|\phi\rangle\in\mathbb{C}^d\) such that

\[\bigl\{\,w(p,q)\,|\phi\rangle\langle\phi|\,w(p,q)^*\,\bigr\}_{p,q=0,\dots,d-1} \tag{23.2}\]

is a SIC-POVM (i.e. a group-covariant SIC under the Heisenberg group).

Problem 3 (Zauner's Conjecture). Let \(z\in U(d)\) be the element of the Clifford group characterised by

\[z\,w(p,q)\,z^* \;=\; w(q-p,\,-p) .\]

Zauner's conjecture asserts that in any dimension \(d\), a fiducial vector \(|\phi\rangle\) for a Heisenberg-covariant SIC-POVM can be chosen among the eigenvectors of \(z\).

What remains

Prove or refute the nested all-dimension SIC, covariance and Zauner-symmetry conjectures without unproved arithmetic assumptions.
