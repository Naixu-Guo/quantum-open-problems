04 / Method

How the audit works

The audit matches each label to the exact archived question. A negative theorem solves a yes-or-no problem; progress on a nearby variant leaves it open.

1

Read the quantifiers

Universal statements need a proof or one valid counterexample. Existence questions stay open until someone constructs the object or proves impossibility.

2

Check the theorem

Abstracts and search snippets often overstate scope. The audit compares the formal theorem with the original problem and records any missing case.

3

Separate strength from maturity

An exact theorem can appear in a preprint. A peer-reviewed paper can offer numerical evidence. The site displays both dimensions.

4

Track corrections

Withdrawals, proof gaps, conditional hypotheses, and source typos stay visible. Each entry shows when its status was checked.
