The source asked whether the second partial transpose of the absolute value of a two-qubit state's partial transpose is always positive semidefinite.

The inequality controls bi-negativity and connects logarithmic negativity with entanglement cost under PPT-preserving operations for two-qubit states.

## Cautions

The affirmative result is specific to two qubits; higher-dimensional failures do not affect the archived statement.
