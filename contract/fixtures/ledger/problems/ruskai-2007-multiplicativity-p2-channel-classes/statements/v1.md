## Formal statement

**Problem 12.** For what classes of quantum channels $\Phi,\Omega$ can the multiplicativity identity
$$\nu_p(\Phi\otimes\Omega)=\nu_p(\Phi)\,\nu_p(\Omega) \tag{24}$$
be proved at $p=2$?
