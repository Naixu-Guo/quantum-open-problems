# Policy version 1

This file is the single place where every vocabulary value is defined and
every threshold is set. Schemas enforce the values; this file says what
they mean. A decision records the policy version it applied, so changing
this file never changes a past decision.

## Thresholds

| Transition | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Problem admitted (`candidate` to `published`) | two independent AI reviews with verdict `verified`, or one human review |
| Statement revision accepted | same as admission, and clause lineage complete |
| Reference accepted | one review of any kind, or automatic for human actors with a prior accepted contribution |
| Contribution reaches `ai-verified`; clause or problem marked `partial` | two independent AI reviews with `verified` or `verified-partial`, or one passing machine check on a checkable artifact |
| Attempt report accepted; auxiliary problems it formulated become `published` | one independent AI review confirming the account |
| Auxiliary problem marked `solved` or `refuted` | two independent AI reviews with `verified`, or one passing machine check |
| Auxiliary problem promoted to the main index | one human decision: the statement is a complete self-contained proposition and the status is open |
| Primary problem marked `solved` | a human `status` decision plus one of: passing machine check, peer-reviewed publication, two independent human reviews |
| Merge, retire, moderation, redaction | one human decision |

Independence between a reviewer and a submitter requires a different
operator, a different model family, and that the reviewer has not read the
submitter's trajectory. Every review must use at least one mechanical
method.

## Vocabularies

### Problem.role
- `primary`: a question collected from humans and published in its own right.
- `auxiliary`: a lemma, reduction, or intermediate conjecture that an agent formulated while attacking a clause of its parent.

### Problem.origin
- `source-stated`: the question is stated in an identifiable source in this form.
- `derived`: an editor derived the question from a documented limitation in a source.
- `editor-formulated`: an editor formulated the question without a stating source.
- `agent-formulated`: an agent formulated the question during an attempt.

### Problem.difficulty
- `unrated`: no difficulty label has been assigned.
- `accessible`: a competent researcher could plausibly settle it with known tools.
- `hard`: settling it is expected to need a new idea.
- `very-hard`: a long-standing question with sustained expert effort and no route in sight.

### Problem.verificationCost
- `unrated`: no verification cost label has been assigned.
- `low`: a correct answer can be checked in hours with the artifacts the record demands.
- `medium`: checking needs days or a reproduction.
- `high`: checking is itself a substantial research task.

### Clause.kind
- `existence`: asks whether an object with stated properties exists.
- `universal`: asks whether a property holds for every object in a class.
- `value`: asks for the exact value of a quantity.
- `construction`: asks for an explicit object or procedure.
- `bound`: asks for an inequality on a quantity.
- `decision`: asks a yes-or-no question not covered above.

### Quantity.direction and Bound.direction
- `upper`: the clause or bound concerns an upper bound on the quantity.
- `lower`: the clause or bound concerns a lower bound on the quantity.
- `exact`: the clause or bound concerns the exact value.

### Bound.valueForm
- `exact-rational`: `value` is a rational number written as `p/q` or an integer.
- `decimal`: `value` is a decimal approximation; the number of digits shown is the claimed precision.
- `expression`: `value` is a closed-form expression in LaTeX.

### Claim.relation
- `resolves`: settles the named clauses affirmatively.
- `refutes`: settles the named clauses negatively by proof or counterexample.
- `narrows`: settles the named clauses for a precisely stated subclass.
- `supports`: gives evidence or a partial result without settling.
- `bounds`: establishes a bound on the clause's quantity.

### Support.maturity
- `peer-reviewed`: published after peer review.
- `preprint`: publicly posted, not peer reviewed.
- `withdrawn`: withdrawn or retracted by its authors or venue.
- `conditional`: depends on an unproved hypothesis stated in the source.
- `unreviewed-artifact`: an artifact that no check has run on.
- `machine-checked-artifact`: an artifact whose automated check passed.
- `ai-verified-artifact`: an artifact confirmed by independent AI reviews.
- `web-record`: a dated record in a curated web database.

### Support.strength
- `exact-theorem`: a proof of the full statement addressed.
- `restricted-theorem`: a proof under added hypotheses or for a subclass.
- `bound`: a proved inequality.
- `numerical-evidence`: computation without proof.
- `construction`: an explicit object with verified properties.
- `counterexample`: an explicit object refuting a statement.
- `survey-assessment`: a survey's assessment of status.
- `correction`: a correction or erratum to earlier work.
- `unaccepted-claim`: a claimed proof or result that has not been accepted.

### Source.kind
- `paper`: a journal or conference paper.
- `preprint`: a preprint server posting.
- `book`: a book or monograph.
- `problem-list`: a published list of open problems.
- `dataset`: a dataset or data release.
- `thesis`: a thesis or dissertation.
- `web-record`: a record in a curated web database.

### Reference.role
- `states-problem`: the source states the problem in this or an equivalent form.
- `listed-in`: the source is a problem list that includes the problem.
- `background`: the source supplies motivation or context.
- `defines`: the source is where notation or definitions come from.
- `prior-attempt`: the source records an attempt at the problem.
- `partial-result`: the source proves a partial result.
- `technique`: the source supplies a method that may apply.
- `related`: the source concerns a neighbouring problem.
- `survey`: the source surveys the problem's status.
- `resolves`: the source claims to resolve the problem.

### Reference.targetType
- `problem`: the reference is about the problem as a whole.
- `statement`: the reference is about a statement version.
- `clause`: the reference is about one clause.
- `claim`: the reference is about a claim.

### Actor.kind
- `human`: a person.
- `agent`: an AI system run by an operator.
- `pipeline`: an automated process run by an operator.
- `system`: the domain service itself.

### Trajectory.kind
- `research`: an attempt at one or more problems.
- `verification`: a review run.
- `maintenance`: a periodic maintenance run by humans with AI.
- `ingestion`: an import of external records.

### Trajectory.visibility
- `public`: readable by everyone.
- `embargoed`: readable by the operator until `embargoUntil`, then public.

### Event.kind
- `read`: the actor read a record or bundle.
- `decompose`: the actor formulated an auxiliary problem.
- `attempt`: the actor started work on a problem or clause.
- `prove`: the actor established a result.
- `refute`: the actor disproved a statement.
- `compute`: the actor ran a computation.
- `stuck`: the actor hit an obstacle.
- `revise`: the actor changed its plan.
- `submit`: the actor submitted a contribution.
- `note`: a free-form remark.

### Event.obstacle
- `missing-lemma`: a needed intermediate result could not be established.
- `refuted-subgoal`: an intermediate conjecture turned out false.
- `computational-limit`: the computation exceeded available resources.
- `ambiguous-statement`: the statement admits more than one reading.
- `out-of-budget`: the run's budget was exhausted.
- `none`: no obstacle.

### Contribution.kind
- `problem-proposal`: proposes a new primary problem with its first statement.
- `statement-revision`: proposes a new statement version.
- `reference`: adds references.
- `attempt-report`: reports a research trajectory: route, claims, auxiliary problems, artifacts, stop reason.
- `evidence-import`: imports results from the literature as claims.
- `merge-proposal`: proposes merging a problem into another.
- `retire-proposal`: proposes retiring a problem.

### Contribution.stopReason
- `solved`: the attempt settled every targeted clause.
- `partial`: the attempt settled some targeted clauses or a subclass.
- `obstacle`: the attempt stopped at a recorded obstacle.
- `refuted-subgoal`: the attempt stopped because an intermediate conjecture was false.
- `ambiguous-statement`: the attempt stopped because the statement was ambiguous.
- `out-of-budget`: the attempt stopped because the budget ran out.
- `abandoned`: the attempt stopped with no finding.
- `none`: not an attempt report.

### Contribution.aiInvolvement
- `none`: no AI system took part.
- `assisted`: an AI system assisted a human who checked the output.
- `autonomous`: an AI system produced the contribution.

### Artifact.kind
- `proof-text`: a proof in prose and LaTeX.
- `lean`: a Lean formalization.
- `coq`: a Coq formalization.
- `code`: source code.
- `certificate`: a machine-checkable certificate.
- `notebook`: a computational notebook.
- `dataset`: data.
- `transcript`: a raw agent transcript.
- `event-log`: a trajectory event log.
- `log`: a tool or execution log.
- `figure`: an image.

### Check.outcome
- `pass`: the check succeeded.
- `fail`: the check ran and failed.
- `error`: the check could not run.

### Review.kind
- `triage`: a gatekeeper review for duplicates, junk, and completeness.
- `verification`: a review of a contribution's correctness and scope.
- `audit`: a maintenance review of an earlier review or decision.

### Review.methods
- `citation-check`: every cited source was located and its content matched.
- `artifact-execution`: artifacts were executed and outputs compared.
- `argument-read`: the argument was read step by step.
- `formal-check`: a formal proof was checked by a proof assistant.
- `reproduction`: the result was reproduced independently.
- `scope-check`: the claim was compared with the clause and its resolution criteria.
- `duplicate-check`: the submission was compared with existing records.

### Review.verdict
- `rejected`: the contribution is wrong or unsupported.
- `duplicate`: the contribution repeats an existing record.
- `junk`: the contribution has no usable content.
- `incomplete`: the contribution lacks required parts.
- `scope-mismatch`: the contribution does not address the clauses it names.
- `unverified-plausible`: the reviewer found no error but could not verify.
- `verified-partial`: the reviewer verified a stated part.
- `verified`: the reviewer verified the contribution as stated.

### Comment.targetType
- `problem`, `statement`, `clause`, `claim`, `reference`, `contribution`, `trajectory`, `review`: the record the comment is attached to.

### Decision.kind
- `admission`: moves a problem from `candidate` to `published`.
- `promotion`: adds an auxiliary problem to the main index.
- `acceptance`: accepts or rejects a contribution, recording the verification level reached.
- `status`: sets a problem's research status.
- `merge`: merges a problem into another.
- `retire`: retires a problem.
- `moderation`: hides or restores a comment.
- `redaction`: replaces a record with a tombstone.
- `release`: freezes a snapshot of the ledger.

### Decision.targetType
- `problem`, `contribution`, `comment`, `statement`, `claim`, `reference`, `trajectory`, `review`: the record the decision acts on.
- `ledger`: the ledger as a whole, for `release`.

### Decision.outcome
- `accepted`: the decision approves the transition.
- `rejected`: the decision refuses the transition.

### Decision.status
- `open`: no accepted proof or counterexample.
- `partial`: a named clause or precise subclass is settled.
- `solved`: every clause is settled.
- `refuted`: the conjecture is false; used for auxiliary problems.

### Decision.verificationLevel
- `triaged`: passed triage only.
- `ai-verified`: met the independent AI review threshold.
- `machine-verified`: a checkable artifact passed its check.
- `human-signed`: a human review or decision applied.

## Derived vocabularies

These values never appear in a record; they are computed.

### Problem catalog state
- `candidate`: proposed, not admitted.
- `published`: admitted.
- `retired`: retired by decision.
- `merged`: merged into another problem by decision.

### Contribution state
- `submitted`: on intake, no decision yet.
- `triaged`: an acceptance decision recorded `triaged` and nothing more.
- `accepted`: an acceptance decision with outcome `accepted`.
- `rejected`: an acceptance decision with outcome `rejected`.
- `superseded`: a later contribution supersedes it.
- `withdrawn`: withdrawn by its actor through a later contribution.

### Clause status
- `open`: no accepted claim settles it.
- `partial`: accepted `narrows`, `bounds`, or `supports` claims exist.
- `resolved`: an accepted `resolves` or `refutes` claim covers it.
