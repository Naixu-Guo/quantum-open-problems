# Policy version 0: the legacy audit

Policy 0 describes the rules under which the legacy catalog was audited on
12 August 2026 and extended on 31 August 2026, before the ledger existed. It
is recorded so that the seed decisions can name the policy they were made
under instead of pretending to satisfy policy 1.

## Rule

One human audit review decides every status. The auditor compared each
archived statement with primary papers and later claims, classified
publication maturity and mathematical strength separately, and recorded
`open`, `partial`, or `solved`. A `solved` label rested on a peer-reviewed
proof or counterexample for all but one problem; the two-copy Werner-state
problem was labeled solved at preprint level on the strength of four
concurrent independent manuscripts, and the audit says so.

## Consequences

- Seed decisions carry `policyVersion: "0"`. The validator applies policy 1
  thresholds only to decisions under policy 1 or later.
- Policy 0 accepts no new decisions after 2 September 2026. A change to a
  seed problem's status is a new decision under the current policy.
- Maintenance should re-examine the preprint-level solved label under
  policy 1 and either find peer-reviewed support or record a second
  independent human review.
